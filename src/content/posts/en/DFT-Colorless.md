Aetherdrift (DFT) prerelease events run on February 7, 2025, and the set is officially released one week later on February 14, 2025. Want to know more about how each card rates in Limited, with some pick advice? Then check out this series of DFT Limited primers!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Aetherdrift Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/DFT-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: An unconditional first pick that gives you a near-winning advantage
4.5: An unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors, giving you a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick. Excellent synergy with its color pair's theme, and can be the deciding card for splashing, switching colors, or committing to the pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set, plus other cards that are strong enough. Great synergy with the color pair's theme and a key card for committing to the pair
3.0: An important card for the color pair's theme, plus other above-average cards. You will want it in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0–1.0: Below average. Generally you do not want to play it, but it can fill out the deck if necessary, just not too many of them
0.5–0.0: Unplayable. Basically do not play it. Playing it will only help you lose

## Conclusion
This series centers on artifacts, and among the colorless cards there are many good Vehicles, which adds a lot of complexity to games and also widens the range of picks in draft. For lands, there are a great many dual lands to help with fixing, which gives this set's Limited builds a lot more flexibility. In addition, the Special Guests include many all-star cards from past artifact themes, and I believe this set's Limited will offer a great play experience!
