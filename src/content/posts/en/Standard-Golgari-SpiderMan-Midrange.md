## Build Update

Hello readers, long time no see. I am [faintmama](/authors/faintmama/), a poor old guy who has had little chance to play lately. When the [TMT](https://scryfall.com/sets/tmt) set had just been released, I already shared [Golgari Midrange: Won't Badger You to Win](https://guildmagesforum.tw/Standard-Golgari-Midrange-without-Cub/) with everyone. Now at the end of the [SOS](https://scryfall.com/sets/sos) season, nearly two and a half months have passed, so what has this build been modified into?

Of course, reheating leftovers always has its reasons, so let me first introduce the recent fortunes of this build:

1. I got a copy of [Abhorrent Oculus](https://scryfall.com/card/sch/50/abhorrent-oculus), so happy happy happy. I will not go into the [tournament report](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1BC4UbWHZx/) in this article.
2. It received thematic boosts one after another in both the [TMT](https://scryfall.com/sets/tmt) and [SOS](https://scryfall.com/sets/sos) sets.

Still, in the excitement, we have to recognize that the boosts the Izzet color pair received are stronger than ours (after all, flexibility and instant speed equal strength). Only by keeping this awareness in mind do we have a chance of finding the road to victory during deck building and games, otherwise it is really easy to get flattened outright by Izzet's efficient instants ~~yes, I am talking about [Prismari Charm](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/211/prismari-charm)~~.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Golgari-SpiderMan-Midrange/b837126fff54.webp)

The detailed decklist is [here](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7819746#paper), and you are welcome to get a feel for how it differs from the past.

## Main Deck 60 Cards Breakdown

Let's start with the new cards from SOS.

[Wildgrowth Archaic](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/297/wildgrowth-archaic) x4: This card is absolutely the brand-new core of this build. The +1/+1 counters that come with it entering fit our earlier ideas perfectly, and as long as it keeps standing on the battlefield, the creatures that enter afterward also get more or fewer +1/+1 counters. Even without counting the card draw, following it up with a resolved [Ouroboroid](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/201/ouroboroid) can dominate the game outright. Beyond that, what the author loves most about it is its **mana value of 4**, which means we can cast it for two mana, yet it has plenty of room to dodge the range of [Day of Black Sun](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/94/day-of-black-sun) and [Vicious Rivalry](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/241/vicious-rivalry). Outrageous, and a shame not to play it.

And then... there are no other new cards from the [SOS](https://scryfall.com/sets/sos) set in the main deck XD, but there are many tweaks made for [Wildgrowth Archaic](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/297/wildgrowth-archaic) (why? Because it deserves it), which I will continue to introduce.

[Llanowar Elves](https://scryfall.com/card/m12/182/zht/%E7%BE%85%E5%A0%B0%E5%A6%96%E7%B2%BE-(llanowar-elves)) x4: Nothing much to say, a regular at tournaments. If the opponent does not deal with it right away, get ready to jump ahead on mana to apply pressure, and eating a removal spell can indirectly protect later creatures ~~but we usually still want it to live~~.

[Pawpatch Recruit](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/187/pawpatch-recruit) x4: In a build focused on pairing [Wildgrowth Archaic](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/297/wildgrowth-archaic) with [Ouroboroid](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/201/ouroboroid), [Pawpatch Recruit](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/187/pawpatch-recruit) has a bigger chance of delivering finishing miracles, since the number of creatures is also one of our focuses. Long live Offspring.

[Mikey & Leo, Chaos & Order](https://scryfall.com/card/tmt/247/mikey-&-leo-chaos-&-order) x1: The creature version of [Hollowmurk Siege](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/192/hollowmurk-siege), though less flexible, greatly frees up this build in the details. Since the clash tempo of the [SOS](https://scryfall.com/sets/sos) season is tighter, the traditional midrange card-draw approach sometimes looks too mild, so I cut it to one copy, and the build as a whole pursues more immediate firepower.

[Scavenging Ooze](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/204/zht/%E8%85%90%E9%A3%9F%E6%B5%81%E6%BC%BF-(scavenging-ooze)) x2: It combines graveyard hate, putting +1/+1 counters, and life gain, giving the pilot the possibility of keeping an eye on graveyard decks from game one.

[Twitching Doll](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/417/twitching-doll) x1: A foolproof card that can turn green into mana of any color, which raises [Wildgrowth Archaic](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/297/wildgrowth-archaic)'s ceiling, but its use is not just that: while producing mana it may also trigger card draw (see the first ability's **Put a nest counter on this creature.**). It has the same problem as [Mikey & Leo, Chaos & Order](https://scryfall.com/card/tmt/247/mikey-&-leo-chaos-&-order), and to pursue more immediate firepower it had to be cut to one copy.

[Kraven the Hunter](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/133/kraven-the-hunter) x1: A super war god. In this build I think it is even better than [Surrak, Elusive Hunter](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/161/surrak-elusive-hunter), as the eight removal spells in the main deck and trades in combat all have a chance to trigger the ability. It can draw cards, put +1/+1 counters, and putting +1/+1 counters may let it draw another card or two.

[Qarsi Revenant](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/426/qarsi-revenant) x1: A quality creature that black players often keep in the sideboard to reduce life pressure. The author thinks it deserves a 1-main, 1-side split in this build, which in a way adds 0.5 of a removal spell. Don't forget that the Renew ability can trigger our theme of putting counters and drawing cards.

[Leatherhead, Swamp Stalker](https://scryfall.com/card/tmt/117/leatherhead-swamp-stalker) x1: Destroy the artifacts and enchantments that need destroying, smash the players or planeswalkers that need smashing. Oh, and when it enters with a hexproof counter, it also has a chance to draw the cards that need drawing.

[Ouroboroid](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/201/ouroboroid) x4: One-mana [Llanowar Elves](https://scryfall.com/card/m12/182/zht/%E7%BE%85%E5%A0%B0%E5%A6%96%E7%B2%BE-(llanowar-elves)), two-mana [Wildgrowth Archaic](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/297/wildgrowth-archaic), four-mana [Ouroboroid](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/201/ouroboroid): if none of them eats a removal spell, get ready to borrow more dice from the players nearby.

[Requiting Hex](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/116/requiting-hex) x3: Early on it is an instant-speed one-mana removal spell, and in the mid to late game you can use the Blight ability to put -1/-1 counters to gain life and thereby draw cards. The ceiling is super high in this build, but note that control decks have been increasing lately...

[Snakeskin Veil](https://scryfall.com/card/pcbb/5/snakeskin-veil) x1: Its floor is a poor man's [Opt](https://scryfall.com/card/sta/19/zht/%E6%8A%89%E6%93%87-(opt)). It works wonders in both Closed List and Open List events.

[Shoot the Sheriff](https://scryfall.com/card/slp/43/shoot-the-sheriff) x2: Universal removal. Just be careful not to name a creature type you should not.

[Bitter Triumph](https://scryfall.com/card/sch/42/bitter-triumph) x1: With the escort of this deck's [Scavenging Ooze](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/204/zht/%E8%85%90%E9%A3%9F%E6%B5%81%E6%BC%BF-(scavenging-ooze)), I think it can fit one copy.

[Origin of Metalbending](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/187/origin-of-metalbending) x1: Its ceiling can give one creature indestructible and put +1/+1 counters, and then it can also be a piece of disruption. As always, flexibility is strength.

[Strategic Betrayal](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/94/strategic-betrayal) x1: A sorcery-speed main-deck graveyard hate card that also handles a creature. It has become very important in the [SOS](https://scryfall.com/sets/sos) season.

[Hollowmurk Siege](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/192/hollowmurk-siege) x3: Sultai or Abzan are both options, testing the player's read of the current board, and of course casting two in a row and choosing one mode each is also a very strong decision. When the opponent puts a stun counter on our creature, we **can draw a card**, which [Mikey & Leo, Chaos & Order](https://scryfall.com/card/tmt/247/mikey-&-leo-chaos-&-order) cannot do.

[Nowhere to Run](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/111/nowhere-to-run) x1: In an environment with plenty of [Izzet Prowess](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/archetype/standard-izzet-prowess-woe#paper) and [Mono-Green Landfall](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/archetype/standard-mono-green-landfall-woe#paper), I think it is worth bringing one copy in the main deck.

[Blooming Marsh](https://scryfall.com/card/potj/266p/blooming-marsh) x4: The four indispensable fast lands in the build. Listing it at the front of the lands means play it as soon as possible, as the saying goes: gather rosebuds while you may.

[Overgrown Tomb](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/266/overgrown-tomb) x4: Anyway, a super great land ~~unless you are in a sudden-death match where changes in your life total make you lose~~.

[Multiversal Passage](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/180/multiversal-passage) x4: After a reminder from [cephille](/authors/cephille/), I chose to max it out. In the tightly paced [SOS](https://scryfall.com/sets/sos) season it supports us in clashing at the right time. When mana is plentiful late in the game, you can name white, or even blue or red (white first, because the build has a [Mikey & Leo, Chaos & Order](https://scryfall.com/card/tmt/247/mikey-&-leo-chaos-&-order); of course, naming blue at random to scare the opponent is also quite fun), expanding the effect of [Wildgrowth Archaic](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/297/wildgrowth-archaic).

[Wastewood Verge](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/268/wastewood-verge) x4: Its floor is that it can only produce green, but in a build that applies early pressure with green creatures first, that is perfectly acceptable.

[Ba Sing Se](https://scryfall.com/card/ptla/266s/ba-sing-se) x2: Besides letting lands join combat, earthbend fits this deck's theme of **putting counters to gain dividends** even better, so bringing two is not excessive at all.

[Restless Cottage](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/304/restless-cottage) x1: A transforming land that sometimes helps us break a stalemate. Standard has entered an explosive tempo where you must hate graveyards as fast as possible, so exiling one card from a graveyard is only a bonus, and you cannot expect it to completely solve the problem.

[Forest](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/291/forest) x4: We always need it.

[Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/289/swamp) x1: We always need it.

## Sideboard 15 Cards Breakdown

Let's start with the new cards from SOS.

[Vicious Rivalry](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/241/vicious-rivalry) x1: The author really likes how this card is written. Although it does not sweep as instantly as [Day of Black Sun](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/94/day-of-black-sun), the flexibility and range it gives are what this deck needs.

And then... there are no new cards from the [SOS](https://scryfall.com/sets/sos) set in the sideboard XD. Below are the rest of the sideboard cards the author brings for the [SOS](https://scryfall.com/sets/sos) Standard environment.

[Duress](https://scryfall.com/card/sta/29/zht/%E9%80%BC%E5%BE%9E-(duress)) x3: If sideboard slots were sufficient, the author would definitely bring four ~~but there will never be enough~~. It can take away one half of the A+B in combo decks, discard the opponent's sweeper or removal, and force others to play a game with few cards in hand alongside you.

[Intimidation Tactics](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/92/intimidation-tactics) x1: A card that discards and also exiles an opponent's creature or artifact, which in a way counts as a means of protecting against ETB (Enter the Battlefield) effects and also lets you gain hidden information. It has a chance to take away one half of the A+B in combo decks, and can also leave decks full of equally powerful creatures short of key spells.

[Requiting Hex](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/116/requiting-hex) x1: The low-cost, high-efficiency removal in this build. In suitable matchups, add one more; otherwise, painfully take the three out of the main deck.

[Day of Black Sun](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/94/day-of-black-sun) x1: Bring it in for matchups where you should sweep the opponent away, it will never be wrong.

[Deep-Cavern Bat](https://scryfall.com/card/sch/33/deep-cavern-bat) x1: The number one victim of [Prismari Charm](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/211/prismari-charm), forced to stay in the sideboard.

[Frenzied Baloth](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/183/frenzied-baloth) x2: A creature with a very interesting use, especially important against control decks. The author has relied on it many times to finish racing for life and close out games in a nail-biting way.

[Origin of Metalbending](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/187/origin-of-metalbending) x1: If there are artifacts or enchantments that need destroying, bring it in.

[Strategic Betrayal](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/94/strategic-betrayal) x2: The payoff against graveyard decks is still far too large, and the author has even considered a 2-main, 1-side ratio.

[Qarsi Revenant](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/426/qarsi-revenant) x1: The other copy is already in the main deck.

## Conclusion

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Golgari-SpiderMan-Midrange/b479a7dbfed1.webp)
>Opening two red-faced newspaper editors from a store championship prize pack reminded this homebody that it is time to rein in the mind and get back to work properly.

I originally wanted to analyze for everyone this deck's winning windows against each build in the environment, but unfortunately I have little leisure time lately, so I will leave this part to readers to interpret on their own. I will only remind you that when facing control decks, you especially need a proper opening hand that applies life pressure, you must discard most of the counter-and-draw components, and rely more on the sideboard's [Frenzied Baloth](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/183/frenzied-baloth) and various discard effects.

The author did not perform well at the Last Chance Qualifier on 2026-05-23, because I inexplicably chose to play another niche deck, and instead did not win the matchups I should have won, so I lost. When I finally admitted my mistake and switched back to Golgari Midrange for the last round, I ran into [Izzet Spellementals](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/archetype/standard-izzet-spellementals-woe#paper) and lost again.

I never expected that a week later, after removing one creature and putting [Nowhere to Run](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/111/nowhere-to-run) back in, it became unusually smooth. This made me realize: when your own main plan is not the strongest in the environment, black players should still focus on disrupting the opponent's plan and use the openings torn open by discard to win.

I really should get going. I am very glad to have found a chance to share with readers again. I wish everyone finds a deck they like and reaps some rewards ~~but how could that be, since this is a zero-sum game~~.
