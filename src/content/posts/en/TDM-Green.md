Tarkir: Dragonstorm (TDM) will hold its Prerelease events on April 4, 2025, and officially launch one week later on April 11, 2025. Want a better understanding of individual card ratings and advice for Limited? Then check out this series of TDM Limited overviews!

- For an introduction to the mechanics, see this article: [Tarkir: Dragonstorm Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/TDM-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

<!---If the rating scale changes, this template will be updated directly. If you need adjustments, authors should submit a template update request to the team, and it will be updated once discussed--->

5.0: An unconditional must-pick that brings an almost game-winning advantage
4.5: An unconditional must-pick if you are in the right colors, bringing a huge advantage
4.0: First-pick value, with excellent synergy with the color pair's theme, and a deciding card for splashing / switching colors or entering a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards, with good synergy with the color pair's theme, and a key card for entering a color pair
3.0: Important cards for the color pair's theme and other above-average cards that you would want in your final deck
2.5: Average level, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average; you generally prefer not to play them, and can use them as filler if necessary, but not too many
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable; basically do not play them, as they will only help you lose

## Conclusion
Gentlemen, I love graveyards! With the support of both Renew and Harmonize, the thickness of your graveyard determines how many resources you have, so just keep milling!
That said, the decks I am most bullish on are still the green-based multicolor decks, as there are truly so many fixing / mana-jump cards that go with them. There are also plenty of expensive multicolor bombs; if you ask, the answer is I want them all.
