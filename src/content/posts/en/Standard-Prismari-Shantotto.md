## Introduction

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Prismari-Shantotto/aa08a4f0dc09.webp)

Strixhaven's Prismari college is famous for its flashy big finishes. The college's signature ability, **Opus**, works like this: when you cast an instant or sorcery spell, you get the base score (the ability's effect), and if you reach the **mana payment threshold (spending 5 or more mana)**, you also get extra artistic points.
Note that this doesn't look at the spell's own mana cost, but at how much mana was actually spent. The condition looks harsh, but can it be achieved in Standard? Professor Shantotto from the Federation of Windurst is here to show us!


## Core Cards

* **Molten-Core Maestro**
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Prismari-Shantotto/2dee986628de.webp)
[Molten-Core Maestro](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/125/molten-core-maestro) is the build-around of this deck: when you cast an instant or sorcery, it permanently grows, and if the cost threshold is met it also produces red mana equal to its power, letting you chain spells until you run out of mana or cards in hand.

* **Shantotto, Tactician Magician**
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Prismari-Shantotto/1c7bb27ddb4e.webp)
The Opus ability is a kindred spirit to the design in the *[Final Fantasy](https://scryfall.com/sets/fin?as=grid&order=set)* set that checks whether you spent 4 or more mana on a noncreature spell. As one of the best at it, [Shantotto](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/241/shantotto-tactician-magician) gets a power boost equal to the mana spent whenever you cast a noncreature spell, and if you spent 4 or more she also draws you a card, making sure you never run out of resources while chaining your big plays.

* **Ashling's Command**
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Prismari-Shantotto/2797a6e05b3b.webp)
[Ashling's Command](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/205/ashlings-command) is currently the best companion spell for this deck in Standard. Setting aside the elemental-copying mode, which isn't useful here, it offers a one-sided sweep, drawing cards, or making an untapped Treasure to speed up color fixing, choose two for flexibility. If you choose the Treasure, it effectively refunds two mana, letting you bridge into your follow-up blue spells or abilities.
Here's how it works in detail: while you control [Molten-Core Maestro](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/125/molten-core-maestro), cast [Ashling's Command](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/205/ashlings-command) and choose the Treasure mode as one of the options. The minimum mana you get starts at 3 red plus 2 Treasures, which matches what you paid to cast [Ashling's Command](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/205/ashlings-command), and as soon as [Molten-Core Maestro](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/125/molten-core-maestro) is even 1 bigger, you profit extra mana, which lets you keep casting one or more other five-mana spells. If [Shantotto](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/241/shantotto-tactician-magician) is also on the battlefield, she gets +5 power and draws a card, refilling the hand you need for the rest of the combo. If you control multiple [Molten-Core Maestro](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/125/molten-core-maestro) or yours is big enough, you can even use [Stormchaser's Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/75/stormchasers-talent) on the battlefield to get [Ashling's Command](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/205/ashlings-command) back and keep this combo running.

* **Sanar, Unfinished Genius**
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Prismari-Shantotto/49bbcea3d565.webp)
[Sanar](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/223/sanar-unfinished-genius-wild-idea) is a great 2-mana 0/4 early blocker. Whenever you cast an instant or sorcery, it can tap to make a Treasure. Its enters-prepared (Prepare) side, Wild Idea, not only triggers Opus once but also tutors [Ashling's Command](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/205/ashlings-command) to your hand, officially announcing the start of the big-spell bombardment.

## Deck Analysis

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Prismari-Shantotto/a74ca508f59a.webp)
[**Decklist**](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7746815#paper)


Besides the cards above, the list has two key noncreature permanents, [Stormchaser's Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/75/stormchasers-talent) and [Ral, Crackling Wit](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/230/ral-crackling-wit), as steady support for winning. The second-level ability of [Stormchaser's Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/75/stormchasers-talent) can return an instant or sorcery from the graveyard, and when mana is plentiful it can recur [Ashling's Command](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/205/ashlings-command) to keep pushing the combo. [Ral, Crackling Wit](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/230/ral-crackling-wit) is a solid 4-mana noncreature play on curve: he triggers [Shantotto](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/241/shantotto-tactician-magician)'s card draw, keeps making prowess [Otter](https://scryfall.com/card/tblb/25/otter) threats to pressure the opponent, and improves your hand quality.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Prismari-Shantotto/32a634ab30d3.webp)

For spell selection, the focus is on spells with **flexible costs** to make sure you can clear Opus's 5-mana threshold.

* **Kicker and Spree**

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Prismari-Shantotto/733f42cdcfbd.webp)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Prismari-Shantotto/62f8c91c364c.webp)

We put in plenty of spells that can be strengthened by paying extra. That includes [Burst Lightning](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/192/burst-lightning), which deals 4 when kicked, the bounce spell [Into the Roil](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/509/into-the-roil), which draws a card when kicked, and the deep-filtering [Consult the Star Charts](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/51/consult-the-star-charts), which takes one more card when kicked. For interaction we chose the spree counterspell [Three Steps Ahead](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/75/three-steps-ahead), which flexibly answers the opponent's big plays and triggers Opus, and can also copy [Molten-Core Maestro](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/125/molten-core-maestro) to speed up the combo.

* **X Spells**

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Prismari-Shantotto/6aae66f404f9.webp)

X spells are the most reliable way to trigger Opus. [Torch the Witness](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/146/torch-the-witness) is extremely strong creature removal: 2 mana deals 2, 3 mana deals 4, 4 mana deals 6, and any excess damage turns into [Clue](https://scryfall.com/card/tmkm/18/clue) tokens to refill your hand. [Traumatic Critique](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/239/traumatic-critique) is this deck's finishing fireball: besides instantly burning a creature or going straight to the face, it comes with draw two, discard one, so early on it can also be a two-mana instant-speed looter and never clogs your hand.

* **Utility Support**


![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Prismari-Shantotto/ff570732c275.webp)

Besides the variable-cost spells above, we also run two efficient support cards: the versatile [Prismari Charm](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/211/prismari-charm), which handles all sorts of situations, and the instant-speed removal [Sear](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/154/sear), which reliably kills mid-sized creatures and fills the removal gaps in midrange matchups.


## Gameplay Demo

{%youtube KUEEWrRyzZM %}

As the video shows, the deck's early tempo is close to a midrange deck. It uses spells to disrupt the opponent's development and uses [Otter](https://scryfall.com/card/tblb/25/otter) to bait out the opponent's removal, raising the survival rate of key creatures. Deep filtering with [Consult the Star Charts](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/51/consult-the-star-charts) finds the core pieces, and once the conditions are met, huge amounts of mana and a massive body end the game quickly. Even without assembling the combo, the midrange structure itself has solid fighting power. Note that this color pair has quite limited removal, and the combo requires precise mana calculation, so it takes extra care to pilot.

## Conclusion

Building the deck as more of a midrange list is the author's personal preference.
