Outlaws of Thunder Junction (OTJ) will hold its prerelease events on April 12, 2024, and officially launch one week later on April 19, 2024. Want to learn more about single-card ratings in Limited and some potentially fun pairings? Then check out this OTJ Limited primer series!

For the mechanics, see this article: [Outlaws of Thunder Junction Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/OTJ-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Must-take, unconditionally
4.0: Worth a first pick
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you'll really want it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick in a draft
2.0-1.0: Below average (you'd rather not play it than play it)
0.5-0.0: Unplayable (basically never play it; it will only help you lose)

## Red

![115](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/d/bd13011e-a4fc-4107-988f-60cfc851ecd3.jpg?1712094968)

**T1BloodMoon:** 2.0

Fits the red-blue second-spell theme and you can meet the condition through plot, but I don't think the payoff is very high. I wouldn't pick this as my primary archetype card.

![116](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/7/e7a70f5a-2056-4c26-b6ea-9f751b5d0d8c.jpg?1711746397)

**T1BloodMoon:** 4.5

Comes in swinging and makes a 2/2 body. The crew cost isn't high either. Once it lands, it puts enormous pressure on your opponent.

![117](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/a/3a2fd0c4-509e-49c2-ad57-f772efcbc207.jpg?1712094975)

**T1BloodMoon:** 3.0

This set has a huge number of creatures with toughness 2 or less (66 of them), and 69 outlaws. A very nice instant-speed way to clear the board. Note that after picking this up, you may want to check whether your creature base leans toward outlaws or non-outlaws.

![118](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/5/b/5b4ac6ea-c67b-4f90-be4f-aa25882f5794.jpg?1711570022)

**T1BloodMoon:** 3.0

A two-drop haste creature of good quality. Mid to late game it can come down off a 2-for-1 and attack with haste right away, giving your opponent unexpected damage pressure.

![119](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/9/e9a50b7a-8741-4520-8d45-8e6b128c2628.jpg?1712004799)

**T1BloodMoon:** 2.5

This set has quite a few 1-2 power flyers early on, and this blocks them very effectively. If your deck is built around committing crimes, triggering it every turn makes it worth even more.

![120](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/5/b/5b491d11-d00a-4541-8389-2785a455eeee.jpg?1712094984)

**T1BloodMoon:** 3.0

An aura-style skin that grows the creature and gives trample, doesn't lose you a card when it goes to the graveyard, and has no drawbacks (such as being unable to block). A great skin.

![121](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/7/3/73b359bf-afd8-439d-a4d2-985db1ad368c.jpg?1711664708)

**T1BloodMoon:** 2.0

Usually used to fill out a basic two-drop curve.

![122](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/f/0/f0e3df9c-0a86-4e6f-a3c7-84a883328a3d.jpg?1711742411)

**T1BloodMoon:** 3.0

Flexible removal. Only about 20 creatures, roughly 1 in 8, have toughness above 4 and survive it. Being instant speed gives great timing, and considering how many powerful artifacts this set has, its value goes up even further.

![123](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/7/8/78db4260-6fc8-4500-afd7-2c845ac0d53b.jpg?1711812402)

**T1BloodMoon:** 3.0

An extremely strong enchantment for aggressive creature decks. It applies damage pressure early, and even if you get swept late, any creature you draw afterwards keeps up the pressure. If it resolves, your opponent is in for a headache.

![124](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/f/5/f568803d-65c0-48d7-916f-671267a9e00e.jpg?1711709707)

**T1BloodMoon:** 1.5

Having crew filter a card is a nice ability, but the body can't come out ahead in trades. You'll probably often be unable to attack with it, or it'll die after filtering only one card, so I rate it low.

![125](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/5/357dd9b2-5d2d-49f6-86f0-f5c4d63474dd.jpg?1711496383)

**T1BloodMoon:** 3.5

Fits both go-wide and crime decks. Very flexible at instant speed, and late in the game it can serve as a finisher.

![126](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/4/84ad8ed7-1429-432e-8217-a4db3b97675c.jpg?1708701733)

**T1BloodMoon:** 2.5

A sorcery-speed removal spell that gets stronger the later the game goes, but it isn't something I'd take first.

![127](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/b/3b99db5b-cd13-4e69-98b7-753e72c781f8.jpg?1712095003)

**T1BloodMoon:** 3.0

Its best tempo is being cast on turn 3 after a 1-drop and 2-drop. In addition, the 1/1 tokens in this set are outlaws too, which lowers its expected cost. A big creature that can sometimes be cast for just one mana is pretty good in this format.

![128](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/5/f/5f348c7e-7d72-40f1-a65f-ee7ff4b09412.jpg?1711664773)

**T1BloodMoon:** 3.5

It has go-wide, outlaw synergy and haste all in one: creatures that enter can immediately get a boost and apply pressure to your opponent. Undeniably a strong card.

![129](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/1/31a88429-9204-4a23-a7a8-babbd6bab79f.jpg?1712004857)

**T1BloodMoon:** 2.0

Early on it's a loot-two-for-one discard, and late game it can be a land dump for two cards. The plot ability fits better with the red-blue second-spell theme.

![130](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/2/324c0af5-7cdf-4c71-84cc-6349f10e0d66.jpg?1711668209)

**T1BloodMoon:** 1.5

It always triggers on entering, but without plot support early on it's hard to use the exiled card after casting this. Unless you need it for your curve, I wouldn't consider it for my deck.

![131](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/c/d/cd7f984a-0b56-45df-958d-6178e4da61ed.jpg?1712004889)

**T1BloodMoon:** 2.5

A 4/5 plotter with a solid body, and its ability fits the red-blue archetype and the crime theme well.

![132](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/9/398d9a16-d72c-42e2-a0ea-d9da642ee046.jpg?1711665609)

**T1BloodMoon:** 2.0

Plot is expensive here. Skipping a turn on 4 mana to deal only 2 isn't strong enough. Casting it as a three-drop might be a bit better, but only a bit. If your opponent doesn't have many flyers, it's just a vanilla creature.

![133](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/4/4443d112-209b-49ec-bc40-3a11dcdb092e.jpg?1711496941)

**T1BloodMoon:** 3.0

A standard two-mana 2/2 that makes a Treasure when you commit a crime. If you don't use it, you can use it to call down a flying haste Dragon. A staple card in crime decks.

![134](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/6/2/62e12566-375f-4f31-aa91-1b13a96d9ece.jpg?1711671368)

**T1BloodMoon:** 1.0

Even when you make it yourself it gets hit. Ouch.

![135](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/9/19cfacff-e884-4954-aa6b-ed56ca942bf2.jpg?1711664806)

**T1BloodMoon:** 2.5

Think of it as a two-mana 3/2 with trample. It makes a Treasure on entering with an easy condition. A pretty good card.

![136](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/f/7/f7502b9c-b759-499a-8e94-22f87f5eb142.jpg?1711812448)

**T1BloodMoon:** 2.5

A quality way to grow a creature, and if you're playing an outlaw deck you can gain cards as well. It can also be used simply as a combat trick or as a loot effect.

![137](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/7/e70f2278-8857-46e4-aa2b-fff5589b750f.jpg?1712004922)

**T1BloodMoon:** 2.5

One-for-one on entering, with two outlaws total. Plainly, simply strong.

A rather unusual combat trick that can suddenly make your opponent's creature lose a trade.
