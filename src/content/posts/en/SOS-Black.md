Secrets of Strixhaven (SOS) prerelease events run on April 17, 2026, with the official release one week later on April 24, 2026. Want to know more about how individual cards rate in Limited, plus some pick advice? Then check out this series of SOS Limited overviews!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Secrets of Strixhaven Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/SOS-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick; gives you a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors; gives you a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick; synergizes extremely well with the archetype, and can be the deciding card for splashing / switching colors or moving into a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards; synergizes well with the archetype and can be the key card for moving into a color pair
3.0: Important cards for the archetype, plus other above-average cards; you want these in your final deck
2.5: Average; usually a decent curve filler
2.0–1.0: Below average; you generally prefer not to play them, can be used as filler if necessary, but not too many
0.5–0.0: Unplayable; basically never play them, they will only help you lose

## Conclusion
I made a ㄅ-to-ㄦ rating chart myself, but the resolution is a bit sad, so just treat it as a little Easter egg.
![Black ㄅ-tier chart](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/SOS-Black/2aaf342acaca.webp)

This is a multicolor set. Looking at it overall, the mono-colored cards basically feel like they exist to pair with multicolor cards; their stats and effects are only acceptable on their own. Also, cards that do many things basically cannot score too low, unless both halves are bad. And from any color, the power level of commons and uncommons has clearly inflated. Maybe in the future the [Grizzly Bears](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/268/grizzly-bears) of the world, a common without a positive ability or two won't count as a common anymore XD.
