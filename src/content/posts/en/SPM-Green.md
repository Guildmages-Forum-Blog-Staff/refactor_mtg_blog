Marvel's Spider-Man (SPM) will hold its prerelease events on September 19, 2025, and officially launch one week later on September 26, 2025. Want to learn more about single-card ratings and recommendations in Limited? Then check out this SPM Limited primer series!

Also, due to licensing issues, the physical cards and the digital cards online (MTGA, MO) in this set will have different card names and different art, though the same rules text (with a few proper nouns still differing); therefore, this Limited primer shows both versions of the card art for readers to compare.

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Marvel's Spider-Man Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/SPM-Mechanism/)
- The physical set is named Marvel's Spider-Man ([SPM](https://scryfall.com/sets/spm))
- The online set is named Through the Omenpaths (tentative translation; English set name: Through the Omenpaths, [OM1](https://scryfall.com/sets/om1))

## Rating Scale

5.0: Must-take, unconditionally; gives you a near-winning advantage
4.5: Must-take if you're in the right colors; gives you a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick, with excellent synergy with the color pair's theme; can be the deciding card for splashing, switching colors, or moving into a color pair
3.5: The best uncommons and commons in the set and other cards of sufficient strength, with good synergy with the color pair's theme; can be the key card for moving into a color pair
3.0: Important cards for a color pair's theme and other above-average cards; you'll want them in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0-1.0: Below average; you generally prefer not to play them, but they can be filler when necessary (not too many)
0.5-0.0: Unplayable; basically never play them, as they will only help you lose

## Conclusion
The green themes this time include Web-Slinging and Large Spell. Large Spell checks the mana value of a spell you cast; spells cast via Web-Slinging or Mayhem still trigger it as long as their mana value exceeds 4, so keep that pairing in mind when building.
This set's color fixing is relatively weak, and green has four common and uncommon cards that help with fixing in total, an important source of fixing in this set. If you want to put multicolor bombs in your deck, green is a great choice as a secondary color.
