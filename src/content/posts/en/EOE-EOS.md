Edge of Eternities prerelease events kick off on July 25, 2025. Want to know more about how each card rates in Limited, with some advice? Then check out this series of EOE Limited guides!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Edge of Eternities Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/EOE-Mechanism/)

Edge of Eternities: Stellar Sights (EOS) is a supplementary subset of the main set Edge of Eternities, and on average one appears in every 8 regular Play Boosters. All 45 cards in the EOS subset are reprinted lands, each with a different name, and they can be used in EOE Limited as well as in the formats where each card was originally legal. If you are supporting the main set's subtheme of "when ..., if you control a number of lands with different names, then ...", EOS is sure to be your best partner!

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick, gives you a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors, gives you a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick, excellent synergy with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card for splashing/switching colors or moving into a color pair
3.5: The set's top uncommons and commons and other sufficiently powerful cards, good synergy with the color pair's theme, a key card for moving into a color pair
3.0: Important card for the color pair's theme, as well as other above-average cards you will want in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average, generally not played, can be used as filler if necessary but not too many
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable, basically never play it, doing so will only help you lose

## Conclusion
Edge of Eternities: Stellar Sights (EOS), as a supplementary subset of the main set Edge of Eternities, can be called rather exciting. Previous sets rarely had this many nonbasic lands, and they don't take up space from nonland cards in deck building, which will noticeably raise the pace and interaction of the whole Limited format and let players experience a somewhat different feel of play. We wish everyone a great time playing!
