Marvel's Spider-Man (SPM) prerelease events run on September 19, 2025, with the official release one week later on September 26, 2025. Want to know more about how individual cards rate in Limited, plus some pick advice? Then check out this series of SPM Limited overviews!

Also, due to licensing issues, this set's physical cards and online digital cards (MTGA, MO) will be presented with different card names and different card art, but the same rules text (a few proper nouns on some cards still differ). Therefore, this Limited overview shows both card arts together so readers can compare.

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Marvel's Spider-Man Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/SPM-Mechanism/)
- The physical card set is named Marvel's Spider-Man ([SPM](https://scryfall.com/sets/spm))
- The online set is named Through the Omenpaths ([OM1](https://scryfall.com/sets/om1))

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick; gives you a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors; gives you a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick; synergizes extremely well with the archetype, and can be the deciding card for splashing / switching colors or moving into a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards; synergizes well with the archetype and can be the key card for moving into a color pair
3.0: Important cards for the archetype, plus other above-average cards; you want these in your final deck
2.5: Average; usually a decent curve filler
2.0–1.0: Below average; you generally prefer not to play them, can be used as filler if necessary, but not too many
0.5–0.0: Unplayable; basically never play them, they will only help you lose

## Conclusion

White this time does not have many especially outstanding cards, but every card performs steadily. Also, among the multicolor cards there are many hybrid-mana cards, such as [Gallant Citizen](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/129/gallant-citizen), [Mary Jane Watson](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/134/mary-jane-watson), and [Spider-Woman, Stunning Savior](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/152/spider-woman-stunning-savior), all quite worth putting in your deck, suiting players who like to build an advantage step by step and play it safe.
