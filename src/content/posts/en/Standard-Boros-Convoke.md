## Introduction

Hi everyone, I'm 楊承孟. I'm very happy to have made Top 8 at this MIT. The deck I played was Boros Convoke. I picked it because I think its style suits me well, I've played it for a long time, and I've done plenty of practice against most of the mainstream decks in the format, so after weighing everything I chose Boros Convoke.

## Deck Overview

[Aven Interrupter](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/4/aven-interrupter) is for control and Domain matchups. Shutting off their sweeper basically means you get one extra turn to kill the opponent.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Convoke/ae237581b9ae.webp)

[Kutzil's Flanker](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/20/kutzils-flanker) is also for control matchups: after a sweeper, you can flash in another big creature to keep up the pressure. As for graveyard hate, I brought an extra [Rest in Peace](https://scryfall.com/card/big/4/rest-in-peace), originally planning to use it against Temur Fireball and 4C Legends. But after thinking it over, there's no need for graveyard hate in those matchups; the right play is to flatten the opponent as fast as possible. In later updates to the list I'll take [Rest in Peace](https://scryfall.com/card/big/4/rest-in-peace) out.

## MIT Round 6 Report

Preliminary result: 5-0-2, ranked 2nd

### R1 Esper Control 2-0 on the draw

Game 1: When I saw the opponent's main-deck sweeper was only the 5-mana [Deadly Cover-Up](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/83/deadly-cover-up), I knew I should just flood the board and hit hard, and I killed them smoothly.
Game 2: The opponent's sideboard has 2 [Temporary Lockdown](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/36/temporary-lockdown), 1 [Path of Peril](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/124/path-of-peril), and no 2-mana [Pest Control](https://scryfall.com/card/big/22/pest-control), so the game plan was to combine [Warleader's Call](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/242/warleaders-call) and [Cavern of Souls](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls) to steadily chip away at their life total and win.

### R2 Esper Midrange 1-1 on the draw

Game 1: Won with a turn-2 convoke.
Game 2: Got watched to death late by [Sheoldred, the Apocalypse](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/107/sheoldred-the-apocalypse).
In game 2 I should have conceded quickly and moved on to game 3, where I'd be on the play to apply pressure. I misjudged it on the spot; I was thinking that if I drew a removal spell I could still win, but going straight to game 3 would have given me a somewhat higher win rate.

### R3 Esper Midrange 2-1 on the draw

Game 1: After working hard to build the board, [Imodane's Recruiter](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/229/imodanes-recruiter-train-troops) backed by an uncounterable [Cavern of Souls](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls) took them out in one wave.
Game 2: Facing a turn-2 [Dennick, Pious Apprentice](https://scryfall.com/card/mid/217/dennick-pious-apprentice-dennick-pious-apparition) and a turn-3 [Raffine, Scheming Seer](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/213/raffine-scheming-seer), I couldn't keep up with the tempo and died. The lesson is that I should have killed Raffine on turn 3. I don't know what I was thinking when I chose to kill Dennick instead, which let Raffine's scheming later swap my hand into a better combination.
Game 3: Won with a turn-2 convoke.

### R4 Temur Fireball 2-1 on the play

Game 1: Won with a turn-2 convoke.
Game 2: Turn 3 [Nissa, Resurgent Animist](https://scryfall.com/card/mat/22/nissa-resurgent-animist), turn 4 [Ill-Timed Explosion](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/207/ill-timed-explosion) as a sweeper, turn 5 [Borborygmos and Fblthp](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/219/borborygmos-and-fblthp), and I still had a [Bonny Pall, Clearcutter](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/196/bonny-pall-clearcutter) in hand, so I had to concede quickly.
Game 3: Turn 1 [Voldaren Epicure](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/182/voldaren-epicure), turn 2 [Warden of the Inner Sky](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/43/warden-of-the-inner-sky). After activating its ability I saw [Invasion of Gobakhan](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/22/invasion-of-gobakhan-lightshield-array) and kept it, blew up the Blood token and then put down another bean. On turn 3 I played Gobakhan and looked at their hand, and they responded by killing Warden of the Inner Sky. I saw a [Shigeki, Jukai Visionary](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/206/shigeki-jukai-visionary), a Nissa, and three lands, exiled Nissa, then flipped Gobakhan and started putting beans on the whole team. The opponent played Shigeki on turn 3, and I killed it on turn 4 with [Case of the Gateway Express](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/8/case-of-the-gateway-express). After drawing on their turn 4, the opponent conceded.

### R5 Temur Fireball 2-0 on the draw

Game 1: Mulliganed twice, then won with a turn-2 convoke.
Game 2: On turn 2 I looked at their hand and saw 2 [Vampires' Vengeance](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/289/vampires-vengeance). After confirming they had no accelerant creatures in hand, I exiled one of the Vampires' Vengeance and chose to play slowly, steadily deploying threats. Then one turn the opponent tapped out to cast Fireball to accelerate and set up a lethal next turn, so I flashed in [Resolute Reinforcements](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/29/resolute-reinforcements) and followed up with a hasty-for-everyone [Imodane's Recruiter](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/229/imodanes-recruiter-train-troops) to kill them.

### R6 Domain 2-1 on the play

Game 1: The opponent had no main-deck [Temporary Lockdown](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/36/temporary-lockdown); after a mulligan I won with a turn-2 convoke.
Game 2: The opponent played [Depopulate](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/10/depopulate) on turn 4, [Archangel of Wrath](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/3/archangel-of-wrath) on turn 5, and [Herd Migration](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/165/herd-migration) on turn 7 to make a pile of 3/3s, so I conceded quickly.
Game 3: After a mulligan I flashed in [Resolute Reinforcements](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/29/resolute-reinforcements) on turn 2 and played [Urabrask's Forge](https://scryfall.com/card/one/153/urabrasks-forge) on turn 3. Later, convoke found [Aven Interrupter](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/4/aven-interrupter) and [Imodane's Recruiter](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/229/imodanes-recruiter-train-troops). When my Forge had three counters, the opponent played [Tranquil Frillback](https://scryfall.com/card/mat/24/tranquil-frillback) to destroy it, and I exiled it with [Aven Interrupter](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/4/aven-interrupter). The next turn I played the Recruiter and killed them together with the board.

### TOP8 Boros Convoke 1-2 on the play

Game 1: I built my board fast, so I won.
Game 2: Soloed by [Warden of the Inner Sky](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/43/warden-of-the-inner-sky).
Game 3: Soloed by [Warden of the Inner Sky](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/43/warden-of-the-inner-sky) again.

After reviewing it over the past few days, I think my opening hand should have been winnable. If I had played a haste creature on turn 3 to hit them for a wave of damage, and then on turn 4 played convoke plus removal, the tempo would have swung back to my side. Since I'd already convoked, I'd have had more creatures in hand and could have won on card advantage. All I can say is that I misjudged it on the spot.

## Deck Conclusion

Boros Convoke is a deck that demands aggressive mulliganing. We need to mulligan to find the best solution. With open decklists, the information you have lets you work out how many turns you can keep applying pressure. The main thing to watch for in the sideboard is basically how many sweepers the opponent brought, and holding back the right cards after sideboarding is the key to keeping up the pressure and winning.
