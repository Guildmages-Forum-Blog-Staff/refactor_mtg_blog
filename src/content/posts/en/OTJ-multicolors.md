Outlaws of Thunder Junction (OTJ) will hold its prerelease events on April 12, 2024, and officially release a week later on April 19, 2024. Want to know more about card ratings for Limited and some potentially fun synergies? Then check out this series of OTJ Limited primers!

For the mechanics, see this article: [Outlaws of Thunder Junction Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/OTJ-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional must-pick
4.0: First-pick quality
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you will really want it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick in a draft
2.0 to 1.0: Below average (you'd rather not play it than play it)
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable (basically don't play it; it will only help you lose)

![189](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/6/8/68fd8548-50db-4243-9154-377f32408d58.jpg?1712007585)

**cephille:** 3.5

A 5/5 flying trampling Scorpion Dragon with four colored mana requirements. The color requirements are so heavy that even in a straight black-red deck it won't necessarily arrive on time on turn four, which cost it some points. The ability's price is a bit high, but there is a chance to sacrifice three little guys ~~to summon Blue-Eyes White Dragon~~ to put a high-cost or color-screwed fatty from your hand onto the battlefield. It may be useful, but in Limited, where creatures are king, trading three for one may not be so great.

![190](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/d/8d4af7c3-a70d-4f71-b27d-b268c4a0f81e.jpg?1711498000)

**cephille:** 3.5

A six-mana 4/5 with flash and three colored mana requirements. When she enters, she digs for a permanent with mana value 3 or less to put onto the battlefield tapped, and whenever she becomes tapped, you exile the top two cards of your library and may play them this turn. Getting two for one on entering is basically no loss and may give you an advantageous blocker, and next turn, when you attack, saddle, or crew, you can impulse-draw extra cards. But note that her second ability exiles two cards whenever she becomes tapped, and this is not optional. So when the opponent taps your Annie on their turn, unless those two cards are instants or have flash, it effectively just exiles the top two cards of your library, so keep that interaction in mind in Limited.

![191](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/6/1624a5f4-f5bc-47c9-85de-c5520ee234ce.jpg?1712356038)

**cephille:** 2.5

A four-mana legendary enchantment with three colored mana requirements. On entering, it deals 5 damage to an opposing creature or planeswalker, which is really a far cry from the other excellent red removal spells (once again it's you, [Scorching Shot](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/145/scorching-shot), your presence is just too bright!). The second part, letting a legendary creature's triggered abilities trigger an additional time, depends too much on synergy. If the same pack had other good mono-colored removal, I wouldn't take it, so I can only give it an average score.

![192](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/0/1/014bf3c6-e46f-48f8-902f-82deeba260b2.jpg?1711667315)

**cephille:** 4.0

A creature-only equipment version of [Touch the Spirit Realm](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/40/touch-the-spirit-realm). Then, paying two mana to equip lets it become the creature exiled by the equipment, which is like spending five mana to grab the opponent's big threat for yourself, and that is quite powerful. Don't forget it can also exile a creature you control, which may have wonderful uses at the right moment!

![193](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/9/897d594d-b5b0-43dc-b877-b483942416ce.jpg?1712356046)

**cephille:** 3.0

A three-mana enchantment. On your turn, Outlaws have first strike, and when you commit a crime you can create a 1/1 red Mercenary (Outlaw). Although it can only trigger once per turn, if the opportunity arises we can take multiple copies of this enchantment and repeatedly commit crimes on our own turn and the opponent's turn to build up an Outlaw army!

**cephille:** 3.0

A five-mana spell that digs up a permanent from the graveyard and returns it to hand, well suited to a black-green deck that excels at grinding out long games.

![195](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/9/e9da18b4-1efc-44b7-8001-a2cfd44c69bf.jpg?1712356055)

**cephille:** 3.0

A four-mana 3/4. Whenever you create one or more tokens each turn, you can create an additional black 1/1 Vampire with lifelink. Although it triggers only once per turn, that's enough. There is also an activated ability that lets you pay two mana to sacrifice another creature or artifact to draw a card. In a token deck it will be an important payoff, but if you aren't, you don't have to play it.

![196](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/3/4383ae7c-58ea-4354-93e4-677ad185c3bb.jpg?1711665833)

**cephille:** 5.0

A six-mana 6/5 Giant with vigilance. It enters with a giant ox whose power and toughness equal the number of lands you control... uh... so that means at least 12 power for six mana, and one of them keeps getting bigger? Okay, let's keep reading: when you attack, you can draw a card and then put a land card from your graveyard or hand onto the battlefield... Hmm, wonderful! I arrive on time on turn six to make 12 power on the board, and if I have a creature that can attack that turn, when I attack I can draw a card, maybe keep ramping, and my ox gets even bigger...

If [Izoni, Center of the Web](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/209/izoni-center-of-the-web) was the apex bomb of MKM, then this blue-green six-drop is likely the king of OTJ bombs. I hope I'm the one who opens it and the opponent doesn't have it, or has it but can't reach six mana. In Limited, when I face this card from an opponent, aside from a sweeper or two removal spells, I don't know how I can win...

![197](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/c/f/cf3bda9e-42af-4f99-a504-c96c25c2794b.jpg?1712356062)

**cephille:** 3.0

With Malcolm here, how could [Breeches, Eager Pillager](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/137/breeches-eager-pillager) be absent? A three-mana 3/3 with menace. Whenever you cast your second spell each turn, you may sacrifice an artifact; if you do, flip a coin, and if you win, copy that spell; if you lose, that's okay, since you can deal damage to any target equal to the sacrificed artifact's mana value. Considering that the artifacts in a blue-red deck are probably zero-mana ones like Treasures, it's still worth a gamble. Winning the flip and copying a spell is quite attractive.

![198](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/2/8/286c55c2-dcc1-4e87-a83f-9981d28ab62d.jpg?1711655193)

**cephille:** 3.5

[Bruse Tarl, Boorish Herder](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/30/bruse-tarl-boorish-herder) from Commander 2016 is a great partner for a herd of oxen! A four-mana 4/3 that exiles the top card of your library when it enters or attacks. If it's a land, you create a 2/2 Ox (which gains double strike from Bruse Tarl's static ability!). If it's a nonland card, you may cast it until the end of your next turn. Both outcomes are profitable, and you only need to protect your Bruse Tarl carefully, and soon he will lead his herd to trample your opponent!

**cephille:** 2.5

This is how red-white Mercenaries build an army. Any deck in this color pair, or touching either red or white, that needs to produce lots of tokens to gain an advantage can play it (for example, with cards like [Kambal, Profiteering Mayor](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor) or [Laughing Jasper Flint](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/215/laughing-jasper-flint), you can consider splashing red/white and playing this sorcery to chase a higher ceiling). However, judging by its value for creating tokens, X likely needs to be at least 3 to be worth it; if you play this color pair and get stuck on three or four lands with a hand full of this card, it is a bit awkward.

[Ghired, Conclave Exile](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/42/ghired-conclave-exile), who debuted in Commander 2019, also makes an appearance in Standard. A three-mana 3/3 with haste and three colored mana requirements. If you can't play it on time on turn three, its value from an attacking perspective drops a lot. The static ability gives your nontoken creatures an additional tap ability to create a token that entered under your control this turn. If you play red-white Mercenaries, red-green Treasures, or other token-producing strategies, you can consider splashing a third color for extra resources, but it is still mostly the icing on the cake.

![206](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/2/82512813-8618-483b-a7f0-e6a611d9d487.jpg?1711498218)

**cephille:** 5.0

[The Gitrog Monster](https://scryfall.com/card/soi/245/the-gitrog-monster) gets better with every version. A five-mana 6/5 with haste is already powerful enough, and after it connects you can sacrifice the creature saddling it. If you do, you draw X cards equal to the sacrificed creature's power, and put up to X lands from your hand onto the battlefield tapped. Just connecting once and drawing a pile is already a huge profit, and you can often do it multiple times. The Gitrog Monster is invincibly strong, and I hope I get to open one in Limited too!

If you see him and your deck touches at least black or green, grab him. You won't be disappointed!

**cephille:** 1.5

Could you be the long-lost brother of [Disinformation Campaign](https://scryfall.com/card/grn/167/disinformation-campaign)? Although discarding is replaced by each opponent losing one life while you gain one, which is much weaker (so this enchantment entering the battlefield doesn't make you commit a crime), committing a crime is still much easier than surveilling. I think it is still a payoff card for blue-black or Grixis decks that care a lot about the commit-a-crime mechanic. ![210](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/f/e/fe30b5c8-4889-4350-bb1d-3e2a67d9dfb2.jpg?1711504239)

**cephille:** 3.0

A three-mana 4/2 isn't great by itself; she should be paired with other red-green creatures with power 4 or greater to make Treasures. She can make them on her own too, but once she attacks she probably won't come back. Interestingly, if the opponent has two 1/1s and you attack with her, you can sacrifice the Treasure created after attacking to kill one of the 1/1s and keep her alive; likewise she can trade with an opposing x/5 creature this way. When you've made enough Treasures you can also use her to finish the game or as a Treasure machine gun to shoot down the opponent's little guys. She's the type with poor stats but a helpful ability, so I'll give her an above-average score for now.

![211](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/5/d53a775d-5898-41a8-b404-9b7d4721c6ba.jpg?1711742980)

**cephille:** 3.5

[Kambal, Consul of Allocation](https://scryfall.com/card/kld/183/kambal-consul-of-allocation) was a real jerk when he first debuted in Kaladesh, and this time is no exception. A three-mana 2/4 is already top-tier stats, and whenever you or an opponent creates a token, it creates a huge advantage for you. It is suited to building around by creating lots of tokens to make the opponent lose a lot of life, and the first time each turn the opponent makes a token, not only do you get a free tapped copy, but they also lose another life. It's the kind of card that may not necessarily lock up the win, but draws maximum hate. Before making tokens, the opponent has to consider the consequences of letting you freeload. And if your opponent doesn't notice its effect... then congratulations, you've made a killing!

![212](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/2/e/2e7f95d5-b279-4469-9c89-1e02630d61e6.jpg?1711665791)

**cephille:** 3.0

Plotting a card with mana value 3 or less on entering is already quite good, and when your deck has enough legendary creatures, every one that enters lets you trigger the whole table! If the card plotted on entering is a legendary creature, it can start working next turn. Taking this enchantment and then collecting enough legendary creatures should be a powerful draft approach. Even if the colors are wrong, as long as the cost is three or less, you can still plot and cast it with this enchantment, which is quite potent!

![213](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/0/4/04dfbc4c-ab21-45db-bbd9-b9d245d60015.jpg?1711498408)

**cephille:** 3.0

A three-mana 3/3 flying lifelinker with three colored mana requirements is very strong. But its ability looks like it needs to link with plotting or other impulse-draw mechanics in Limited, and when it triggers successfully you can cast from hand a permanent card with total mana value equal to or less than that spell, and if you don't, you may instead put a land onto the battlefield.

Uh... I really don't think this passive ability is very profitable in Limited. First you have to plot, and you also need the right cards in hand, which is not easy to pull off as a way to gain resources; and even if it works, merely putting a land onto the battlefield isn't that great. So this card's score is stats plus ability: not bad, but not worth a first pick, and don't forget it has three colored mana requirements, so it may not come down on time on turn three.

A three-mana 4/3. If it survives the turn, starting from your next upkeep it starts stealing from the opponent (NTR-style), and if you cast their creatures this way, they can become your endless Mercenary battalion, growing your number of Outlaws more and more! Besides completing the five enemy-color fastlands, this set also has 10 two-color Deserts, all of which deal 1 damage to the opponent on entering, and this effect happens to link with this set's mechanic **commit a crime**, so these 10 two-color lands also have a nickname: "**crime deserts**."

Basically, all two-color fixing lands are worth 3.0, and considering that a single land can pair with both commit a crime and the Desert theme, these two-color Deserts may deserve an even higher pick order in a draft.

## Conclusion

The multicolor cards of Outlaws of Thunder Junction should be quite fun to use in Limited, but I'm still a bit worried that cards with three or more colors will be unplayable because of fixing difficulties. When drafting, picking fixing may become even more important in pick order, and taking Treasures or other fixing means will also matter more for two-color decks that need to splash a third color. I hope everyone has fun in the coming OTJ Limited season!
