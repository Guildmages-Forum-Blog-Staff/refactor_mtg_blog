The Lost Caverns of Ixalan (LCI) holds its prerelease events on November 10, 2023, with the official release a week later on November 17, 2023. Want to know more about how each card rates in Limited and some interesting possible combinations? Then check out this LCI Limited review series!

For the mechanics, see this article: [The Lost Caverns of Ixalan Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/LCI-mechanic/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick
4.0: Worth a first pick
3.5: Strong enough but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you would very much like it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick in a draft
2.0 to 1.0: Below average (you would rather not play it than play it)
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable (basically don't play it; playing it will only help you lose)

**cephille**: 3.5
A 1/5 body for three mana is very sturdy. At the beginning of each player's end step, if an artifact entered the battlefield under your control that turn, you can cast a [Nagging Thoughts](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/6/d65332fe-44d2-440b-b481-5702ff41c1b4.jpg?1562709727) effect. As long as you pair it with some artifacts with flash, or cards like [Brackish Blunder](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/e/dea3d0f4-76f5-416f-93ba-8b003b967816.jpg?1699043535) and [Ancestors' Aid](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/9/e90d5b7d-e27d-42d6-ba7a-d4a6b0dd8549.jpg?1699044222) that can instantly make artifact tokens, you can steadily gain card advantage.

![047](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/LCI-Blue/e67201564051.webp)

**cephille**: 4.0
After it enters, you get three chances to remove a net counter from it, which can tap a nonland permanent, and while it stays tapped its activated abilities can't be used. Although it isn't like [Icy Manipulator](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/f/9/f9632147-7a55-4a4c-a3ec-de3a3916ad8b.jpg?1645607787), which you can use over and over for great effect, it adds the ability to lock down activated abilities, and used well it should be able to disrupt the opponent's plans and gain an advantage. In Limited, you should also get to see the amusing sight of planeswalkers or Cases tapped down and unable to move.

And Craft costs very little, so after it transforms you can pair it with your artifacts for a big draw, and it also puts the card back as the third card from the top of your library so you can keep using it, tapping over and over for great effect!

> Small reminder: cards with text like "for as long as..." only have their effect last until the condition stops being met. So once the nonland permanent locked down by this net untaps, the lock ends, and it will not stay locked just because the permanent becomes tapped again. You can refer to the text of cards like [Quicksmith Spy](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/0/f/0f922e87-1744-4966-9e3a-54917f7f3d9e.jpg?1576381538) (_after Quicksmith Spy leaves the battlefield, the designated artifact no longer has the "tap: draw a card" ability_) to help understand!

![055](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/LCI-Blue/07d002842def.webp)

**cephille**: 3.0
This set has quite a lot of artifacts (counting Treasure and Map tokens too) and other permanents with activated abilities. As a one-drop it can't be used the turn it enters, but afterward it makes your activated abilities quite smooth, and it can perhaps be seen as a weaker or nearly equivalent [Training Grounds](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/9/9/991c27ed-f53c-48c6-8c12-282f44b8d441.jpg?1693171015). As for Craft, it needs nine mana plus four nonland cards with activated abilities, which at first glance may seem unlikely to achieve, but if your deck is good at making Treasure and Map tokens, transforming it may not be that hard. After it transforms, besides being able to use the activated abilities of the cards exiled to pay for Craft, it also lets you copy each of your activated abilities that isn't a mana ability once. Just sacrificing a Map to explore twice is already a great deal. Of course, if you have no abilities worth using, simply treating it as a small Training Grounds without transforming is also fine.

Of course, don't forget that it can produce mana first and then use that mana to pay for its own Craft ability! So in practice it only takes seven mana to activate.

![056](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/LCI-Blue/48f5db3c4e22.webp)

**cephille**: 3.0
I would treat it as a Divination that triggers artifacts entering and speeds up descend. Descend 8 is somewhat hard to reach, but it's not impossible. If you can reach it and transform it, besides getting an extra land, if you spend the mana it produces on casting a permanent spell, another of your permanents can become a copy of it until end of turn. Basically, it's likely that you cast a creature and make a land to attack with. In short, it is a Divination with a very high ceiling, and Divination should do well in more midrange-leaning environments, so I believe it can perform quite well in LCI too.

This is a great tempo card. But remember that as soon as the Merfolk leaves the battlefield, the locked card's abilities return to normal, so you still need to take the chance to remove the opponent's bomb to eliminate the lingering threat.

> Incidentally, Craft is also an activated ability. Exiling itself and the other designated materials is the cost, and transforming is the effect. If you use this Merfolk to counter a Craft ability, besides countering the transform effect, the cards exiled as the activation cost are also gone forever.

**cephille**: 3.0
This Aura turns your artifact into a 5/4 artifact creature, and when it goes from the battlefield to the graveyard you can discover 3. When you have artifacts like Treasures on the battlefield that aren't of any use yet, you can spend three mana to cast it and make a big guy to join combat, and you also have a chance to discover 3 when it hits the graveyard and immediately gain resources. A comparison that comes to mind is [Mightstone's Animation](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/c/8c540e42-22e2-4127-bbd9-2e9200023fec.jpg?1674420567) from BRO. In LCI, also an artifact environment, I think this three-mana Aura will perform better, and discover 3 usually brings a board advantage more directly than drawing a card.
