## Monocolored Hybrid Mana
![Hybrid Mana](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TDM-mechanism/4735831b48f5.webp)

Hybrid mana is back, but this time the combination is a less common one. The monocolored hybrid mana symbols in this set can be paid with either 2 mana of any color or 1 mana of the specified color, and for this kind of hybrid mana, whichever side you choose to pay with, the higher side is used when calculating mana value.

For example, if you cast [Rakshasa's Bargain](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/214/rakshasas-bargain), even if you paid the required specified colors and spent only 3 mana in total to cast it, it is still counted as a spell with mana value 6, and so it can be countered by [Disdainful Stroke](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/47/disdainful-stroke). By the same logic, [Rakshasa's Bargain](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/214/rakshasas-bargain) in the graveyard is a six-mana card, so to pay for [Deadly Cover-Up](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/83/deadly-cover-up)'s collect evidence 6, you only need to exile this one card.




## Endure
![Endure](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TDM-mechanism/1eabbe92a201.webp)

Representative color pair: Abzan (black-white-green)

Endure is a new keyword action, always followed by a number N. Endure N means you can put N +1/+1 counters on this creature, or create an N/N white Spirit creature token.

You choose which to do only when endure resolves, so if the creature that triggered endure is removed in response before the ability resolves, you can still choose to create the N/N token.




## Mobilize
![Mobilize](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TDM-mechanism/37f71c40f2ce.webp)

Representative color pair: Mardu (red-black-white)

Mobilize is a triggered ability that triggers when attacking, always followed by a number N. Mobilize N means "Whenever this creature attacks, create N tapped and attacking 1/1 red Warrior creature tokens. Sacrifice them at the beginning of the next end step."

These tokens are already attacking when they enter, and were never declared as attackers, so abilities that trigger "whenever a creature attacks" don't trigger. Also, you can freely choose which target they are attacking when they enter the battlefield, and it doesn't have to be the same target the mobilize creature that made them is attacking.

After a mobilize ability triggers, even if that creature is removed in response, the attacking tokens can still be created when mobilize resolves. Likewise, whether or not the mobilize creature that made the tokens is still on the battlefield, the "sacrifice them" trigger will still go off at the end step.




## Flurry
![Flurry](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TDM-mechanism/f6f5aaf02f20.webp)

Representative color pair: Jeskai (red-blue-white)


Flurry triggers whenever each player casts their second spell of the turn, and the effect after triggering depends on each card's description.

Flurry tracks all spell-casting records this turn. A permanent with flurry must be on the battlefield and see you cast your second spell of the turn for it to trigger. If a card with flurry happens to be the second spell you cast this turn, then it has no chance to trigger flurry this turn.




## Renew
![Renew](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TDM-mechanism/a6da6fc58351.webp)

Representative color pair: Sultai (blue-black-green)

Renew is an activated ability activated from the graveyard. The activation cost always includes exiling this card, and it can only be activated at sorcery speed. The effect after activation depends on each card's description, but the effects in this set all involve placing counters.




## Harmonize
![Harmonize](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TDM-mechanism/65b9773c888c.webp)

Representative color pair: Temur (red-green-blue)

When a card with harmonize is in the graveyard, you can cast it from the graveyard by paying its harmonize cost (this is a kind of alternative cost). When casting, you can choose to tap a creature with power X as an additional cost, and if you do, the spell's cost is reduced by X, but this can't reduce the colored part of the cost.

Of course, if you don't want to, or can't, tap your creature, it's perfectly fine to just pay the full harmonize mana cost.

A spell cast with harmonize is exiled when it leaves the stack (this includes resolving normally and being countered).



## Behold
![Behold](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TDM-mechanism/5be0729a63c4.webp)

Behold is a keyword action. To behold means to reveal a Dragon card from your hand, or choose a Dragon you control (you only choose it, you don't target it).

Most of the behold cards in this set are designed as an optional additional cost when casting a spell, and as long as you perform behold, you gain some additional effects.




## Omen
![Omen](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TDM-mechanism/a4ad191eadf8.webp)

Omen is very similar to adventure, and even the card frame art style is nearly the same, but the two are different mechanics! When you see this kind of card in the future, look carefully at the difference in the type line.

Just like adventure, when you cast the creature side, you can completely ignore the omen section's description, and it's simply a plain creature.

And if you cast the omen's instant/sorcery spell, when that spell resolves successfully, it doesn't go to the graveyard but is shuffled back into its owner's library.

An omen card only has the characteristics of an instant/sorcery while on the stack. At all other times it only has the characteristics of the permanent side (including name, type, cost, and so on). Take [Marang River Regent](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/51/marang-river-regent-coil-and-catch) as an example: if you try to use some effect to return an instant/sorcery card from your graveyard to your hand, you can't return [Marang River Regent](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/51/marang-river-regent-coil-and-catch), and when you cast the omen-side instant spell [Coil and Catch](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/51/marang-river-regent-coil-and-catch), on the stack it is an instant spell with a total mana value of 4 named "Coil and Catch."
