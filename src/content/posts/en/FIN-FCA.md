## Grading Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick, gives you a nearly game-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors, gives you a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick, synergizes extremely well with a color pair's theme, and can be the deciding card for splashing/switching colors or settling into a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards, synergize well with a color pair's theme, and can be key cards for settling into a color pair
3.0: Important cards for a color pair's theme, plus other above-average cards that you want in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average, generally not played, can be used as filler if necessary but not too many
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable, basically never play it, doing so only helps you lose

## Introduction to Final Fantasy: Through the Ages (FCA)

For this bonus set, 4 pieces of original art were specially picked from each installment, from the original Final Fantasy through Final Fantasy XVI (the 16th), as alternate-art reprint cards. The non-foil FCA cards appear in Play Boosters with a 33% chance, that is, 1 FCA card per 3 packs. If you open an FCA card in Limited, you can put it in your deck to boost its power, the same concept as the Special Guests (SPG) opened in previous sets.

## Conclusion

The legendary creatures that FCA sends out this time are all quality creatures of comparable strength, and the other cards also have real in-game value. With a distribution of about one per three booster packs, it will surely affect Limited to a greater degree than past SPGs.
