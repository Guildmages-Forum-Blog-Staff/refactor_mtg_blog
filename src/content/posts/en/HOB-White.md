## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick, gives a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors, gives a huge advantage
4.0: Worth first-picking, extremely synergistic with the color pair's theme, and can be the deciding card for splashing/switching colors or moving into a color pair
3.5: Top uncommon or common of the set, or another strong card, synergistic with the color pair's theme, and can be the key card for moving into a color pair
3.0: Important card for the color pair's theme, or another above-average card that you want in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a solid curve filler
2.0～1.0: Below average, generally leave it out, can be used as filler when necessary but not too many
0.5～0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play it, playing it only helps you lose

## Conclusion
White's cards roughly divide into two types: one focuses on going wide with Recruit, and the other achieves Enduring Story or brings Equipment to accumulate late-game advantage.

At first glance I thought Storied might have the higher payoff, because the proportion of legendary creatures is really high, so it might be achieved in around 4 turns; and if it is achieved that quickly, the card advantage may be better than other cards.

As for Recruit, because it adds no toughness, I imagine it may not perform as well in Limited as connive did before, especially if the card itself already gives up stats.

That's about it for white; let's test which side is stronger in practice, see you at the prerelease!
