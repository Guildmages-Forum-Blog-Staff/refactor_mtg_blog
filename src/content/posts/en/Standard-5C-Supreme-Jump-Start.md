## Before We Start

Murders at Karlov Manor brought us back to Ravnica for the fourth time, along with a batch of powerful two-color cards. Add the five-color domain mana base available in the Standard card pool, and [Niv-Mizzet, Supreme](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/a/da1ff886-d3c8-43e5-8bf0-ba4f0b259781.jpg) from March of the Machine: The Aftermath finally has a great environment to step onto the stage. Let's build it and see.

## Card Breakdown

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-5C-Supreme-Jump-Start/fc9fa3edcf08.webp)
[Niv-Mizzet, Supreme](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/a/da1ff886-d3c8-43e5-8bf0-ba4f0b259781.jpg)

> Flying, hexproof from monocolored
> Each instant and sorcery card in your graveyard that's exactly two colors has jump-start. (You may cast the card from your graveyard by paying its mana cost and discarding a card in addition. Then exile it.)

As the progenitor of the real-world ten guilds and the Izzet League on Ravnica, [Niv-Mizzet, Supreme](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/a/da1ff886-d3c8-43e5-8bf0-ba4f0b259781.jpg) gives jump-start to every guild-colored instant and sorcery in your graveyard. Not only does it lack the "can't be countered" debuff of [Lier, Disciple of the Drowned](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/8/e874b439-f964-41a5-9b70-e90509cfee27.jpg?1646258539), it is also a 5/5 flying, hexproof-from-monocolored finisher that, in the current metagame, dodges almost every form of removal other than bounce and sweepers. The deck is built as follows:

[Decklist](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/6205479#paper)

To pair with [Niv-Mizzet, Supreme](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/a/da1ff886-d3c8-43e5-8bf0-ba4f0b259781.jpg), the new set gave us a trio of Jeskai-colored cards: [Lightning Helix](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/1/4101e3fe-b0e7-4f0f-b9ac-9b61a4d628b3.jpg), [No More Lies](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/e/1e0c695d-62f9-4805-9e2f-7032e8464136.jpg) and [Ill-Timed Explosion](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/0/b/0b5cdb01-eaa4-4a0a-b42a-332bcf4d6fff.jpg), covering cheap removal, counterspells, life gain, card draw and sweepers all at once. [Lightning Helix](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/1/4101e3fe-b0e7-4f0f-b9ac-9b61a4d628b3.jpg) is an old card with a great effect: it improves your early survival, and late in the game you can jump-start it for a lethal blow. [No More Lies](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/e/1e0c695d-62f9-4805-9e2f-7032e8464136.jpg) is the new generation's [Mana Leak](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/7/c/7c257108-d0eb-45ee-99d9-d1cfec8c8517.jpg), and its extra exile clause can work wonders against opponents who use their graveyards. Besides working as a sweeper, just like its keyword says, [Ill-Timed Explosion](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/0/b/0b5cdb01-eaa4-4a0a-b42a-332bcf4d6fff.jpg) can also be cast without discarding as a [Divination](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/7/4/746a5919-637a-4fbd-8327-c37871e33f22.jpg), so it won't be a dead card in matchups where you don't need a sweeper.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-5C-Supreme-Jump-Start/fc983f423623.webp)

To go five-color in the current Standard, the domain route is absolutely the best choice. [Leyline Binding](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/c/3c3ac3dd-35db-447f-8674-37b4680a1ef7.jpg) and [Herd Migration](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/0/b0244a1f-e696-4223-9c14-22c2ca3cb738.jpg) shore up removal and color fixing plus life gain respectively, but [Invasion of Zendikar](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/f/8fed056f-a8f5-41ec-a7d2-a80a238872d1.jpg) isn't what this control deck needs. So I swapped it for [Case of the Shattered Pact](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/2/a/2a70f0ae-d49b-4cc8-9f76-895039c3dc39.jpg), which works well with [Niv-Mizzet, Supreme](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/a/da1ff886-d3c8-43e5-8bf0-ba4f0b259781.jpg): early on you don't have to worry about being stuck without green, and late in the game it lets Niv-Mizzet become a combo-vigilance demigod.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-5C-Supreme-Jump-Start/1c8212aab831.webp)

Is the setup above enough? As mentioned earlier, although [Niv-Mizzet, Supreme](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/a/da1ff886-d3c8-43e5-8bf0-ba4f0b259781.jpg) doesn't fear monocolored spot removal, and you can perhaps handle sweepers by countering them with [No More Lies](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/e/1e0c695d-62f9-4805-9e2f-7032e8464136.jpg), it is quite fragile against bounce. So in the five-mana finisher slot I mixed things up and added two dragon buddies, [Hidetsugu and Kairi](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/1/81039daf-0d54-4474-a833-fa287ec10cf9.jpg) and [Zurgo and Ojutai](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/a/eacbcd82-36e6-424c-bd4e-ec3a584836c5.jpg). [Hidetsugu and Kairi](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/1/81039daf-0d54-4474-a833-fa287ec10cf9.jpg) refills a card in hand through [Brainstorm](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/5/6/56ae04a0-2402-4f48-bcb5-3cd89faab07a.jpg) as soon as it enters, and if the opponent wants to deal with it using black removal, they have to gamble that the top of your library isn't a big play like [Herd Migration](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/0/b0244a1f-e696-4223-9c14-22c2ca3cb738.jpg). As for [Zurgo and Ojutai](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/a/eacbcd82-36e6-424c-bd4e-ec3a584836c5.jpg), see the [previous article](https://guildmagesforum.tw/Standard-5C-Sunbird-Ojutai/). I also added one [Invasion of Ravnica](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/1/invasion-of-ravnica-guildpact-paragon)
as extra removal and a resource engine.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-5C-Supreme-Jump-Start/d1c9ca11f7a1.webp)

Finally, a couple of support pieces for the above framework: [Push // Pull](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/5/85835473-b9b6-4f4a-bb93-fef93d5ec57b.jpg) and [Urgent Necropsy](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/2/d2ac346a-fc46-4023-aa60-4d55170697dc.jpg). [Push // Pull](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/5/85835473-b9b6-4f4a-bb93-fef93d5ec57b.jpg) is mainly used on the Pull side to bring back dragons that have hit the graveyard. It pairs with [Kairi](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/1/81039daf-0d54-4474-a833-fa287ec10cf9.jpg) and [Ojutai](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/a/eacbcd82-36e6-424c-bd4e-ec3a584836c5.jpg): the former can connect once, then be sacrificed to earn another [Brainstorm](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/5/6/56ae04a0-2402-4f48-bcb5-3cd89faab07a.jpg) and a top-of-library trick, and the latter can return a dragon to hand to dodge the sacrifice ability. Just note that [Push // Pull](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/5/85835473-b9b6-4f4a-bb93-fef93d5ec57b.jpg) is a three-color card, so it can't gain jump-start. [Urgent Necropsy](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/2/d2ac346a-fc46-4023-aa60-4d55170697dc.jpg) is a powerful instant-speed one-for-many removal spell, but it needs a high-cost collect evidence, which you can satisfy with [Herd Migration](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/0/b0244a1f-e696-4223-9c14-22c2ca3cb738.jpg) or [Ill-Timed Explosion](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/0/b/0b5cdb01-eaa4-4a0a-b42a-332bcf4d6fff.jpg).

## Conclusion

Murders at Karlov Manor set things up just right for [Niv-Mizzet, Supreme](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/a/da1ff886-d3c8-43e5-8bf0-ba4f0b259781.jpg), making a Niv-Mizzet control deck possible on top of a stable five-color domain base. Because of the large number of tapped lands, this control deck has a lower chance of smoothly casting a key spell on turn two than a normal three-color control deck, and it doesn't get much out of matchups against Boros Convoke or Red Aggro. But compared with the traditional domain decks, the large number of interactive spells also means fewer openings. For players tired of going fast, this is a variant well worth trying.
