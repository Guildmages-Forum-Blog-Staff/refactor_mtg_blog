## Rating Scale

5.0: Always take it; gives you a near-winning advantage
4.5: Always take it if you're in the right colors; gives you a huge advantage
4.0: First-pick quality; excellent synergy with the archetype's theme, and can be the deciding card for splashing, switching colors, or entering a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set, plus other strong cards; good synergy with the archetype's theme, and a key card for entering a color pair
3.0: Important cards for an archetype's theme, plus other above-average cards; you'll want them in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average; you generally prefer not to play them, and can use them as filler if necessary, but not too many
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable; basically never play it, it will only help you lose

## Conclusion

The biggest difference between a Vehicle environment and a regular set is the difference in sorcery/instant timing for creature removal cards <font color="#AAAAAA">(players who experienced the Kaladesh environment should feel this deeply)</font>. Although in general we all want removal to be instant speed, sorcery speed isn't a big problem as long as the cost and range are efficient enough. In a Vehicle environment, though, things are completely different: sorcery-speed removal may never even see a Vehicle at the time it becomes a creature. So when picking removal in this set, whether it can remove a Vehicle that has become a creature in time is an important dividing line. On that point black in this set does a pretty good job, as many sorcery-speed removal spells remember to include Vehicles in their range.
