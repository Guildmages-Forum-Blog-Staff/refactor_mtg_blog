Marvel's Spider-Man (SPM) has its prerelease events on September 19, 2025, and officially releases a week later on September 26, 2025. Want to know more about how each card rates in Limited, with recommendations? Then check out this series of SPM Limited reviews!

In addition, because of licensing issues, the physical paper cards and the digital cards online (MTGA, MO) for this set appear with different card names and different art, but with the same rules text (a few proper nouns on some cards still differ). Therefore, this Limited review shows both versions of the art side by side so readers can compare.

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Marvel's Spider-Man Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/SPM-Mechanism/)
- The physical card set is named Marvel Universe ([MAR](https://scryfall.com/sets/mar))
- The corresponding online set is named Through the Omenpaths Bonus Sheet ([OMB](https://scryfall.com/sets/omb))

> That said, the MAR set code says Marvel Universe, and if there are other Marvel IP crossovers in the future, it may well continue to be used in a way similar to Special Guests (SPG). Let's See.

## Rating Scale

5.0: Always take it; gives you a near-winning advantage
4.5: Always take it if you're in the right colors; gives you a huge advantage
4.0: First-pick quality; excellent synergy with the archetype's theme, and can be the deciding card for splashing, switching colors, or entering a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set, plus other strong cards; good synergy with the archetype's theme, and a key card for entering a color pair
3.0: Important cards for an archetype's theme, plus other above-average cards; you'll want them in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average; you generally prefer not to play them, and can use them as filler if necessary, but not too many
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable; basically never play it, it will only help you lose

## Conclusion
This time MAR has many efficient removal spells and very powerful minions. If you open them in Limited and they match your color pair, they will surely give you a big advantage.

The first time I saw Spider-Man was more than twenty years ago. I didn't even know Spider-Man now has so many parallel-universe branches and characters. Having this chance to collaborate, I also saw many homages and memorable scene recreations in MAR's card art, which brought back quite a few memories. I hope both Magic players and Spider-Man fans can enjoy this collaboration.
