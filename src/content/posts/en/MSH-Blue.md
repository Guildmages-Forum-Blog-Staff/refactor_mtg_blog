 ## Conclusion
 Blue in this set has a lot of card-draw effects, and many combat tricks even draw a card for themselves so you do not lose card advantage. It feels like a step up from the card-advantage capabilities of previous sets, rolling out a steady stream of resources in the late game. All I can say is, truly worthy of Marvel: more value, and then even more value.
