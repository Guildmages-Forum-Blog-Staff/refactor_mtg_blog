## If You Still Don't Know What Draft Is

Since this article is promotional, please allow us to briefly introduce "draft" once more.

Straight to the point: Magic: The Gathering draft is **insanely fun**. You get to crack packs, pick cards from them, snag the good stuff nobody else took (usually because they aren't playing that color this time and reluctantly pass it to you), build a deck, play matches, take home souvenirs provided by the store, and relax and trash-talk with a few friends. Or you can play seriously, so seriously that you can carry on to the Pro Tour.

This year, Wizards is promoting **Pick-Two Draft**, which simply means you take two cards per pick instead of one. 魔風集會所 may try it at the next event, and we'll share our experience and how the signals differ for you then, so stay tuned.

## Player Feedback

### [JerobaMTG](/authors/JerobaMTG/)'s Sharing:

#### What do you think was the most important pick today, and why? Please name the card.
[Anti-Venom, Horrifying Healer](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/1/anti-venom-horrifying-healer). My first pick of the day, a super bomb with a very steep casting cost. Opponents today just conceded when they saw it come down.

#### How did the key turns of today go?
I held off the opponent with the Spider tokens made by [Spiders-Man, Heroic Horde](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/117/spiders-man-heroic-horde), [Wall Crawl](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/121/wall-crawl) and [Spider-UK](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/17/spider-uk). [Silk, Web Weaver](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/145/silk-web-weaver) pumped the whole team for one big attack, and I successfully traded off the webs and held the line.

#### Do you like draft? Any draft memories worth looking back on?
I don't have any special memories of draft, but I do have one about sealed. At the 2014 Taipei GP, we played M15 sealed. As I recall, Day 1 was nine rounds, and six wins got you into Day 2. I started 5-0, then lost a bunch in a row and dragged it all the way to the last round. In that match I took an aggressive line early and pressed the opponent, then on turn six they slammed [Soul of Theros](https://scryfall.com/card/m15/34/soul-of-theros). I figured that if I let them activate the lifelink I'd be done, so I had to trade an extra card to deal with it. I thought I could breathe a little, and then I saw the opponent slam [Garruk, Apex Predator](https://scryfall.com/card/m15/210/garruk-apex-predator)...

#### How would you recommend draft?
Draft is a great format that adds fun to opening packs: you don't just open packs and be done, you get to enjoy the whole process. If you feel like you have bad luck, in draft you may get to pick up good stuff passed from the player upstream and soak up some good luck from your tablemates. If you already have some idea of Magic, draft lets you sharpen your sense of card power, curve building and format characteristics, and you might even discover little-known combos and find a new direction in how you play Magic.

### [Jru](/authors/JruMTG/)'s Sharing:

#### What do you think was the most important pick today, and why? Please name the card.

[Spider-Manifestation](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/148/spider-manifestation) & [Professional Wrestler](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/110/professional-wrestler): after testing this set's limited, these are the two most important common curve cards for green-based decks. Creatures in this set are relatively small, and the creatures that reach 4/4 or better in power and toughness at four mana or less start at rare, except [Professional Wrestler](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/110/professional-wrestler), the only common. On top of that, it can't be blocked by more than one creature, so it's a high-quality creature that is very hard to block. The Treasure that [Professional Wrestler](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/110/professional-wrestler) makes on ETB, plus the mana ability of [Spider-Manifestation](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/148/spider-manifestation), also gives the deck more room for powerful big creatures. My creature curve this time was high, but with these extra accelerants the whole thing ran very smoothly, and simply casting big creatures back to back was often enough to suppress the opponent all the way.

#### How did the key turns of today go?

With 2 [Spider-Manifestation](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/148/spider-manifestation) on the battlefield, I cast 3 [Professional Wrestler](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/110/professional-wrestler) in a single turn with just 4 lands, and with other creatures on board that trigger when I cast a spell with mana value 4 or greater, everything exploded in an instant.

#### Do you like draft? Any draft memories worth looking back on?

In an [EOE](https://scryfall.com/sets/eoe) draft, I successfully built the infinite lifegain combo of [Lightless Evangel](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/109/lightless-evangel) + [Susurian Voidborn](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/118/susurian-voidborn) + 2 [Perigee Beckoner](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/112/perigee-beckoner).

#### How would you recommend draft?

In a newbie's first few drafts, you'll surely have the feeling of "Who am I, where am I, what am I picking?" But as long as you consciously review and re-examine the logic behind your picks, you should make a big breakthrough within 5 drafts.

### [ZHAN](/authors/ZHAN/)'s Sharing:

#### What do you think was the most important pick today, and why? Please name the card.
I think it was the two copies of [Spider-Man India](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/151/spider-man-india). I originally thought one was enough, but since I had no other good creatures to stabilize the board, I still took the second. I drew it reliably throughout the matches, but because of matchup dynamics, it couldn't come down and show its advantage every game.

#### How did the key turns of today go?
One turn I had [Spider-Man India](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/151/spider-man-india) on the battlefield, and in hand two [Spectacular Tactics](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/15/spectacular-tactics) and too many lands. The opponent had a 4/3, a 3/3 and a 1/2. Since both of us were playing Web-slinging decks but only I had [Spider-Man India](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/151/spider-man-india), I figured the opponent would badly want to stop it, so I attacked with my creatures hoping the opponent would choose to block with everything. The opponent really did want to stop me from using [Spider-Man India](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/151/spider-man-india) to widen my advantage, so I was able to use the two [Spectacular Tactics](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/15/spectacular-tactics) to destroy the opponent's 4/3 and give myself +1/+1, establishing a board advantage.

#### Do you like draft? Any draft memories worth looking back on?
Rather than saying I like draft, I enjoy playing happily with a few friends, and Magic is a great medium for that. Whether you're a Commander player, a tournament player, or even a friend who hasn't played in ages but remembers the rules, everyone can enjoy the charm of Magic together.
Right after I finished learning the rules of Magic, I was pulled to a draft table. I no longer remember the details, but I'll never forget that when the opponent gained more than 20 life and I asked about it, they answered that the rules have no life total cap. I think that was the first time I felt the charm of a physical card game.

#### How would you recommend draft?
Draft can be enjoyed casually with a few friends, or you can compete seriously for the prizes the store provides. It's a good format for easily enjoying the charm of Magic.

You can crack packs, pick cards, build a deck, play... let me put it this way: I honestly can't find a downside to the draft format.
