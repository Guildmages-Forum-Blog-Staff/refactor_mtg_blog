Outlaws of Thunder Junction (OTJ) prerelease events take place on April 12, 2024, with the official release a week later on April 19, 2024. Want to know more about how each card rates in Limited, along with some potentially fun synergies? Then check out this series of OTJ Limited guides!

For the mechanics, see this article: [Outlaws of Thunder Junction Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/OTJ-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick
4.0: Worth a first pick
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you will really want it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around a 6th or 7th pick in the draft
2.0 to 1.0: Below average (you would rather not play it than play this card)
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable (basically never play it, doing so will only help you lose)

## White

![001](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/f/4f8dc511-e307-4412-bb79-375a6077312d.jpg?1712094731)

**Egavas:** 1.5

When you pick this in Limited, you will definitely want to cast it with a relatively high X, hoping for a big play with X of 2 or more. But even at X=2, the overall value of enters effects, which are mostly on commons and uncommons in Limited, is not high. For this card to perform well, you need creatures on the board with high-value or numerous enters effects, and the requirement to make it really pay off is too high.

An instant that combines protection, a combat trick, and a board wipe. The first mode, as a trick, can strip an evasive creature of its abilities, turning it into a dummy that you can block easily; the second gives a creature indestructible, occasionally saving your best creature; the third wipes the board at instant speed, so after sweeping on your opponent's turn you can immediately redeploy creatures on the backswing. Extremely flexible.

## Conclusion

White's cards perform adequately. Although there are a few bombs rated 4 or above such as [Final Showdown](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/11/final-showdown), there is no 5.0 card that can easily win the game after sticking around for a few turns. White creatures' overall stats and curve are not bad, though, and there are quite a few low-rarity cheap removal spells such as [Mystical Tether](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/19/mystical-tether) and [Eriette's Lullaby](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/10/eriettes-lullaby). Overall, white is well suited as a base to fill out your mana curve and the value of your small cards, then add bombs from other colors depending on the situation. Rather than a color decided by a first-pick bomb, white's role in this set is more of a color pair you commit to in the middle of the draft after you see or take enough quality small cards.
