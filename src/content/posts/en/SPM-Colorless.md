Marvel's Spider-Man (SPM) will hold its Prerelease events on September 19, 2025, and officially launch one week later on September 26, 2025. Want a better understanding of individual card ratings and advice for Limited? Then check out this series of SPM Limited overviews!

In addition, because of licensing issues, this set's physical paper cards and its online (MTGA, MTGO) digital cards will be presented with different card names and different card art, but with the same rules text (a few proper nouns on some cards still differ). Therefore, this Limited overview presents both versions of the card art together for readers to compare.

- For an introduction to the mechanics, see this article: [Marvel's Spider-Man Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/SPM-Mechanism/)
- The physical card set is named Marvel's Spider-Man ([SPM](https://scryfall.com/sets/spm))
- The online version of the set is named Through the Omenpaths ([OM1](https://scryfall.com/sets/om1))

## Rating Scale

5.0: An unconditional must-pick that brings an almost game-winning advantage
4.5: An unconditional must-pick if you are in the right colors, bringing a huge advantage
4.0: First-pick value, with excellent synergy with the color pair's theme, and a deciding card for splashing / switching colors or entering a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards, with good synergy with the color pair's theme, and a key card for entering a color pair
3.0: Important cards for the color pair's theme and other above-average cards that you would want in your final deck
2.5: Average level, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average; you generally prefer not to play them, and can use them as filler if necessary, but not too many
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable; basically do not play them, as they will only help you lose

## Conclusion
This set's colorless cards can be called merely passable, with few surprises, but there are still some cards that can shine with different themes, as well as some very powerful Equipment. As for lands, the biggest surprise is none other than the return of the three-color lands. If included in your deck, they can make the multicolor effect of a given color pair even better. Wishing everyone a good time with this set. Go and feel the charm of the crossover set!
