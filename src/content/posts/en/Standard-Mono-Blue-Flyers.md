## Before We Start

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mono-Blue-Flyers/c5bec1e20e0e.webp)

In [Aetherdrift](https://scryfall.com/sets/dft?as=grid&order=set), what surprised me most is that [Mu Yanling, Wind Rider](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/52/mu-yanling-wind-rider) got a role in this set, and it's also her first appearance as a legendary creature. Her ability is well suited as a card-draw engine for a fliers deck, but she looks pale next to the powerful card-refilling ability of the [Duskmourn: House of Horror](https://scryfall.com/sets/dsk?as=grid&order=set) card [Enduring Curiosity](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/51/enduring-curiosity). The two sets rotate out of Standard together, so it seems [Mu Yanling, Wind Rider](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/52/mu-yanling-wind-rider) will never have her day in the sun... is that really so? Let's try to rescue her!

## Card Analysis

First, let's compare the differences between these two four-mana card-draw engines:

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mono-Blue-Flyers/bbe5fde046d0.webp)
>Legendary Creature | Human / Wizard / Pilot
>When this creature enters, create a 3/2 colorless Vehicle artifact token with crew 1.
>Vehicles you control have flying.
>Whenever one or more creatures you control with flying deal combat damage to a player, draw a card.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mono-Blue-Flyers/09e9b8a66b64.webp)
>Enchantment Creature | Cat / Glimmer
>Flash
>Whenever a creature you control deals combat damage to a player, draw a card.
>When Enduring Curiosity dies, if it was a creature, return it to the battlefield under its owner's control. It's an enchantment. (It is no longer a creature after returning.)

Compared with [Enduring Curiosity](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/51/enduring-curiosity), the advantages of [Mu Yanling, Wind Rider](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/52/mu-yanling-wind-rider) are:
* Toughness 4
* Brings a Vehicle on entering
* Gives the Vehicle flying

Toughness 4 means [Mu Yanling](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/52/mu-yanling-wind-rider) won't die to a kicked [Torch the Tower](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/153/torch-the-tower), and even if she herself is removed, a 3/2 [Vehicle](https://scryfall.com/card/tdft/12/vehicle) remains on the battlefield to deal damage. If she survives, she gives the Vehicle flying, which gives it a better chance of drawing cards consistently than the [Curiosity](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/51/enduring-curiosity) that has no evasion.

The weaknesses are:
* Legendary creature
* No flash
* Lower card-draw ceiling

[Mu Yanling](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/52/mu-yanling-wind-rider)'s Vehicle on entering and [Curiosity](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/51/enduring-curiosity)'s return on death are both one-for-twos, so let's call that a tie. But compared with the [Curiosity](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/51/enduring-curiosity), which has flash, no legendary restriction, and draws a card for however many creatures connect, [Mu Yanling](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/52/mu-yanling-wind-rider) pales in comparison. So if you want to find her a stage, the key is probably the following three points:
* Drawing an extra card on your own turn has an additional effect
* Fliers as the main body
* Powerful Vehicles

Cards that meet these criteria are the ones that make a flier when you draw your second card, namely [Mischievous Mystic](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/47/mischievous-mystic) and [Thopter Fabricator](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/68/thopter-fabricator).

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mono-Blue-Flyers/baf58d1e9985.webp)

The decklist derived from this foundation is as follows:

[Decklist](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/6972556#paper)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mono-Blue-Flyers/a49cd6da5260.webp)

The first problem this deck has to solve is stabilizing a looting effect every turn, so besides the two thematic card-draw engines, I added [Spectral Sailor](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/164/spectral-sailor), [Malcolm, Alluring Scoundrel](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/63/malcolm-alluring-scoundrel) and [Kaito, Cunning Infiltrator](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/44/kaito-cunning-infiltrator) as reinforcements. Among them, [Malcolm](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/63/malcolm-alluring-scoundrel), which has flash and triggers on hit, is the main looting source, while [Kaito](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/44/kaito-cunning-infiltrator), relying on his planeswalker status, uses +1 each turn to loot and grant reliable evasion, and the [Ninja](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/12/ninja) token made by his -2, although it can't fly, can serve as a ground blocker or crew [Thopter Fabricator](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/68/thopter-fabricator). [Spectral Sailor](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/164/spectral-sailor) is a backup card-drawer on the one-mana curve, and if you don't mind the curve you can swap in the better-performing [Faerie Mastermind](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/58/faerie-mastermind).

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mono-Blue-Flyers/254b16e11879.webp)

As for spells, I chose [Bounce Off](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/39/bounce-off), an upgraded Unsummon, the flexible counterspell [Three Steps Ahead](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/75/three-steps-ahead), and [Into the Roil](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/509/into-the-roil), which can boost card draw, to stall and disrupt the opponent's tempo. [Three Steps Ahead](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/75/three-steps-ahead) can be used as [Cancel](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/505/cancel), and it can also meet the condition of drawing two and making a flier on your opponent's turn, or copy a creature or Vehicle to further expand your board advantage. [Into the Roil](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/509/into-the-roil) has kicker for a draw, and on your main turn it can be used to trigger the draw-two condition.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mono-Blue-Flyers/fe646b601872.webp)

For the remaining four slots, I put in [Possession Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/54/possession-engine), [Avishkar Raceway](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/249/avishkar-raceway) and [Amonkhet Raceway](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/248/amonkhet-raceway). [Possession Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/54/possession-engine)'s ability to hijack the opponent's creature isn't just a creature answer; its body is decent, it's usable as a plain beater when the opponent has no creatures, and the key point is that it also gets [Mu Yanling](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/52/mu-yanling-wind-rider)'s flying buff. Steal the opponent's flagship creature, crew it, fly up and hit the opponent to draw cards. Just thinking about it is thrilling. [Avishkar Raceway](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/249/avishkar-raceway) is included as a land that provides looting, and I added one [Amonkhet Raceway](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/248/amonkhet-raceway) as well to provide the haste that blue lacks most.

## Gameplay Demo

{%youtube u3OsBi-tR5Q %}

Red aggro is a rather tricky matchup for mono-blue, but in the first game, [Mu Yanling](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/52/mu-yanling-wind-rider) not only crewed [Thopter Fabricator](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/68/thopter-fabricator) to attack on entering, but the [Vehicle](https://scryfall.com/card/tdft/12/vehicle) she made also successfully deterred the opponent's offense. The next turn [Possession Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/54/possession-engine) came in and took the opponent's [Screaming Nemesis](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/157/screaming-nemesis), and then a flying Vehicle fought back strongly, winning the game surprisingly easily. The second game was [Kaito, Cunning Infiltrator](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/44/kaito-cunning-infiltrator)'s turn. The combination with [Thopter Fabricator](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/68/thopter-fabricator)'s self-made soldiers kept the opponent's creature removal from getting going, [Thopter Fabricator](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/68/thopter-fabricator) directly attacked down a +1 [Liliana of the Veil](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/97/liliana-of-the-veil), the mana I held back countered the opponent's [Sheoldred, the True Scriptures](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/125/sheoldred-the-true-scriptures), and the opponent's [Pyroclasm](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/149/pyroclasm) sweep was also futile; faced with the re-established air force, they could only concede. Game three, Black-Green graveyard, is a favorable matchup for the fliers deck: the opponent was not only slow to warm up, but their ground creatures could do nothing at all about fliers, and I won without breaking a sweat.


## Conclusion

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mono-Blue-Flyers/31bc351addff.webp)

On card stats, [Enduring Curiosity](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/51/enduring-curiosity) looks better overall than [Mu Yanling](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/52/mu-yanling-wind-rider), but in actual testing, [Mu Yanling](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/52/mu-yanling-wind-rider)'s built-in 3/2 flying [Vehicle](https://scryfall.com/card/tdft/12/vehicle) lets her handle situations more comprehensively than [Enduring Curiosity](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/51/enduring-curiosity), and if you pair her further with medium to large Vehicles, chipping away life is surprisingly efficient.
