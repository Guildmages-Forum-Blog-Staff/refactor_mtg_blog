## Before We Start

The traditional archetype of Mono-Green Stompy, which wins on sheer muscle, has been in the doldrums ever since Kaldheim rotated out of Standard. Even with powerful cards like [Polukranos Reborn](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/7/47f7d313-8333-41fe-8bfa-c96774dac228.jpg) and the green Praetor [Vorinclex](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/2/e24b5289-d3b5-4b4d-bb37-69bf2c3b48bc.jpg), it still falls short against the many powerful black cards led by the black Praetor. How much of a turnaround can the powerful green cards of The Lost Caverns of Ixalan, headed by Dinosaurs, bring?

## Woodland Champion: Card Analysis

First, let's talk about why mono-green declined after Kaldheim rotated out. For one, creature bodies lost their former advantage; before the release of Phyrexia: All Will Be One there wasn't even a single mono-green creature costing four or less that could compete with [Sheoldred, the Apocalypse](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/6/d67be074-cdd4-41d9-ac89-0a0456c4e4b2.jpg), and the [Evolved Spinoderm](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/d/dd4bcb42-f5cf-410d-8f20-1006b0f92abe.jpg) released in All Will Be One still got ground into the dirt by the same set's [Archfiend of the Dross](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/6/86b0edaf-8cfd-4508-9554-6f0fddc2dfc4.jpg); it's no exaggeration to say it was a notch weaker.

For another, quality removal rotated out. The rotation of the one-mana one-sided fight spell [Blizzard Brawl](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/4/3401a24b-f2b4-43c8-a3f0-477dcb3c1148.jpg) left green without an efficient removal option. Even though the instant-speed [Tail Swipe](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/9/5/95a39b26-8c83-40ea-b492-036251366d73.jpg?1673307819) and [Master's Rebuke](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/1/b11ddc71-6469-49bf-bb6d-46e0cd188434.jpg) are available, they can't do anything without a size advantage, not to mention the existence of [Glissa Sunslayer](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/2/b2bf633e-0470-4b87-99ed-5ad1683b0954.jpg) with built-in deathtouch, [Sheoldred, the Apocalypse](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/6/d67be074-cdd4-41d9-ac89-0a0456c4e4b2.jpg), and [Phyrexian Obliterator](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/6/7/67a9c38b-6b3a-4056-a87c-fc48446f854f.jpg), which makes the controller of the damage source sacrifice permanents.

So what exactly does the new set offer mono-green decks in terms of reinforcement? The new core cards are [Pugnacious Hammerskull](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/6/3/632e5635-a9bc-473a-a885-02e1fd258f7b.jpg) and [Hulking Raptor](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/5/45f763af-5a6a-404c-8e8c-4dbed71277bc.jpg), newly debuted in The Lost Caverns of Ixalan, and [Feral Encounter](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/e/de21251f-40bf-4f7e-9e85-9033207a788f.jpg) from Wilds of Eldraine.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mono-Green-Stompy/5c7ec98fdc77.webp)

I trust there's no need to say much about how formidable [Pugnacious Hammerskull](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/6/3/632e5635-a9bc-473a-a885-02e1fd258f7b.jpg) is. A three-mana 6/6 has plenty of intimidation just by standing there, its size overpowers every other creature costing four or less, and it's the best base for green removal; often you don't even need to care about the headbutt side effect, and just punch right through. [Hulking Raptor](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/5/45f763af-5a6a-404c-8e8c-4dbed71277bc.jpg) has a high 5/3 damage output, and the extra two green mana it provides each turn helps you cast higher-cost spells and transform permanents, so whether it's the fully realized [Nissa, Ascended Animist](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/d/1dd64b1d-bcef-476c-bf0b-3ac7df7cbed3.jpg), [Ulvenwald Behemoth](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/back/5/f/5fdf5fc4-69c8-4a59-9095-c2feefb64371.jpg) or [The Grand Evolution](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/back/e/2/e24b5289-d3b5-4b4d-bb37-69bf2c3b48bc.jpg), none of it is a pipe dream. Both of the above are strong reinforcements for mid-cost creatures, while [Feral Encounter](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/e/de21251f-40bf-4f7e-9e85-9033207a788f.jpg) is a superb boost to mono-green removal: it's both one-sided removal and can dig a creature out of the top five cards of your library to cast, so it won't become a dead card even if the opponent has no creatures. ## Conclusion

The biggest difference between the current version of mono-green and the past is the extensibility in the mid to late game. In the past it mostly relied on the size advantage of three- to five-drops to decide games, and larger creatures or powerful activated abilities were often seen but out of reach: either you got stuck on mana at the crucial moment, or you barely made it and got hit by removal and were out immediately, or you drew a hand full of fight spells and had no way out because you had no creatures. The current version has solutions to all these problems, and it feels very satisfying to play.
