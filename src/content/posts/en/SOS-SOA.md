## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional must-pick, brings an almost game-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional must-pick in the right color pair, brings a large advantage
4.0: First-pick quality, extremely synergistic with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card for splashing / switching colors or entering a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other cards strong enough, synergistic with the color pair's theme, can be a key card for entering a color pair
3.0: Important cards for the color pair's theme, and other above-average cards you'll want in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average, generally not played, can be used as filler when necessary but not in excess
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable, basically never play it; it will only help you lose

## Conclusion

The overall power level of this Mystical Archive spans a very wide range, from nearly unplayable cards to top-tier powerhouses that can directly swing a game. Rather than choosing purely by how high the score is, pay more attention to how well they fit your deck's strategy. A well-chosen Mystical Archive card may turn the game around at a critical moment, or even lock in the win outright.
