## Color Pair Themes
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MSH-Multicolors/16ab97db8634.webp)

### <span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span>-<span style="color:#C3B270">White</span> Teamwork

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MSH-Multicolors/41c36015bc1d.webp)

Teamwork doesn't just make dreams work, it can also double the power of your spells! The core of the White-Blue deck is the perfect combination of spells with the "Teamwork" ability and creatures that can tap. {% mtglink "Captain America, Living Legend" %} leads your creatures to victory with steadfast resolve. Add spells and creatures that make tokens, such as {% mtglink "Borough Backup" %} and {% mtglink " S.H.I.E.L.D. Deployment Drone" %}, to fully support your Blue-White deck!

### <span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span>-<span style="color:#808080">Black</span> Draw the Second Card

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MSH-Multicolors/dec284a04ec4.webp)

Use this Blue-Black deck to control the flow of time and the tempo of the game! {% mtglink "Kang, Temporal Tyrant" %} and his companions place great value on the effect of "drawing two cards in a single turn". It is recommended to add low-cost card-draw spells and creatures with Connive to support them. Don't forget that you can also trigger these abilities on your opponent's turn, so instants like {% mtglink "Thirst for Knowledge" %} and {% mtglink "Visions of Villainy" %} will be especially impactful.

### <span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span>-<span style="color:#808080">Black</span> Rise of the Villains

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MSH-Multicolors/0982c6716b27.webp)

Lead a horde of villains to carry out your evil plans! Use {% mtglink "Madame Hydra" %} and other powerful creatures to produce an endless stream of Villain tokens. The set also includes several black "Plan" enchantments. If you open multiple copies of {% mtglink "The Masters of Evil" %} and a few exciting Plan cards, you can build a perfectly sinister deck and use it to power up strong spells like {% mtglink "Doom Reigns Supreme" %}!

### <span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span>-<span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span> Power-up

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MSH-Multicolors/aa05717a6cad.webp)

Some deck styles call for shrewd plans, precise calculation, and masterful play... Red-Green is not that kind of deck. Use {% mtglink "Hulk, Gamma Goliath" %} and other creatures with the "Power-up" ability to smash your opponent's blockers directly! Want to double your creatures' crushing power? {% mtglink "Wonder Man, Hollywood Hero" %} keeps the spotlight forever on the best creature in your deck!

### <span style="color:#C3B270">White</span>-<span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span> Heroes Assemble

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MSH-Multicolors/d48d0d8102a5.webp)

Gather heroes from all across the Marvel universe and fill your battlefield! The Green-White deck shares several key white cards with the Blue-White Teamwork deck. Use white cards that create "Hero" tokens to repeatedly trigger the abilities of {% mtglink "Black Panther, Vanguard" %} and {% mtglink "Wakandan Royal Guard" %}. Once you've built up a huge roster of heroes, use {% mtglink "White Widow, Free Agent" %} and {% mtglink "Training Regimen" %} to boost their power and launch an all-out assault to win the game!

### <span style="color:#C3B270">White</span>-<span style="color:#808080">Black</span> Lone Wolf Mission

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MSH-Multicolors/279f592ea56d.webp)

Use a range of creatures, Equipment, and more cards to fully support your lone-wolf agent! When a creature attacks alone, {% mtglink "Black Widow, Double Agent" %} and {% mtglink "Voltaic Whip" %} both bring you rich rewards. All you need is to find the right super hero for the mission, such as {% mtglink "Luke Cage, Power Man" %}.

### <span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span>-<span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span> Artifact Army

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MSH-Multicolors/aeb8ae413647.webp)

Build the world's greatest inventions and arm your creatures! Put a high density of artifacts in your deck to power up {% mtglink "Iron Lad, Diverging Destiny" %} and {% mtglink "HYDRA Assault Robot" %}. This archetype places great value on the number of artifacts you've accumulated, even if their effects are negligible. Whether it's Treasures, Clues, or Robot Villain tokens, they all add to the power of {% mtglink "Iron Man, Master of Machines" %}!

### <span style="color:#808080">Black</span>-<span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span> Creatures in the Graveyard

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MSH-Multicolors/6a9447c3ae6b.webp)

Turn your creatures' deaths into fuel for your deck! Several cards check whether your graveyard contains "two or more creature cards". Early in the game, use "Mill" effects or creatures with "Cycling", such as {% mtglink "Roxxon Brutes" %}, to meet the condition quickly. Once you've put two creatures into your graveyard, cards like {% mtglink "Killmonger, Scourge of Wakanda" %} and {% mtglink "Arnim Zola, Bio-Fanatic" %} can win you the game all on their own!

### <span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span>-<span style="color:#C3B270">White</span> Noncreature Spells

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MSH-Multicolors/9e2101a058b0.webp)

Cast a series of noncreature spells to prove your deck's worth! Creatures with "Prowess" like {% mtglink "Agent of Atlas" %} and {% mtglink "Thor Odinson" %} can swell into game-winning behemoths with just a few spells. This archetype needs more low-mana-value cards than a typical deck, so when drafting, pay close attention to multiples of cards like {% mtglink "Super Speed" %} and {% mtglink "Vibranium Energy Daggers" %}.

### <span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span>-<span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span> +1/+1 Counters

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MSH-Multicolors/9c85c54d0307.webp)

Grow your creatures big and crush your opponent from on high! Use instants, sorceries, and other support effects to turn creatures with trample like {% mtglink "Beast, Erudite Aerialist" %} into titanic threats. Be sure to include protection in the deck to guard your counter-laden creatures. At the critical moment, {% mtglink "Kid Loki" %} can grant your creature "Hexproof", while {% mtglink "Claim the Kingdom" %} can give your strongest creature permanent protection through an "Indestructible" counter.
