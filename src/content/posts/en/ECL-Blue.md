## Rating Scale

> **Note**: ECL Limited places a high demand on tribal synergy within each color pair. If a creature card has the tribe that is the theme of the color pair (e.g. Merfolk, Goblin, Kithkin, Elemental, Elf, etc.), the rating already reflects its strength within that color pair.

5.0: Unconditional first pick, brings a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you're in the right colors, brings a huge advantage
4.0: First-pick value, extremely synergistic with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card for splashing, switching colors, or entering the color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other cards that are strong enough, synergistic with the color pair's theme, can be the key card for entering the color pair
3.0: Important cards for the color pair's theme and other above-average cards, you'll want them in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0~1.0: Below average, basically leaning toward not playing, can be used as filler when necessary, but not too many
0.5~0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play them, playing them only helps you lose
