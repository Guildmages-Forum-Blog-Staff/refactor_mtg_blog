## Vivid
![Vivid](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/ECL-Mechanism/088040d82f82.webp)

Vivid is an ability word. These vivid cards all check the number of colors among permanents you control. This number is at minimum 0 and at maximum 5. As for what happens after counting how many colors your permanents have, that depends on each individual card's effect.

>PS: Lands are colorless, unless the land is given a color by some other effect.





## Blight
![Blight](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/ECL-Mechanism/54c2cb6919b7.webp)

Blight is followed by a number N. Blight N means putting N -1/-1 counters on a creature you control.

Blight doesn't target. You only choose which creature to put the -1/-1 counters on when you actually perform the blight. If you blight 2, it's perfectly fine to put both -1/-1 counters on a single 1/1 creature. Blight doesn't care whether your creature can survive this calamity.

Because this set uses -1/-1 counters, no cards in this set can place +1/+1 counters. There is, however, an important rule to note about these two kinds of counters:
If a permanent has both +1/+1 and -1/-1 counters on it, the two kinds cancel out in pairs. For detailed rules, see [state-based actions (SBA)](https://guildmagesforum.tw/What-is-SBA/).





## Kindred
![Kindred](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/ECL-Mechanism/7a15959b4743.webp)

Kindred is a card type (on the same level as instant, sorcery, land, and so on, and different from a supertype such as legendary).

Every Kindred card has another card type, and that card's timing and rules depend on its non-Kindred type.

Kindred cards are never creature cards, but they have a creature subtype. Suppose an effect lets you search your library for a Faerie card and put it into your hand (without requiring a creature card). Then [Morcant's Eyes](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/185/morcants-eyes), a Kindred enchantment with the Faerie subtype, is also a valid search target.

> PS: Kindred was formerly called Tribal, but it was renamed for [political correctness](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/card-updates-coming-with-khans-of-tarkir-on-mtg-arena). All cards that use the word Tribal are automatically treated as having been changed to Kindred.





## Changeling
![Changeling](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/ECL-Mechanism/a8d67cbd6ca9.webp)

Changeling is a [characteristic-defining ability (CDA)](https://guildmagesforum.tw/Characteristic-Defining-Ability/). An object with changeling has every creature type, and this effect works in all zones. Suppose an effect lets you search your library for a Faerie creature card and put it into your hand. Then you can also find a changeling creature card and put it into your hand.





## Behold
![Behold](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/ECL-Mechanism/475fe1ebc741.webp)

Behold is a new mechanic that appeared not long ago in [Tarkir: Dragonstorm](https://guildmagesforum.tw/TDM-mechanism/#behold). It returns in this set with additional expansion.

Behold is a keyword action, always followed by a certain quality. In this set, that quality is always a creature type. Behold means revealing a card with that quality from your hand, or choosing a permanent you control with that quality (you only choose it, it isn't targeted). This time, some beholds go a step further: instead of only asking you to reveal or choose, they require you to exile it.





## Convoke
![Convoke](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/ECL-Mechanism/7b7ce2fa9a2f.webp)

When you cast a spell with convoke, as you pay its cost, you may tap any number of untapped creatures you control instead of paying mana. You can tap any creature to pay {1}, or, for a colored mana symbol, tap a creature of that color to pay that colored mana. Even if your creature is still affected by summoning sickness, you can tap it for convoke.





## Evoke
![Evoke](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/ECL-Mechanism/5f3e47779d1a.webp)

A card with evoke has an alternative cost. You may choose to pay the evoke cost to cast it instead of paying its normal cost. If you choose to cast it for its evoke cost, a triggered ability of **"Sacrifice this permanent"** triggers when the permanent enters.

The evoke creatures in this set also have other enter-the-battlefield abilities. These abilities and evoke's enter-and-sacrifice trigger at the same time, so you choose the order in which they go on the stack.





## Persist
![Persist](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/ECL-Mechanism/58f08a082b4f.webp)

Persist is a triggered ability. When a creature with persist dies, if it had no -1/-1 counters on it, return it to the battlefield under its owner's control with a -1/-1 counter on it.
