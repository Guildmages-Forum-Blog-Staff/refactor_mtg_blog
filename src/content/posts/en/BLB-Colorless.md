Bloomburrow (BLB) holds its prerelease events on July 26, 2024, and launches officially a week later on August 2, 2024. Want to know more about how each card rates in Limited, with recommendations? Then check out this series of BLB Limited introductions!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Bloomburrow Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/BLB-mechanism/)
- For the Limited archetypes, see this article: [Bloomburrow Limited Preliminary Look](https://guildmagesforum.tw/BLB-Limited-Preliminary/)

> The articles in this Limited series go by ratings. Some average cards, or cards with fairly simple abilities, won't get a dedicated comment.

## Rating Scale

5.0: Always take it
4.5: Always take it if you are in the right colors
4.0: Worth a first pick
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you would really like it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick of a draft
2.0–1.0: Below average (you would rather not play it than play it)
0.5–0.0: Unplayable (basically don't play it; it will only help you lose)

Comments:

- [Heirloom Epic](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/246/heirloom-epic): Once a Limited game turns into a grind, a perpetual card-draw engine like this easily builds a huge resource gap. This set is also very good at making tokens, and some creatures you would never attack with anyway (they would just be giving away a free kill) are perfect fodder for paying the draw cost.
- [Patchwork Banner](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/247/patchwork-banner): With the right creature types, it is a strong [Glorious Anthem](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/21/glorious-anthem). With scattered types it is a bit awkward, so it has a better chance of paying off the tribal bonus in a draft. At worst it is a [Manalith](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/239/manalith), usable when your deck has truly extreme color-fixing needs.
- [Short Bow](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/248/short-bow): For a 2-mana cast with a 1-mana equip, the abilities it grants are very good. A cheap-to-equip piece of gear is also an excellent valiant trigger source.

Comments:

- [Relentless Rats](https://scryfall.com/card/spg/57/relentless-rats): Unlike the Rat Colony above, which cares about the creature type, this one's pump is tied to creatures with the same name. I have never seen anyone assemble two copies of the same SPG card in a Limited game.
- [Kindred Charge](https://scryfall.com/card/spg/58/kindred-charge): With a correctly built deck its ceiling is very high, nearly one-card victory, but the odds of assembling a tribal deck that pure in Limited (especially Sealed) are low. In actual play it probably just puts a wave of pressure on your opponent under fairly specific conditions.
- [Sylvan Tutor](https://scryfall.com/card/spg/59/sylvan-tutor): At its core it is a card-disadvantage tutor. The bomb or key creature it fetches is often worth more than a card, but it comes with limits: you get it a turn late, and it does nothing when you need a noncreature card. If your deck has no bomb-level or key creature, don't play it. Conversely, if your deck has a powerful creature bomb that can decide the game on its own, it effectively raises your bomb count by one.
