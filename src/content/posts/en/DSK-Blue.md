## Rating Scale

5.0: Always take it
4.5: Always take it if you're in the right colors
4.0: First-pick quality
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you'll really want it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick of a draft
2.0 to 1.0: Below average (you'd rather not play it than play it)

## Conclusion

Blue in this set has a huge number of card-draw engine cards, though most of them work through attacking, and about half of the creatures have evasion. My guess is that blue's game plan this time won't be a purely defensive control style, but a tempo style that earns value while it fights.
