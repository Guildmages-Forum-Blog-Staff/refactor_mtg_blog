Reality Fracture (FRA) will hold its prerelease events on September 25, 2026, and launch officially a week later on October 2, 2026. Want to know more about individual card ratings and advice for Limited? Then check out this series of FRA Limited reviews!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Reality Fracture Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/FRA-Mechanism/)

In addition, this FRA release includes 10 Special Guest ([Special Guest, SPG](https://scryfall.com/search?order=set&q=e%3Aspg+cn%E2%89%A5159+cn%E2%89%A4168&unique=prints)) cards. You can open 1 non-foil copy in every 55 regular Play Boosters, and they can be used in FRA Limited as well as in whichever formats the cards were originally legal in.

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional must-pick, brings an almost game-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional must-pick if you're in the right colors, brings a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick, fits the color pair's theme extremely well, and can be the deciding card for splashing / switching colors or moving into a color pair
3.5: The top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards that fit the color pair's theme well, and can be a key card for moving into a color pair
3.0: Important cards for a color pair's theme, plus other above-average cards that you'd want in your final deck
2.5: Average level, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average, generally leaning toward not playing; can be used as filler when necessary, but not too many. A 2.0 can also be the score for a strong sideboard card.
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play it, since playing it only helps you lose
