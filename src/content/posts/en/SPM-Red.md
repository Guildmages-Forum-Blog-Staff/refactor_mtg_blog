Marvel's Spider-Man (SPM) will hold its prerelease events on September 19, 2025, and officially launch a week later on September 26, 2025. Want to learn more about how each card rates in Limited, with recommendations? Then check out this series of SPM Limited reviews!

Also, because of licensing issues, the physical cards in paper and the digital cards online (MTGA, MO) will be presented with different card names and different card art, but the same rules text (with a small number of proper nouns on a few cards still differing). Therefore, this Limited review shows both versions of the card art together so readers can compare.

- For an introduction to the mechanics, see this article: [Marvel's Spider-Man Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/SPM-Mechanism/)
- The paper set is named Marvel's Spider-Man (Marvel's Spider-Man, [SPM](https://scryfall.com/sets/spm))
- The online set is named Through the Omenpaths (tentative translation; the English set name is Through the Omenpaths, [OM1](https://scryfall.com/sets/om1))

## Rating Scale

5.0: An unconditional first pick that brings you close to winning on its own.
4.5: An unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors; brings a huge advantage.
4.0: Worth first-picking. Great synergy with the color pair's theme, and can be the deciding card for splashing / switching colors or moving into the color pair.
3.5: The set's top uncommons and commons, plus other cards of sufficient power. Good synergy with the color pair's theme, and a key card for moving into the color pair.
3.0: An important card for the color pair's theme, or another above-average card that you want in your final deck.
2.5: Average. Usually a decent curve filler.
2.0 to 1.0: Below average. You generally lean toward leaving it out, and can use it as filler when necessary, but not too many.
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable. Basically don't play it; putting it in only helps you lose.

## Conclusion

Although the Mayhem ability of the new set looks about 87% similar to the old Madness ability, and the shadows of [Shadows over Innistrad](https://scryfall.com/sets/soi?as=grid&order=set) cards are clearly visible in the design, having to follow casting timing is a major weakening, and the restrictions on discard engines in Limited are also many, so flexibility is directly cut in half, which is the unfortunate part of the new ability. But since the designs are similar, if the sequencing lines up it can be extremely fierce (for example, a turn-three 5/4 haste), so it's well suited to players with a strong right hand!
