## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick, brings a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick in the right archetype, brings a huge advantage
4.0: Has first-pick value, has excellent synergy with the archetype's theme, and can be the deciding card for splashing/switching colors or entering the archetype
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards, with good synergy with the archetype's theme, and can be a key card for entering the archetype
3.0: Important cards for the archetype's theme, as well as other above-average cards, that you want in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0-1.0: Below average, generally not played, can be used as filler if necessary but not too many. A 2.0 can also be the score for a powerful sideboard card.
0.5-0.0: Unplayable, basically do not play it; playing it only helps you lose

## Conclusion

![The burn spells](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Red/4804bdf43322.webp)

Red's CU cards this time include 5 burn spells, with different costs and different effects and targets, so each got a 3.0. But I have to say, FRA red has plenty of removal this time, and that is before counting the color-hate cards {% mtglink "Essence Burn" %} and the enchantment version of {% mtglink "Ajani's Anguish" %}! However, the drawback of relying on burn as your main removal is that its reach is often not enough to deal with the key threats. During games, players should think more about whether the removal in hand needs to be used right now or saved for a more worthwhile target.
