## If You Still Don't Know What Draft Is

Since 魔風集會所 is a site for both the refined and the casual, XD, please allow us to first briefly introduce what "draft" is ~~although we believe our longtime readers all know~~ .

Getting straight to the point in one sentence: Magic: The Gathering draft is **insanely fun**. You get to crack packs, pick cards from them, snag the good stuff nobody else took (usually because they aren't playing that color this time and reluctantly pass it to you), build a deck, play matches, take home souvenirs provided by the store, and relax and trash-talk with a few friends. Or you can play seriously, so seriously that you can carry on to the Pro Tour...

No doubt about it, it's that great a game! After the draft, the cards you picked are of course yours, and you can take them home and add them to your constructed decks, which makes for a perfect experience. In particular, Magic's draft design is full of depth and fun, and I believe veteran players still can't forget it or are still deeply hooked.

Please allow me to be lazy for a moment and cite an earlier article, [How Newbies Can Improve in Limited: The Draft Edition](https://hackmd.io/klF-Yzt-SrKFfOj-KFNcKQ). Newbies and advanced players who want a deeper understanding afterward are welcome to head over after reading this record.

## Player Feedback

### [Chennel](/authors/Chennel/)'s Sharing:

#### What do you think was the most important pick today, and why? Please name the card.
Definitely seeing the super bomb [Summon: Bahamut](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/1/summon:-bahamut) right away in my first pack and taking it without a second thought. After that I started worrying about how to build a deck that could summon Bahamut. After the first round of picks, I found that the Landfall-related pieces had all been taken, so a Blue-Green ramp theme probably wouldn't work. After taking [Syr Konrad, the Grim](https://scryfall.com/card/fca/10/syr-konrad-the-grim) with the fourth pick of the first pack, I started thinking about going Black-Green, but judging by the cards passed from the first pack, black removal was very rare, so starting in pack two I chose to add blue to fill out the deck's removal, and ended up with a Sultai-colored control deck.

#### How did the key turns of today go?
In game two against Peter, he mulliganed twice to start, and in the meantime I cast [Diamond Weapon](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/183/diamond-weapon) and [Syr Konrad, the Grim](https://scryfall.com/card/fca/10/syr-konrad-the-grim) in a row, and he drew two [Sephiroth's Intervention](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/116/sephiroths-intervention) in a row to clear them both. After that my topdecks were terrible and I got pushed to death by the opponent. This game is so fun, XD.

#### Do you like draft? Any draft memories worth looking back on?
In one [MH3](https://scryfall.com/sets/mh3) match I had a hand disaster and mulliganed three times, starting with only four cards in hand, definitely a huge disadvantage. But luckily on turn four I used [Phyrexian Tower](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/303/phyrexian-tower) to sacrifice a creature for two mana of acceleration and summoned [Shilgengar, Sire of Famine](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/109/shilgengar-sire-of-famine), then hit the opponent with it for three turns in a row and beat them to death, XD.

#### How would you recommend draft?
Draft is the most convenient and affordable format. Just come to the card store empty-handed, buy sleeves and three booster packs, and you can start playing right away. Every match feels very different, and after playing you can take the deck you built home, which is great value.
The process of picking cards in draft is also very fun: while building the color pair you want to play, you also take away strong cards others might use. It's very suitable for learning how to build a good deck and getting familiar with how each color plays.

### Peter's Sharing:

#### What do you think was the most important pick today, and why? Please name the card.
Perhaps this will surprise everyone, but the most important pick was actually [Brainstorm](https://scryfall.com/card/fca/28/brainstorm) at the end of pack two. The general impression of [Brainstorm](https://scryfall.com/card/fca/28/brainstorm) is that it's a card that struggles to be effective in limited: since there's no land-fetching, intuitively [Brainstorm](https://scryfall.com/card/fca/28/brainstorm) just rearranges the order of your upcoming draws, letting you find and use the next three cards first, but if your hand or the top of your library isn't good, it can't change anything. However, in my deck that day there were actually quite a few ways to remove the two cards put back (the unwanted ones), including two creatures that surveil, two creatures that mill on entering, two cards that cycle to find basic lands, and a World Map, which made my [Brainstorm](https://scryfall.com/card/fca/28/brainstorm) skyrocket in strength, and it saved me more than once in matches.

#### How did the key turns of today go?
The most critical turns today were nothing other than the first game of Round 2: against Red-White Equipment. For nearly ten turns with my life down to single digits, I blocked with small creatures while continuously building board and hand advantage, and finally found an answer and successfully turned the game around. After the match I chatted with my opponent: that's just how this game is, as long as you haven't truly lost, you can't give up.

#### Do you like draft? Any draft memories worth looking back on?
I remember one time going to Taoyuan to play a sealed-draft MITQ, and in one round I was paired against [cephille](/authors/cephille/). In the last two turns of game two, he used Treasures to make two mana of a third color, and in a deck mostly Red-Blue he cast the two-black-mana [Gisa, the Hellraiser](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/89/gisa-the-hellraiser) and reaped the win directly. To this day I still have a vivid memory of that match.

#### How would you recommend draft?
To me, draft is the best medium for building the basic logic of this game, because besides the most basic combat it cares about very limited resource management and deckbuilding. In this format you must know your 40 cards inside out, and every decision has a big effect on winning or losing.

### 關東煮師爺（子懷）'s Sharing:

#### What do you think was the most important pick today, and why? Please name the card.
[Choco, Seeker of Paradise](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/215/choco-seeker-of-paradise). In this draft I deliberately chose to play a Chocobo deck, and the multicolor Chocobo can gain lots of resources when there are many Chocobos on the board. Putting lands onto the battlefield from the top of the library can pump up the Chocobos to attack and let you turn a losing game around.

#### How did the key turns of today go?
The Chocobo deck needs to accumulate Chocobos on the board, and in the first few turns it takes a lot of patience to wait for the right moment. In the late turns, after accumulating a certain amount of resources, I happened to draw [Bartz and Boko](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/175/bartz-and-boko). By coming down with the cost reduction and damaging the opponent's big creature, and finally attacking with the Chocobos combined with [Choco, Seeker of Paradise](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/215/choco-seeker-of-paradise)'s putting lands from the top of the library onto the battlefield to pump up the Chocobos' attacks, I got the final victory!

#### Please share your feelings about taking part in the draft
Invited to play draft this time, I got to share the fun of picking cards and building decks with other newbies and veteran players. From the very first pick you can start thinking about the deck direction, and once the deck is done you can play matches to show off your build, which is very fun!
During matches you can observe the opponent's deck logic and make use of your own card skills to try to find the way to victory, and the moment of winning really gives a sense of accomplishment! Draft is a format very suitable for going from newbie to regular player, opening packs and exchanging ideas with friends, enjoying the charm of constructed-style building. My personal suggestion when playing draft is to be sure to first understand how the deck's theme plays and its details, so that during picking and building you can better grasp the direction of play and experience the draft happily.
