## Before We Start

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Sunmare/3d42a157e12b.webp)

After [Outlaws of Thunder Junction](https://scryfall.com/sets/otj?as=grid&order=set) introduced the [Mount](https://scryfall.com/search?as=grid&order=name&q=type%3Amount+%28game%3Apaper%29) theme, [Aetherdrift](https://scryfall.com/sets/dft?as=grid&order=set), a set built around racing, added one-mana Mounts and a quality big white Mount, [Guardian Sunmare](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/15/guardian-sunmare). The lists using one-mana Mounts were already shown in this series' [Standard Preliminary](https://guildmagesforum.tw/DFT-Standard-Preliminary/), and among big Mounts, [Calamity, Galloping Inferno](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/116/calamity-galloping-inferno) from [Outlaws of Thunder Junction](https://scryfall.com/sets/otj?as=grid&order=set) has huge one-hit-kill potential. So how can [Guardian Sunmare](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/15/guardian-sunmare) be played?

## Card Analysis

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Sunmare/40fab2d6cd6d.webp)
>Creature | Horse / Mount
>Ward {2}
>Whenever this creature attacks while saddled, search your library for a nonland permanent card with mana value 3 or less,
put it onto the battlefield, then shuffle.
>Saddle 4

[Guardian Sunmare](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/15/guardian-sunmare) is not only a solid 5/5 with built-in ward; when saddled and attacking, it can search for a nonland permanent with mana value 3 or less and put it straight onto the battlefield, which means every attack further expands your board advantage and finds the best answer for the situation. However, the five-mana cost and Saddle 4 requirement are the problems we need to solve. The decklist is as follows:

[Decklist](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/6927091#paper)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Sunmare/303d6e24d4b7.webp)

First, we need to get [Guardian Sunmare](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/15/guardian-sunmare) out quickly, so mana acceleration is crucial. [Llanowar Elves](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/227/llanowar-elves), which returned to Standard with [Foundations](https://scryfall.com/sets/fdn?as=grid&order=set), [Intrepid Stablemaster](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/169/intrepid-stablemaster), the mana dork from [Outlaws of Thunder Junction](https://scryfall.com/sets/otj?as=grid&order=set) that specifically supports Mounts and Vehicles, and this set's green Vehicle bigwig [Lumbering Worldwagon](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/168/lumbering-worldwagon), which can fetch a basic land onto the battlefield when it enters or attacks, set the tempo at one-three-five mana or two-five mana, aiming to land [Guardian Sunmare](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/15/guardian-sunmare) on turn three.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Sunmare/36fad291de47.webp)

For crew, the main job falls to [Wylie Duke, Atiin Hero](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/239/wylie-duke-atiin-hero), who draws cards when tapped; the rest depends on creatures and [Pilot](https://scryfall.com/card/tdft/1/pilot) tokens working together, including the Mount and Vehicle themed legend [Miriam, Herd Whisperer](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/221/miriam-herd-whisperer), [Defend the Rider](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/157/defend-the-rider), which can double as a protection spell, and, when needed, the manland [Restless Prairie](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/281/restless-prairie) to fill the gap.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Sunmare/1475522805bd.webp)

Removal consists of the main piece [Get Lost](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/14/get-lost), [Unidentified Hovership](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/37/unidentified-hovership), which can be fetched by [Guardian Sunmare](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/15/guardian-sunmare) and exiles a creature with toughness 5 or less, and [Detention Chariot](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/12/detention-chariot), an answer to artifacts or creatures that has a chance to land on turn three, for ten pieces in total.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Sunmare/9fa9ccf32697.webp)

For lands, besides the dual lands [Restless Prairie](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/281/restless-prairie), [Brushland](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/259/brushland), [Razorverge Thicket](https://scryfall.com/card/one/257/razorverge-thicket) and [Hushwood Verge](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/261/hushwood-verge), I also threw in one [Drannith Ruins](https://scryfall.com/card/mat/50/drannith-ruins) to bulk up the [Pilot](https://scryfall.com/card/tdft/1/pilot) tokens and [Llanowar Elves](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/227/llanowar-elves), letting them crew [Guardian Sunmare](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/15/guardian-sunmare) and [Lumbering Worldwagon](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/168/lumbering-worldwagon) smoothly.

## Gameplay Demo

{%youtube P7XfPXpD0_4 %}

Unlike traditional Vehicle and Mount decks, in this one the main early and mid-game beaters are the crew members themselves. In game one against Dimir, the two green-white legends dealt plenty of damage, [Defend the Rider](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/157/defend-the-rider) successfully fended off several attempts to jump ship, and once [Guardian Sunmare](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/15/guardian-sunmare) came down and got going the game became one-sided; two [Unidentified Hovership](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/37/unidentified-hovership) exiled the Dimir Gearhulk [Oildeep Gearhulk](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/215/oildeep-gearhulk) to decide the match. In the second game, the acceleration went smoothly and [Guardian Sunmare](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/15/guardian-sunmare) landed on turn three; although the opponent interfered, it did not matter overall, and the indestructible black-white god [Ketramose, the New Dawn](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/209/ketramose-the-new-dawn) meant little in front of [Unidentified Hovership](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/37/unidentified-hovership), and a final-draw [Detention Chariot](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/12/detention-chariot) cleared the way and ended the game.

## Conclusion

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Sunmare/1261c2a05229.webp)

Among big Mounts, compared with the all-or-nothing [Calamity, Galloping Inferno](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/116/calamity-galloping-inferno), [Guardian Sunmare](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/15/guardian-sunmare), with its library search, has considerable room for development. Like [Zur the Enchanter](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/206/zur-the-enchanter), it can find the best answer for the situation when it attacks, and as long as you can meet the Saddle requirement, whether you splash a third color to widen the search range or pull out the matching hate cards in sideboard games, it works great.
