## 1. Introduction

Hi everyone, I'm ZengZengZeng, a Taipei player who mainly hangs out at 遊戲平方 and ALG. 
This time I made the MIT#10 Modern Top 8 with Izzet Prowess. 
Past results: MIT#4 Pioneer Top 8 with Izzet Phoenix. 
Both of my MIT Top 8s were with Izzet decks. 



## 2. The Deck 

The most-watched tournament before the MIT was the EOE PT, where Blue Cannon dominated. But when I looked at the standings, Justin's Izzet Prowess, the Swiss round first-place deck, caught my eye first. I was already quite interested in tempo decks, and 台灣黑熊, the idol of the 遊戲平方 group chat (fan page: [標準玩家碎碎念](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100092580668030)), also chose this deck and immediately bought Justin's Sideboard Guide, so I settled on the same deck, thinking we could exchange ideas and practice together. In the end, because of other commitments, I didn't keep up with buying the Guide, which I feel is quite a pity.

So I started looking for some free guides to read, and saw EthanMTG's 5-0 video with a free Guide included. Once I saw it, I knew this was what I wanted! Three double strike spells in the main deck! Compared with Justin's 2 main and 1 sideboard, it's more aggressive and fully fits my needs.

> Video link: [MODERN IZZET PROWESS | 5-0 League! 🏆 | MTGO Gameplay & Sideboard Guide | MTG Modern](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p-GkLXv9eJE)

While practicing, I found that many decks were nearly unbeatable (Resonance, Titan, Zoo). At first, to counter these decks I mixed in white and turned it into Jeskai, adding three [Wear//Tear](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/456/wear-tear) to deal with things red-blue has trouble answering such as [Urza's Saga](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/259/urzas-saga), [Amulet of Vigor](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/121/amulet-of-vigor), and [Leyline Binding](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/24/leyline-binding). The win rate in sideboarded games did go up, but only a little. The day before the tournament, after practicing with 盈孝 (fan page: [豬鼻子的mtg世界](https://www.facebook.com/Pignosemtgworld)) and T1BloodMoon (fan page: [炸地．血月．組合技](https://www.facebook.com/T1BloodMoon)), I found that if the sideboard plan slows down the deck's game plan just to answer cards, it's really not worth it, so at the last minute I switched back to the original Izzet Prowess.

The difference from EthanMTG's list is that instead of three [Violent Urge](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/164/violent-urge), I changed one to the sorcery [Assault Strobe](https://scryfall.com/card/som/82/assault-strobe). The reason is that in matchups where you need to win quickly (Tron, Blue Cannon, Titan), you'll side out [Cori-Steel Cutter](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/103/cori-steel-cutter) and [Expressive Iteration](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/288/expressive-iteration) entirely, which makes it very likely you'll be one card type short of delirium for Violent Urge's double strike, so I chose this card with a guaranteed double strike. Then I swapped one sideboard [Into the Flood Maw](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/52/into-the-flood-maw) for [Spell Pierce](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/80/spell-pierce), because in practice [Into the Flood Maw](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/52/into-the-flood-maw) really had no targets. And that completed this Izzet Prowess list.

## 3. Main Rounds with Excellent Dodging

### R1. Sam Combo (lost the die roll, 2-1)

Opponent: 尚峰 (fan page: [紅的喜氣](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100083415367398)). 紅的喜氣 didn't play red, which surprised me a bit XD

- G1: My early offense was blocked by [Young Wolf](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/227/young-wolf), I didn't draw a single burn spell, and got taken out by 2 [Marionette Apprentice](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/100/marionette-apprentice) plus a sacrifice outlet.
- G2: I had [Cori-Steel Cutter](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/103/cori-steel-cutter) and drew 2 [Lava Dart](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/134/lava-dart), clearing the board while pushing damage through.
- G3: I had [DRC](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/121/dragons-rage-channeler) and [Unholy Heat](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/145/unholy-heat), clearing the board while pushing damage through.
- A favorable matchup, but if I draw too few removal spells, I can still easily get blocked and then countered-attacked.


### R2. Colorless Tron (lost the die roll, 2-0)

- G1: The opponent didn't assemble Tron, but one [Dismember](https://scryfall.com/card/mm2/79/dismember) and one [Kozilek's Command](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/11/kozileks-command) blocked my early offense, and at the crucial moment I won with double strike.
- G2: The opponent played Ugin with 7 mana on turn 3. I played [DRC](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/121/dragons-rage-channeler) on turn 1 and passed turn 2 to counter everything with [Consign to Memory](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/54/consign-to-memory). The next turn [Kozilek's Command](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/11/kozileks-command) was hit by [Spell Pierce](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/80/spell-pierce), and since they drew nothing else, I slowly beat them down.
- A favorable matchup. Three double strike spells are strong! For the sideboard plan, just play steadily against what shows up.



### R3. Mono-Green Lizard Blade (lost the die roll, 2-1)

Opponent: T1BloodMoon (fan page: [炸地．血月．組合技](https://www.facebook.com/T1BloodMoon))

- G1: I got greedy with a turn-1 [DRC](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/121/dragons-rage-channeler) + [Mishra's Bauble](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/274/mishras-bauble) one-land opening hand, and then drew 6 straight non-lands and was punished and died.
- G2: I was on the play. On turn 1 I played [Preordain](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/161/preordain), and on turn 2, with 2 lands, [Cutter](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/103/cori-steel-cutter) + [Mishra's Bauble](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/274/mishras-bauble) made a Token to attack for 2, with a land, an artifact, and a sorcery in the graveyard. On turn 2 the opponent played two [Eldrazi Temple](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/992/eldrazi-temple) into [Thought-Knot Seer](https://scryfall.com/card/ogw/9/thought-knot-seer), and looked at my hand of 1 land, 1 [Violent Urge](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/164/violent-urge), 2 [Dart](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/134/lava-dart), and 1 [DRC](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/121/dragons-rage-channeler). They made the wrong choice and took [DRC](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/121/dragons-rage-channeler). The next turn I played a land and 2 [Dart](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/134/lava-dart) to hit Thought-Knot Seer, gave Violent Urge to the Token I made, sacrificed 2 lands and shot [Thought-Knot Seer](https://scryfall.com/card/ogw/9/thought-knot-seer) dead with 2 more [Dart](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/134/lava-dart), and a 6/6 with prowess 5 plus a 6/5 double striker with trample carrying the [Cutter](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/103/cori-steel-cutter) at prowess 3 simply hit for 18 to end it. This was lethal from the cards in hand alone, and I should have calculated it.
- G3: I had [DRC](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/121/dragons-rage-channeler) and [Unholy](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/145/unholy-heat), clearing the board while pushing damage through.
- A favorable matchup. For the sideboard plan, just play steadily against what shows up.



### R4. Izzet Prowess (lost the die roll, 2-1)

Opponent: 竑宇

- G1: At the crucial moment, I didn't use [Dart](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/134/lava-dart) + flashback on my own turn, losing a land, to kill [Slickshot Show-Off](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/146/slickshot-show-off) directly. I only killed it the next turn, after it was saved by a self-drawn [+2/+2](https://scryfall.com/card/mm2/149/mutagenic-growth), and after that it was hopeless.
- G2: On the play, a turn-1 [DRC](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/121/dragons-rage-channeler) with three [Mishra's Bauble](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/274/mishras-bauble) hit delirium immediately. The opponent misplayed [Unholy](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/145/unholy-heat) without delirium, so I had to blow up extra lands with flashback [Dart](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/134/lava-dart) to recover, but couldn't catch up.
- G3: On the draw, I drew [Spell Pierce](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/80/spell-pierce) and countered [Cutter](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/103/cori-steel-cutter), and [Spell Snare](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/132/spell-snare) countered [Iteration](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/288/expressive-iteration), after which the opponent had no resources and I slowly killed them.
- A mirror match, winner take all: the side that draws worse loses.



### R5. Sam Combo (won the die roll, 2-1, first time on the play!)

Opponent: 志懋

- G1: My early offense was blocked by [Young Wolf](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/227/young-wolf), they played [Birthing Ritual](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/146/birthing-ritual) to generate resources, and in the end 2 [Marionette Apprentice](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/100/marionette-apprentice) plus a sacrifice outlet took me out.
- G2: I had [Cutter](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/103/cori-steel-cutter) and enough burn, clearing the board while pushing damage through.
- G3: The opponent played Gilded Goose on the play. On the draw, I used [Unholy](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/145/unholy-heat) to kill it, stalling them on one land, and steadily beat them down.
- A favorable matchup, same as R1. With great dodging, I reached 5-0!!!


### R6 & R7. ID 

At the end it was 5-0-2, a sure Top 8. In the Swiss rounds, my dodging was truly excellent, and apart from one mirror match, all my matchups were favorable, which is how I went straight 5-0 to earn a ticket to the finals. Normally, the top 4 would get to be on the play in the first Top 8 match, but because of the temptation of [Aerith](https://scryfall.com/card/ppro/2025-3/aerith-gainsborough), the three 5-1 tables behind all had one player concede to push a 6-1 up. I didn't expect that many 6-1s to come up, otherwise I should have played the last round after 5-0-1, since winning would give me the play, while losing or drawing would leave me on the draw, same as just making Top 8.

I finished fifth. After four straight times on the draw, I was on the draw in the finals too, and I ran into Titan, which had a win rate below 30 percent...



### R8. Top 8: Amulet Titan (on the draw, 0-2)

Opponent: 承翰

- G1: A very bad game. My opening hand was 2 lands, 1 [Violent Urge](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/164/violent-urge), 1 [Preordain](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/161/preordain), 1 [Mishra's Bauble](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/274/mishras-bauble), 1 [Slickshot](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/146/slickshot-show-off), and 1 growth [+2/+2](https://scryfall.com/card/mm2/149/mutagenic-growth). That is a hand that can kill on turn 3! [Preordain](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/161/preordain) even showed me [Lightning Bolt](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/141/lightning-bolt) and a second growth [+2/+2](https://scryfall.com/card/mm2/149/mutagenic-growth), giving me hope. But the opponent played [Arboreal Grazer](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/245/arboreal-grazer) on turn 1 to ramp into [Crumbling Vestige](https://scryfall.com/card/ogw/170/crumbling-vestige) for mana to play [Amulet of Vigor](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/121/amulet-of-vigor), played a land on turn 2, and on turn 3 played a land with four lands for a [Scapeshift](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/201/scapeshift) kill...
- G2: I kept an opening hand of 1 [Preordain](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/161/preordain), 1 [Consign](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/54/consign-to-memory), 2 [Mishra's Bauble](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/274/mishras-bauble), 1 [Violent Urge](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/164/violent-urge), and 2 lands. [Preordain](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/161/preordain) looked at two lands and I still drew a land, the 2 [Mishra's Bauble](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/274/mishras-bauble) drew 1 land and 1 [DRC](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/121/dragons-rage-channeler), and then I drew two more lands... The opponent drew two [Arboreal Grazer](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/245/arboreal-grazer) and also used [Green Sun's Zenith](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/150/green-suns-zenith) to find a third [Arboreal Grazer](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/245/arboreal-grazer) to build a defensive line, while I had only one [DRC](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/121/dragons-rage-channeler) on the battlefield without delirium...
