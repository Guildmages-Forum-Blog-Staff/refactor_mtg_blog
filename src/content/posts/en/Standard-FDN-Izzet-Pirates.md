## Before We Start

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/068103921efa.webp)

[Foundations](https://scryfall.com/sets/fdn?as=grid&order=set) reprinted [Corsair Captain](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/506/corsair-captain) from [Jumpstart](https://scryfall.com/sets/jmp?as=grid&order=set) into Standard, solving the long-standing problem of Pirates lacking a three-mana lord, but how much does it really help in practice? This time we'll try something new and examine its performance in both Standard and Pioneer together!


## Card Analysis

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/c514f3237690.webp)
>Corsair Captain
>Creature — Human Pirate
>When this creature enters, create a Treasure token.
>Other Pirate creatures you control get +1/+1.

A three-mana lord that gives a [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure) on entering, as simple and easy to remember as its name. Although it's a design so simple it's almost suspected of laziness, an extra [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure) always adds a good amount of flexibility, and it does interact with Pirates. First, [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure)'s artifact type pairs with [Goblin Tomb Raider](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/151/goblin-tomb-raider) and [Captain Storm, Cosmium Raider](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/227/captain-storm-cosmium-raider) to boost your striking power.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/d7e497b808c7.webp)

Next, it can also be used to cast spells, triggering the second-spell trigger of [Malcolm, the Eyes](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/219/malcolm-the-eyes) and [Breeches, the Blastmaker](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/197/breeches-the-blastmaker); at worst it's a mana jump and color fix.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/9aa72c9f4195.webp)

In the Pioneer card pool, besides lubricating three-color Pirates, [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure) is also an important source of revolt, so your [Fatal Push](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/93/fatal-push) is always ready to send away the opponent's key creature.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/13811d3134fd.webp)

## Standard: Izzet Pirates

In the pre-rotation Standard environment, which lacked a lord, we built an artifact Pirate route centered on [Captain Storm, Cosmium Raider](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/227/captain-storm-cosmium-raider). Now that we have a proper lord in [Corsair Captain](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/506/corsair-captain), we don't need to depend on [Captain Storm](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/227/captain-storm-cosmium-raider)'s unreliable pumping ability; in addition, the new Standard, with enchantments rampant, is unfriendly to red-blue and needs a more aggressive style of play, so we made the following adjustments:

[Decklist](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/6768661#paper)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/c2caa85001fb.webp)

We replaced the [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure) package's [Diamond Pick-Axe](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/143/diamond-pick-axe) and [Dire Flail](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/145/dire-flail-dire-blunderbuss) with [Leyline Axe](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/129/leyline-axe) and [Blacksmith's Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/125/blacksmiths-talent), using double strike and trample to boost breakthrough power and punch through the opponent's creature defenses. For removal we also added [Torch the Tower](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/153/torch-the-tower), which handles extreme aggro, and [Into the Flood Maw](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/52/into-the-flood-maw), which temporarily deals with enchantments.

{%youtube kA8pKtFb2mY %}

In [Game 1](https://youtu.be/kA8pKtFb2mY?t=1s) against Boros Auras, the [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure) from the turn-3 [Corsair Captain](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/506/corsair-captain) played a key role the next turn. At [46 seconds](https://youtu.be/kA8pKtFb2mY?t=46s) the opponent tried to put [Sheltered by Ghosts](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/30/sheltered-by-ghosts) on it as removal; an ordinary three-mana lord would mostly just get pressed down by the enchantment and then be beaten up by the aura creature. However, with the [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure), I responded with [Into the Flood Maw](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/52/into-the-flood-maw) to bounce the opponent's [Optimistic Scavenger](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/21/optimistic-scavenger), directly collapsing the opponent's tempo. At [1:08](https://youtu.be/kA8pKtFb2mY?t=1m08s) the opponent played a second [Sheltered by Ghosts](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/30/sheltered-by-ghosts), but by then I had enough mana to pay for ward, and a [Rebel Salvo](https://scryfall.com/card/one/144/rebel-salvo) saved the pressed-down [Breeches, Eager Pillager](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/137/breeches-eager-pillager) and decided the game.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/a805290ee0f6.webp)

The [second half](https://youtu.be/kA8pKtFb2mY?t=1m30s) demonstrates a [Leyline Axe](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/129/leyline-axe) opening hand: [Goblin Tomb Raider](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/151/goblin-tomb-raider) put out the firepower of a [Goblin Guide](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/127/goblin-guide), and picking up [Leyline Axe](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/129/leyline-axe) on turn 3 beat the opponent down to 6, and the game ended on turn 5.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/392e43baebc1.webp)




## Pioneer: Grixis Pirates

[Decklist](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/6764711#paper)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/8ffc109e5eaf.webp)

The biggest difference between Pioneer and Standard is that the Pirate cards from the old [Ixalan](https://scryfall.com/search?as=grid&order=name&q=%28set%3Arix+OR+set%3Axln%29) environment are available, so you no longer need to rely on artifacts and can play a pure tribal axis. The biggest boost from the old sets' black Pirates is the turn-2 hand disruption of [Kitesail Freebooter](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/107/kitesail-freebooter), which, together with [Thoughtseize](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/334/thoughtseize) and [Siren Stormtamer](https://scryfall.com/card/xln/79/siren-stormtamer) as escorts, keeps you from being helpless against all sorts of decks. The missing striking power is made up by the lord effects of [Corsair Captain](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/506/corsair-captain) and [Admiral Beckett Brass](https://scryfall.com/card/xln/217/admiral-beckett-brass).

{%youtube P9-9fqdO6vA %}

In [Game 1](https://youtu.be/P9-9fqdO6vA?t=01s) we faced a Phoenix deck, and the opponent smoothly pulled out two [Arclight Phoenix](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/100/arclight-phoenix) on turn 3. However, on the [next turn](https://youtu.be/P9-9fqdO6vA?t=52s) I drew [Corsair Captain](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/506/corsair-captain), and three creatures hit the opponent hard, dropping their life to 8 and making their play more conservative. The opponent clearly smelled [Fatal Push](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/93/fatal-push), and [threw out removal one after another](https://youtu.be/P9-9fqdO6vA?t=1m22s) trying to lure me into using up the [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure), but I didn't take the bait. At [2:42](https://youtu.be/P9-9fqdO6vA?t=2m42s) I sacrificed the [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure) to flash in [Spectral Sailor](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/164/spectral-sailor) and followed with [Fatal Push](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/93/fatal-push) to clear the way; the next hit dropped the opponent to 1, and then [Thoughtseize](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/334/thoughtseize) discarded the last nonland card in their hand, [Treasure Cruise](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/120/treasure-cruise), for the win.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/b410cec0c49e.webp)

[Game 2](https://youtu.be/P9-9fqdO6vA?t=3m01s) was a tribal-deck showdown, with the opponent on mono-blue Spirits. On [turn 3](https://youtu.be/P9-9fqdO6vA?t=3m25s), right after [Corsair Captain](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/506/corsair-captain) entered, I immediately used the [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure) to cast [Thoughtseize](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/334/thoughtseize) and discard the opponent's [Shacklegeist](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/112/shacklegeist), breaking up their creature interference. Although the opponent gained the advantage in the life-total race with two [Supreme Phantom](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/115/supreme-phantom) lords, unfortunately they hadn't accounted for [Admiral Beckett Brass](https://scryfall.com/card/xln/217/admiral-beckett-brass)'s ability, and at [4:09](https://youtu.be/P9-9fqdO6vA?t=4m09s) I commandeered one of their [Supreme Phantom](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/115/supreme-phantom), leaving their damage insufficient, and the opponent conceded outright.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/1941d5d977bd.webp)


## Conclusion

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-FDN-Izzet-Pirates/02060b871b7e.webp)

In the demonstrations above, we can see the advantage brought by [Corsair Captain](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/506/corsair-captain)'s [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure): that one mana of key interference often steers the wind in my direction, and as a proper three-mana lord it also gives Pirates the go-wide striking power they lacked, letting a bunch of little scrubs deal damage effectively. Although the design is simple and unremarkable, it's a solid card that significantly improves Pirate decks' strength.
