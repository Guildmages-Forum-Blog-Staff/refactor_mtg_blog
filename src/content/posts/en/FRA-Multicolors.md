## Color Pair Themes

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Multicolors/1a9212b24d3e.webp)

### <span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span> <span style="color:#C3B270">White</span> Surveil Aggro

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Multicolors/30e4eb80c8e6.webp)

The students of Fatehold turn knowledge of the future into great power. Through {% mtglink "Your Fate Ends Here" %}, {% mtglink "Semester Foreseer" %}, and your Jace tokens, you can surveil and scry to do the same. Once you have arranged the best cards on top of your library for the situation in front of you, you can use {% mtglink "Desperate Futurescribe" %} and {% mtglink "Proft, Consulting Detective" %} to turn what you have learned into deadly weapons.

### <span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span> <span style="color:#808080">Black</span> Graveyard Counting

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Multicolors/2fd8c2c3fdcf.webp)

The scholars of Theorix excel at using the rules of mathematics and twisting their opponents' minds. Your goal is to get seven or more cards in your graveyard, so pile on mill effects like {% mtglink "Theorix Metamage" %} and cheap spells like {% mtglink "Unsummon" %}. Once you reach this threshold, cards like {% mtglink "Void Extrapolator" %} become much stronger. If you have already stuffed your graveyard with creatures and planeswalkers, {% mtglink "Winter, Tormented Loner " %} can deal your opponent a fatal blow.

### <span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span> <span style="color:#808080">Black</span> Going Face

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Multicolors/41ecf51756d0.webp)

The alumni of Stingerquill turn their words into blades. Use direct damage spells and removal effects like {% mtglink "Essence Burn" %}, {% mtglink "Fulminous Forte" %}, and {% mtglink "Stingerquill Charm" %} to ruthlessly weaken your opponent and their creatures. Dealing noncombat damage unlocks the abilities of {% mtglink "Grim Repriser" %} and other Stingerquill cards, letting a well-timed burn spell bring the game to a cruel curtain call.

### <span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span> <span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span> Mana Growth

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Multicolors/cd0625afd911.webp)

Konstrari trains artisan mages to build giant creations for the Shapers. Ramp with the heartwood tokens from {% mtglink "Woodwork Prodigy" %} and {% mtglink "Konstrari Improviser" %}. The extra mana from these tokens lets you cast your biggest creature spells far ahead of schedule, so don't be afraid to fill your deck with several {% mtglink "Archive Arbiter" %} or {% mtglink "Vinelasher Adept" %}. This deck's mana curve may lean toward expensive spells, so don't stick to convention, just like a true Konstrari artisan.

### <span style="color:#C3B270">White</span> <span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span> Life Gain

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Multicolors/f5ec8a8df1f9.webp)

For Vigorbloom, medicine and botany have always gone hand in hand. Use {% mtglink "Vigorbloom Vanguard" %} to turn the life you gain into growth: it puts a +1/+1 counter on your creature while healing you. The core of this archetype is nurturing counters and maintaining your life total, making sure you have both. {% mtglink "Graft Surgeon" %} keeps the counters on the battlefield after your creature dies, and {% mtglink "Bloombrute" %} makes sure your hand never runs out of resources whenever you gain life.

### <span style="color:#C3B270">White</span> <span style="color:#808080">Black</span> Liliana's War of Attrition

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Multicolors/d9faa33fd175.webp)

In the Echoverse, Liliana became a healer rather than a necromancer. Use tiny creatures like {% mtglink "Rank Rat" %} and {% mtglink "Campus Crier" %} to wear your opponent down bit by bit while playing both sides of the graveyard. Once these creatures have outlived their usefulness, you can use {% mtglink "Edgar, Ancient Bloodlord" %} to turn their deaths into your reward, or use {% mtglink "Twisted Fates" %} to transform them into something more powerful.

### <span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span> <span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span> Chandra's Prowess

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Multicolors/e13ecfe794a3.webp)

What if Chandra became a force of dominating control rather than fighting for freedom? Use cards that care about noncreature spells, like {% mtglink "Saheeli, Jewel of Avishkar" %}, to establish your absolute control over the game. Then flood the battlefield with cheap spells like {% mtglink "Unsummon" %} and {% mtglink "No Admittance" %}, making sure your cards keep triggering and the opponent's creatures can't stay on the battlefield.

### <span style="color:#808080">Black</span> <span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span> Garruk's Bestiary

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Multicolors/00e29563c2a2.webp)

The hunter has become the very thing he was bent on destroying! Follow Garruk's relentless advance and trample your opponent with an army of creatures like {% mtglink "Primal Witchstalker" %} that roll out huge value. Every creature you cast brings a benefit, whether it is the protection of {% mtglink "Teyo, Diamondblade Mage" %} or the removal of {% mtglink "Hapatra, the Desert Fang" %}. If your creatures unfortunately fall, don't worry, just use {% mtglink "Rewrite Regrets" %} to pull them back.

### <span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span> <span style="color:#C3B270">White</span> Ajani's Army

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Multicolors/b3207b376fd9.webp)

Ajani has always been a warrior, but his Echoverse counterpart is more fierce than friendly. Turn that warlike spirit into +1/+1 counters and pour them into your creatures. Look for sources of Cadet tokens like {% mtglink "Hexhaven Battalion" %} and {% mtglink "Heartstring Puller" %}, since these cards can usually trade one card for multiple creatures. That speed is the key to winning the game, especially when paired with heavy Equipment like {% mtglink "Warrior's Blades" %}.

### <span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span> <span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span> Jace's Control

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/FRA-Multicolors/3b5785e407b3.webp)

Jace, the puppet master behind the multiverse, is ready to do whatever it takes to win. Start by empowering Jace, then keep squeezing value out of your planeswalker tokens throughout the game. {% mtglink "Kiora of Salt and Sand" %} can give your Jace tokens extra abilities, {% mtglink "Countersculpt" %} costs less whenever you behold Jace, and {% mtglink "Inspired Tethermage" %} grows along with the copies of this mind mage.



## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional must-pick, grants a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional must-pick in the right colors, grants a huge advantage
4.0: First-pick quality, excellent synergy with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card for splashing/switching colors or moving into a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons, commons, and other sufficiently strong cards of the set, good synergy with the color pair's theme, a key card for moving into a color pair
3.0: Important card for the color pair's theme, and other above-average cards you want in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average, generally leaning toward not playing, usable as filler when necessary but not in large numbers. A 2.0 can also be the score for a powerful sideboard card.
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play it, it will only help you lose

## Conclusion
In this Echoverse we get to see a different side of many old friends, which must be quite interesting. The set's power level is obviously super strong too, and Constructed is bound to see big changes as well. I even want to call back some of my old playing friends to play this very fun new set.
