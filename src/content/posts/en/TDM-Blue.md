## Conclusion

Many of the blue cards in this set have relatively low costs, which is great for triggering Flurry and extremely key in the Jeskai color pair. Temur has five creatures that can trigger Ferocious. Sultai has relatively fewer cards related to the graveyard, but can rely on Harmonize to trigger leave-the-graveyard effects.

This is a pretty good three-color environment, and Limited should be quite fun to play. I also look forward to some cards entering Constructed and affecting the format.
