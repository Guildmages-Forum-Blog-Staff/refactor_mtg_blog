Reality Fracture (FRA) prerelease events take place on September 25, 2026, with the official release a week later on October 2, 2026. Want to know more about individual card ratings and advice for Limited? Then check out this series of FRA Limited overviews!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Reality Fracture Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/FRA-Mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick, brings an almost game-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors, brings a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick, great synergy with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card to splash/switch colors or to move into the colors
3.5: Top uncommon/common of the set and other cards of sufficient power, good synergy with the color pair's theme, a key card for moving into the colors
3.0: An important card for the color pair's theme, and other above-average cards; you will want it in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average, generally not played, can be used as filler when necessary but not in excess. A 2.0 can also be the rating for a powerful sideboard card.
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play it, it will only help you lose

## Conclusion
It's very heartwarming to see so many old friends return in new forms. If there are homages we haven't noticed, please be sure to let us know, readers. Players who like big creatures will definitely love this set! [Ruric Thar](https://scryfall.com/card/fra/265/ruric-thar-magecrusher) and [Ghalta](https://scryfall.com/card/fra/260/ghalta-the-unstoppable) are both so cool! And green's fixing and mana acceleration are plentiful and good, so you can build decks very flexibly in Limited.

We highly recommend that readers go to a nearby store and try this set's Limited!
