Murders at Karlov Manor (MKM) prerelease events take place on February 2, 2024, with the official release a week later on February 9, 2024. Want to know more about individual card ratings in Limited and some possibly interesting synergies? Then check out this series of MKM Limited overviews!

For the mechanics, see this article: [Murders at Karlov Manor Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/MKM-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick
4.0: Worth a first pick
3.5: Strong enough but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you would very much like it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick in a draft
2.0 to 1.0: Below average (you would rather not play it than play this card)
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable (basically don't play it, it will only help you lose)

## Green

**GY_Player:** 2.5

It goes well with this set's collect evidence ability. Toughness 3 defends well against the face-down 2/2 creatures that will be very common in this set, but it has no trading ability.

The activated ability can have a very high payoff in certain deck types.

**GY_Player:** 2.5

The cost is high for a combat trick, but the upside is that it permanently adds toughness, and removing the suspected status has both good (it can block) and bad (it loses menace) sides. It will be stronger in sealed.

## Conclusion

Personally, I feel green has no especially outstanding super bombs this time; it is a solid, middle-of-the-road color.
The advantage is better fixing; I think green-based multicolor decks (splashing other colors' bombs and removal) will be quite strong.

This is the first time in all my years of playing Limited that I've given ratings. I hope it helps everyone.
