 ## Conclusion

This time the colorless slot has many serviceable filler creatures that lubricate a build nicely in Limited. The 10 common two-color lands and the 5 rare two-color lands also make this set's multicolor builds more stable, and it is definitely a set very friendly to "three-color" decks.
This is probably the most words I have ever written on Limited card ratings. While analyzing power level, I also shared many classic little Marvel stories with everyone, and I hope you enjoy them.
The prerelease will officially ignite the battle on June 19, 2026! Whether you want to be a Hero who saves the world or a Villain scheming in the shadows, I wish you all great luck when opening packs and perfect draws in every game at the event!
