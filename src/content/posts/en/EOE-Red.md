## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick, brings a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick in the right archetype, brings a huge advantage
4.0: Has first-pick value, has excellent synergy with the archetype's theme, and can be the deciding card for splashing/switching colors or entering the archetype
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards, with good synergy with the archetype's theme, and can be a key card for entering the archetype
3.0: Important cards for the archetype's theme, as well as other above-average cards, that you want in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0-1.0: Below average, generally not played, can be used as filler if necessary but not too many
0.5-0.0: Unplayable, basically do not play it; playing it only helps you lose

## Conclusion

Red can be called the representative color of "multi-line bursts" in this set: it carries both the traditional "burn-the-face aggro" and acts as the linking hub for the new mechanics "Warp / Station / Void / artifacts entering and leaving." Compared with other colors, red decks can often suddenly open up a life total gap within two to three turns, or deliver a destructive wave of attacks in the mid to late game by sacrificing resources.

## Beginner Advice

#### Want a quick start? Grasp two tips first
1. "Red = speed + firepower + keyword bursts." Aggro and going face are still red's main theme, but this set hides speed within three mechanics: Warp, Station, and "sacrifice-triggered Void."
2. First ask yourself: can I trigger it reliably? Red has many cards that look great, but if you lack Spacecraft, artifacts, or sacrificable permanents on the battlefield, their power drops instantly.
3. Artifact density ≧ 7, Spacecraft ≥ 2-3, sacrificable resources ≥ 5. Once you hit these, red's burst will fire reliably.
4. If you cannot reach these three, put the cards that rely heavily on them in the sideboard.

#### Deckbuilding Quick Checklist

**Removal:** 3 or more single-target damage spells (Bombard/Bolt/Plunge...).
**Theme counts:**
- Artifacts ≥ 7 (if going blue-red / Station)
- Spacecraft ≥ 2 (triggers most red-white payoffs)
- Sacrificable resources ≥ 5 (more if going Void)
- Finishers: 2-3 big creatures or high-burst cards.
- Combat tricks: about 2 is enough; too many will clog your hand.

**Sideboard weapons:** Bring in targeted cards against opponents with too many artifacts, but please do not touch them in the main deck.
