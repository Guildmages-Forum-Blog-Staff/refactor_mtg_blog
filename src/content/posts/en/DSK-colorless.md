## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors
4.0: Worth a first pick
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you would really like it in your final deck)
2.5: Average level, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick in a draft
2.0–1.0: Below average (you would rather not play it than play this card)
0.5–0.0: Unplayable (basically never play it; it will only help you lose)

## Conclusion
This set's main axes are none other than delirium and enchantments, and this phenomenon is easy to see in the colorless cards and SPG too: there are many cards that work with those two themes, and some ways to disrupt them. I believe that after reading this analysis everyone will feel more confident about this set's Limited, especially since themes and card synergy are the more important concepts in this set. And very luckily, this set's lands are very strong, giving deckbuilding more flexibility. Dear readers, why not take this chance to try multicolor decks and experience a richer Limited journey.
