## Before We Start

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Cabaretti-Voia/a4b3ba145046.webp)

At the Murders at Karlov Manor prerelease, we had a chance to get three mythic rare legendary cards that can't be found in regular booster packs: [Melek, Reforged Researcher](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/0/1/01c5ede0-a098-4f21-8b7e-795a83e75aae.jpg), [Tomik, Wielder of Law](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/2/c/2c5a7550-fe1a-4797-9583-70ab56cfac0d.jpg), and [Voja, Jaws of the Conclave](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg). Among them, [Voja](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg) has a sturdy body and excellent tribal synergy, and the Standard card pool has plenty of quality Wolves and Elves too. What sparks can they make together?

## Card Analysis

As usual, let's first see what [Voja](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg) can do:

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Cabaretti-Voia/816091a244f3.webp)

[Voja, Jaws of the Conclave](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg)

> Vigilance, trample, ward {3}
> Whenever Voja, Jaws of the Conclave attacks, put X +1/+1 counters on each creature you control, where X is the number of Elves you control. Draw a card for each Wolf you control.

Judging by the body and keyword abilities alone, [Voja](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg) is a five-mana war god. Besides standing tall to attack and having trample, it also has ward 3, second only to [Iymrith, Desert Doom](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/2/5/25f5eed5-75e3-4362-9ab2-cacfd276bd3b.jpg), so it's a tough customer that won't be removed casually. On top of that, when it attacks it draws cards equal to the number of Wolves you control and puts +1/+1 counters on your whole team based on the number of Elves you control. Even on its own it draws at least one card, and with Elves your whole team's stats balloon quickly; if you can swing a second time, that usually decides the game.

Based on [Voja](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg)'s dual tribal synergy, I made the following build:

First, let's go over the Elf choices. Although [Voja](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg) comes from the White-Green guild Selesnya, its colors are actually Red-Green-White, and the cards in Standard that satisfy all three colors and the tribal requirement are naturally the Cabaretti Elves of New Capenna. [Brazen Upstart](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/c/bc7aaaeb-bea9-4d63-9baf-8c2b32c51c07.jpg) and [Fleetfoot Dancer](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/a/5/a5fc22e2-a1a7-40ed-bbbd-5f6b09359253.jpg) are good choices among them. [Brazen Upstart](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/c/bc7aaaeb-bea9-4d63-9baf-8c2b32c51c07.jpg)'s 4/2 vigilance body can trade head-on with [Graveyard Trespasser](https://scryfall.com/card/mid/104/graveyard-trespasser-graveyard-glutton) and [[叛教幫傳教士]](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/8/9/89345f55-2b32-4356-945a-d56dded39909.jpg), and when it dies it looks at five cards and takes a creature to make up the card disadvantage, which makes it a very capable filler for an aggressive deck. As for [Fleetfoot Dancer](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/a/5/a5fc22e2-a1a7-40ed-bbbd-5f6b09359253.jpg), there's nothing to complain about: it has haste, trample and lifelink, and is useful whether you're racing for life with haste or in a life-total race. I also bring along a [Cabaretti Ascendancy](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/0/7/0754c42a-b92f-40ac-bd4c-75e1db2462e8.jpg) as a source of staying power.

Besides the Cabaretti Elves, I also brought in [Llanowar Loamspeaker](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/d/bd5611db-82dd-464d-8b03-70d7619dcefe.jpg) and [Yavimaya Iconoclast](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/b/1b1af9be-7f5e-48e9-a6e7-9261e199812e.jpg) from Dominaria, and [[半狐保鑣]](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/4/4494dfa1-1343-417e-b0c5-2b096442dd0e.jpg) from Eldraine (yes, you read that right, it's an Elf!) to fill out the curve. [Llanowar Loamspeaker](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/d/bd5611db-82dd-464d-8b03-70d7619dcefe.jpg) is not only solid two-mana ramp but can also animate a land to add to your damage, and the key point is that it works well with the Restless lands in Standard, letting you trigger their attack abilities without paying mana. [[半狐保鑣]](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/4/4494dfa1-1343-417e-b0c5-2b096442dd0e.jpg) is a [Banisher Priest](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/0/e/0e518f9e-d74d-4a6f-8fde-5e3ec5834e63.jpg) with built-in flash. I often use it at instant speed to exile an opponent's strong creature and clear the way; against a swarm of tokens attacking it can even make a clean one-for-one removal, and it can also exile my own creatures to dodge removal or sweepers. [Yavimaya Iconoclast](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/b/1b1af9be-7f5e-48e9-a6e7-9261e199812e.jpg) is just a curve filler, and the bonus of being able to kick it for haste to help race is a big plus.

For the Wolves, we turn to the wolf packs of Innistrad. Although there are mythic and rare options such as [Primal Adversary](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/6/1/615f530c-1675-4f99-a3d0-52104671957d.jpg) and [Cemetery Prowler](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/8/d8a136da-6a49-4b90-8631-e2541a8e1cc7.jpg), after testing I kept only the one-mana [Ascendant Packleader](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/2/12e2feda-6a45-474c-92b9-3f94171a81ef.jpg) and the four-mana planeswalker [Arlinn, the Pack's Hope](https://scryfall.com/card/mid/211/arlinn-the-packs-hope-arlinn-the-moons-fury), mainly as supporting players that increase how many cards [Voja](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg) draws and as attack-line reinforcements. [Arlinn](https://scryfall.com/card/mid/211/arlinn-the-packs-hope-arlinn-the-moons-fury) herself can be seen as two 2/2 Wolf tokens; after surviving a turn, use the +1 flash to build a tactical advantage, and at the same time flip to her night side, [Arlinn, the Moon's Fury](<(https://scryfall.com/card/mid/211/arlinn-the-packs-hope-arlinn-the-moons-fury)>), for a powerful attack. At five mana I additionally include the true partner [Tolsimir, Midnight's Light](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/0/8/08d22402-c41d-43d7-be1f-42be1e300726.jpg) and his own token [Voja Fenstalker](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/2/1/2175200f-3ed4-4b39-ac76-a7d25967aa8a.jpg) as extras (the 2-1 split is my personal habit; that slot could also hold a third [Voja, Jaws of the Conclave](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg)). For removal I chose [Lightning Helix](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/1/4101e3fe-b0e7-4f0f-b9ac-9b61a4d628b3.jpg) and [Depart the Realm](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/5/2/522aa72b-2b8c-484c-872b-f082101cee35.jpg) to broadly answer threats of all sizes, and to finish off damaged creatures.

Along the way the opponent seemed to try to use [Path of Peril](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/7/378ddf9d-6049-4243-ba85-649b7b378ed3.jpg) followed by a bail-out play to deal with [Voja](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg), but luckily I had [[半狐保鑣]](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/4/4494dfa1-1343-417e-b0c5-2b096442dd0e.jpg) in hand to step in in time and foil the opponent's attempt. The other game was against red aggro. While I was stuck on lands, I flashed in [[半狐保鑣]](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/4/4494dfa1-1343-417e-b0c5-2b096442dd0e.jpg) to disrupt the opponent's offense, which let me stall until my fourth land arrived; after slamming [Fleetfoot Dancer](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/a/5/a5fc22e2-a1a7-40ed-bbbd-5f6b09359253.jpg) the situation turned around, and in the end lifelink won the life-total race.

## Conclusion

Most of the components of this [Voja](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg) deck come from the 2022 Standard year, a limited-time product that only came together after Standard was extended. Judging from the upcoming Standard sets previewed so far, there doesn't seem to be much room for reinforcement, so if you're interested in [Voja](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/f/bfa1bd2f-25bd-4fbd-877b-cef00ab7f92f.jpg), you'd better seize the chance to build it.
