Aetherdrift (DFT) will hold its prerelease events on February 7, 2025, and be officially released a week later on February 14, 2025. Want to know more about how individual cards rate in Limited, with recommendations? Then check out this series of DFT Limited introductions!

- For a mechanics breakdown, see this article: [Aetherdrift Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/DFT-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional must-pick, brings a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional must-pick if in the right colors, brings a huge advantage
4.0: First-pick value, extremely synergistic with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card for splashing/switching colors or entering a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other cards of sufficient power, synergize well with the color pair's theme, can be a key card for entering a color pair
3.0: Important cards for the color pair's theme and other above-average cards, you'd want them in your final deck
2.5: Average level, usually a decent curve filler
2.0~1.0: Below average, basically leaning toward not playing; can be used as filler if necessary, but not too many
0.5~0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play it, playing it will only help you lose

## Conclusion

White has quite a lot of Vehicles with excellent bodies, and even ways to cheat them into motion. But I'd remind everyone that you can't take too many Vehicles; you also need a certain number of crew creatures to get the Vehicles moving.
