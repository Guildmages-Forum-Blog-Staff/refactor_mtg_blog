3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards; synergizes well with the archetype and can be the key card for moving into a color pair

2.5: Average; usually a decent curve filler

0.5–0.0: Unplayable; basically never play them, they will only help you lose

## Conclusion

This time black's creatures are relatively conservative, replaced by plenty of quality removal and tricks. Picking the right uncommon creature can take your deck's theme up a level. From what we see so far, black's cards supporting Abzan and Mardu are the more powerful ones this time. Build an army, and use black to destroy the enemies in your way!!
