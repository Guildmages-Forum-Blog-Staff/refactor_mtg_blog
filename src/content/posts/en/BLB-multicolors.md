Bloomburrow (BLB) will hold its prerelease events on July 26, 2024, and officially release a week later on August 2, 2024. Want to know more about individual card ratings and recommendations in Limited? Then check out this series of BLB Limited reviews!

- For the mechanics overview, see this article: [Bloomburrow Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/BLB-mechanism/)
- For a first look at the Limited themes, see this article: [Bloomburrow Limited Preliminary](https://guildmagesforum.tw/BLB-Limited-Preliminary/)

> This Limited review series is based on ratings; some average cards, or cards with relatively simple abilities, will not receive specific commentary.

## Grading Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick
4.5: Unconditional first pick if the colors are right
4.0: Worth a first pick
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you will really want it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick in a draft
2.0-1.0: Below average (you would rather not play it than play it)
0.5-0.0: Unplayable (basically never play it, it will only help you lose)

Review:

- [Camellia, the Seedmiser](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/207/camellia-the-seedmiser) is a very important standalone card for the black-green Squirrel theme. If you see it, consider moving into black-green and building around it.

  > Forage is a cost for putting the +1/+1 counters. If you choose to sacrifice a Food as that cost, then after the ability goes on the stack, it will trigger the ability that makes a 1/1 Squirrel token. When the stack resolves, the Squirrel token enters first, and only then are +1/+1 counters put on all Squirrels.

- [Clement, the Worrywort](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/209/clement-the-worrywort) is a three-mana 3/3 vigilance with two positive abilities, and is also an important card for the blue-green enters/leaves theme.

Review:

- [Fireglass Mentor](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/213/fireglass-mentor) exiling cards is mandatory, which is awkward when you don't want to trigger its effect (e.g., not enough cards in your library), and in bad cases it can even affect whether you attack that turn. Used well it is still a card that earns resources, but you have to watch the board state when using it; it is not always good.
- [Gev, Scaled Scorch](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/214/gev-scaled-scorch) is not only able to actively trigger its enters-and-grow effect when you cast Lizards while on the battlefield; as long as you have first made the opponent lose life, other non-Lizard creatures that enter afterward also get bigger. If the opponent wants to remove it, they have to pay 2 life first.
- [Glarb, Calamity's Augur](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/215/glarb-calamitys-augur) is a very powerful and very profitable card, one of the few three-color cards worth splashing a third color for. Its own tap ability to surveil 2 also supports blue-black Threshold and black-green Forage, and helps make the top of your library more controllable.
