Foundations (FDN) will hold its prerelease events on November 8, 2024, and officially launch a week later on November 15, 2024. Want to learn more about how each card rates in Limited, with recommendations? Then check out this series of FDN Limited reviews!


## Rating Scale

5.0: An unconditional first pick that brings you close to winning on its own.
4.5: An unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors; brings a huge advantage.
4.0: Worth first-picking. Great synergy with the color pair's theme, and can be the deciding card for splashing / switching colors or moving into the color pair.
3.5: The set's top uncommons and commons, plus other cards of sufficient power. Good synergy with the color pair's theme, and a key card for moving into the color pair.
3.0: An important card for the color pair's theme, or another above-average card that you want in your final deck.
2.5: Average. Usually a decent curve filler.
2.0 to 1.0: Below average. You generally lean toward leaving it out, and can use it as filler when necessary, but not too many.
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable. Basically don't play it; putting it in only helps you lose.

## Conclusion
Red has quite a few good mid-to-late-game bombs this time. If you open one, it's well suited to being your main color. The thing to watch out for in particular is that the damage from the power-doubling card can be a bit terrifying.

> Of course, you should also be careful when your life total is exactly ten.
