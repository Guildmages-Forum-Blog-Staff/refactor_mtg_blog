## Color Pair Themes

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/EOE-Multicolors/b5231e0c95b5.webp)

### <span style="color:#C3B270">White</span><span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span> — Second Spell

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/EOE-Multicolors/29e5182fda41.webp)

Cast your second spell in a turn (the same as TDM's [Flurry](https://guildmagesforum.tw/TDM-mechanism/#%E9%80%A3%E5%92%92%EF%BC%88Flurry%EF%BC%89) mechanic) and gain extra resources. For example, {% mtglink "Cosmogrand Zenith" %} and {% mtglink "Station Monitor" %} can both make a lot of tokens, or later use +1/+1 to grow creatures and overpower the opponent. Note that casting a second spell on the opponent's turn also triggers it.


### <span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span><span style="color:#808080">Black</span> — Artifact Control

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/EOE-Multicolors/16ef6fac15ff.webp)

Build an artifact-based deck and slowly win by draining the opponent's resources. {% mtglink "Alpharael, Dreaming Acolyte" %} lets you refill your hand and, by discarding artifact cards, costs you no resources; after {% mtglink "Dubious Delicacy" %} enters and deals with an opposing creature, whether you use it to drain life or to finish the opponent off, it is quite good either way.

### <span style="color:#808080">Black</span><span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span> — Void

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/EOE-Multicolors/e3f8fd85e2f0.webp)

Use the Void mechanic, paying with creatures and artifacts to trigger devastating events. If {% mtglink "Interceptor Mechan" %} keeps triggering its Void ability, it is a terrifying presence as an air attacker; when {% mtglink "Alpharael, Stonechosen" %} attacks and triggers Void, it halves the opponent's life, and that damage is quite considerable.

One last reminder: the opponent's nonland permanents leaving the battlefield also trigger Void effects

### <span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span><span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span> — Landers and Landfall

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/EOE-Multicolors/ce6c0bc6e31a.webp)

Use Lander tokens and Landfall to create a mana advantage. Landers help you trigger Landfall effects more easily; they appear in all colors but are mainly distributed in red-green, such as {% mtglink "Biotech Specialist" %}. In addition, the Landfall ability of {% mtglink "Tannuk, Memorial Ensign" %} slowly drains the opponent to death and brings card advantage.

### <span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span><span style="color:#C3B270">White</span> — +1/+1 Counters

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/EOE-Multicolors/2900c518b171.webp)

Strengthen your whole army with +1/+1 counters, stack them up and watch your enemies fall. {% mtglink "Haliya, Ascendant Cadet" %} makes sure your creatures are fully armed and armored, and that your hand stays well supplied; {% mtglink "Drix Fatemaker" %} ensures your big creatures with counters trample the opponent to death.

### <span style="color:#C3B270">White</span><span style="color:#808080">Black</span> — Going Wide

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/EOE-Multicolors/5b5eb87579b9.webp)

Build the biggest army on the battlefield and conquer your opponent with sheer numbers. {% mtglink "Syr Vondam, the Lucent" %} can strengthen your many tokens or small creatures, and going a step further, you can defeat the opponent with double strike creatures like {% mtglink "Dual-Sun Adepts" %}.

### <span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span><span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span> — Artifact Aggro

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/EOE-Multicolors/f4f37998f443.webp)

Quite a few red and blue cards in this set care about artifacts entering or being tapped. {% mtglink "Syr Vondam, the Lucent" %} widens its attacking threat through cheap artifacts entering; whether used to attack or to pay for a Spacecraft's Station, they bring you extra looting value.

### <span style="color:#808080">Black</span><span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span> — Graveyard Digging

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/EOE-Multicolors/2412b7b299ce.webp)

Stack up your graveyard early through milling or surveil, then win by reanimating Spacecraft and creatures. {% mtglink "Seedship Broodtender" %} is a good early creature for black-green, and then through itself or {% mtglink "Xu-Ifit, Osteoharmonist" %} you bring back stronger creatures to get far ahead of the opponent in one go.

### <span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span><span style="color:#C3B270">White</span> — Spacecraft Station

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/EOE-Multicolors/e75e7f2a8043.webp)

Tap creatures to Station your Spacecraft, then reap the rewards of your dedicated crew. {% mtglink "Sami, Ship's Engineer" %} cares about whether your creatures are tapped, and if so you can make more creatures; {% mtglink "Sami, Ship's Engineer" %} is a good Spacecraft to Station.

### <span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span><span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span> — Mana Ramp

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/EOE-Multicolors/986d1897f30b.webp)

Blue-green uses Landers to ramp, quickly reaching the late game to suppress the opponent with expensive resources. {% mtglink "Biomechan Engineer" %} is a quality creature for this color pair and also brings cards and creature resources in the late game; in addition, blue-green has cards that care about you controlling lands with different names, such as {% mtglink "Fungal Colossus" %}, which can get down to two mana or even less to cast, and Landers make this easier to achieve.

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick, brings an almost game-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors, brings a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick, great synergy with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card to splash/switch colors or to move into the colors
3.5: Top uncommon/common of the set and other cards of sufficient power, good synergy with the color pair's theme, a key card for moving into the colors
3.0: An important card for the color pair's theme, and other above-average cards; you will want it in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average, generally not played, can be used as filler when necessary but not in excess
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play it, it will only help you lose

## Conclusion

Returning to a main-line Magic set after a long time, space is also a very popular theme. Spacecraft soar across the battlefield and creatures flit through the exile zone; let's fly together into the boundless cosmos of Sotheran.

(An aside: why does this space set have so many sweepers? Mono-white, mono-red, mono-black, black-red and blue-black each have one.)
