## Before We Start

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Abzan-Felothar/bb6b62f1a5cc.webp)

The Tarkir clan article series has finally reached the last clan, Abzan. The original timeline's Abzan khan [Anafenza, Unyielding Lineage](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/2/anafenza-unyielding-lineage) is thoroughly dead (although we can still faintly see her shadow), so the khan's seat in this set is held by the new kid [Felothar, Dawn of the Abzan](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/184/felothar-dawn-of-the-abzan). How can this new khan perform in Standard?

## Card Analysis

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Abzan-Felothar/714d231e6355.webp)
>Felothar, Dawn of the Abzan
>Legendary Creature | Human Warrior
>Trample
>Whenever Felothar enters or attacks, you may sacrifice a nonland permanent. When you do, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control.

[Felothar](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/184/felothar-dawn-of-the-abzan)'s ability sacrifices board resources to grow the whole team. The best way to pair it is of course with creatures that make all kinds of tokens: [Clue](https://scryfall.com/card/tmkm/18/clue), [Food](https://scryfall.com/card/tblb/27/food), [Map](https://scryfall.com/card/tlci/17/map), [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/23/treasure)... there is basically no shortage of fodder. But if you take [Felothar](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/184/felothar-dawn-of-the-abzan) out, can these creatures still come together? The decklist is as follows:

[Decklist](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7097981#paper)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Abzan-Felothar/8d1830a263aa.webp)

The key to linking these creatures together is Ixalan's [Kellan, Daring Traveler // Journey On](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/231/kellan-daring-traveler-journey-on). [Kellan](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/231/kellan-daring-traveler-journey-on) can not only Adventure to make a [Map](https://scryfall.com/card/tlci/17/map), but when attacking can also take a creature card with mana value three or less from the top of your library straight into your hand. [Felothar](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/184/felothar-dawn-of-the-abzan) interacts well with his ability, so I built around [Kellan](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/231/kellan-daring-traveler-journey-on). The [Map](https://scryfall.com/card/tlci/17/map) package also has the backbone attacker [Sentinel of the Nameless City](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/211/sentinel-of-the-nameless-city), which reliably makes [Maps](https://scryfall.com/card/tlci/17/map). It is a 3/4 vigilance that makes a [Map](https://scryfall.com/card/tlci/17/map) on entering and attacking, helping you hit land drops while continuously growing.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Abzan-Felothar/11b64c6ca30d.webp)

There are many choices for the [Food](https://scryfall.com/card/tblb/27/food) package. I use [Vinereap Mentor](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/238/vinereap-mentor), which makes [Food](https://scryfall.com/card/tblb/27/food) on entering and dying, and [Gumdrop Poisoner // Tempt with Treats](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/93/gumdrop-poisoner-tempt-with-treats), which can make [Food](https://scryfall.com/card/tblb/27/food) for one mana at instant speed. [Vinereap Mentor](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/238/vinereap-mentor) is a two-mana curve filler creature, built on the premise of being removed or traded, and its 3 power hurts to be hit by, giving it a better chance to trade off an opposing creature. [Gumdrop Poisoner](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/93/gumdrop-poisoner-tempt-with-treats) is the life-total puller, and once the land is down it has a chance to act as removal. Since I wanted more one-mana token-making on the curve, I also brought in the [Clue](https://scryfall.com/card/tmkm/18/clue) package's [Novice Inspector](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/29/novice-inspector).

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Abzan-Felothar/1428431b8259.webp)

Next are the two free three-drop slots. This curve does not lack quality creatures: [Preacher of the Schism](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/113/preacher-of-the-schism), [Unstoppable Slasher](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/119/unstoppable-slasher), [Braids, Arisen Nightmare](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/133/braids-arisen-nightmare), and so on are all top choices of the moment, so decide by your own preference. I chose the versatile, powerful solo fighter [Glissa Sunslayer](https://scryfall.com/card/one/202/glissa-sunslayer).

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Abzan-Felothar/acd74fcf6481.webp)

With such a traditional midrange creature base, is it competitive enough in today's Standard, with demons dancing everywhere? The removal choices are the key. Given that artifacts and enchantments are currently extremely rampant in Standard, I main-deck four [Troublemaker Ouphe](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/194/troublemaker-ouphe) to handle them. Anyway, kicker costs basically nothing in this deck, and it is both a walking two-mana exile answer and something [Kellan](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/231/kellan-daring-traveler-journey-on) can pull up, so it should perform well.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Abzan-Felothar/3476d307acec.webp)

For instant and sorcery removal, I set up a middle-of-the-road mix of [Duress](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/606/duress), [Get Lost](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/14/get-lost), and [Go for the Throat](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/102/go-for-the-throat).

## Gameplay Demo

{%youtube -20RC-tMPvo %}

The games in the test video... in a nutshell, it is all about the unrivaled [Troublemaker Ouphe](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/194/troublemaker-ouphe). First the [Ouphe](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/194/troublemaker-ouphe) tears apart the opponent's artifacts and enchantments to disrupt them, then [Felothar](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/184/felothar-dawn-of-the-abzan) forcefully holds the line and beats the opponent down (in the last two games, the opponent surrendered after just seeing [Felothar](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/184/felothar-dawn-of-the-abzan) on top of the library). In the third game the opponent was clearly rattled after their [Cutter](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/103/cori-steel-cutter) got torn apart on time, and handed over [Monstrous Rage](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/142/monstrous-rage) at a strange moment, throwing away a chance to turn the game around for nothing.

## Conclusion

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Abzan-Felothar/32f8937a65ef.webp)

Although at first glance it looks like a pile of mixed cards at midrange, it actually performs surprisingly well. The reason may be [Troublemaker Ouphe](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/194/troublemaker-ouphe), but [Felothar](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/184/felothar-dawn-of-the-abzan)'s team-wide growth is the key to kicking the opponent down. The unfortunate part is that the test video did not capture [Felothar](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/184/felothar-dawn-of-the-abzan)'s performance in a long game, so here I use a screenshot from when I cleared the quest with the old version against mono-white lifegain to illustrate (it differs from the new version only by [Honest Rutstein](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/207/honest-rutstein)). In that game, early on [Troublemaker Ouphe](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/194/troublemaker-ouphe) tore apart the opponent's [Authority of the Consuls](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/137/authority-of-the-consuls), [Kellan](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/231/kellan-daring-traveler-journey-on) kept refilling creatures to hold off the opponent's [Ajani's Pridemate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/135/ajanis-pridemate), and [Glissa](https://scryfall.com/card/one/202/glissa-sunslayer)'s first strike plus counter removal created a tactical advantage. The result was [Felothar](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/184/felothar-dawn-of-the-abzan) fattening up the whole row of creatures and in turn suppressing the opponent, draining their 50-plus life points dry to win.
