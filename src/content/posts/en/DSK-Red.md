## Grading Scale

5.0: Always take it
4.5: Always take it if you are in the right colors
4.0: Worth a first pick
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you will really want it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick in a draft
2.0～1.0: Below average (you would rather not play it than play it)
0.5～0.0: Unplayable (basically never play it; it will only help you lose)

## Conclusion
Compared with the heavily tribal themes of Bloomburrow, in this set single-card strength matters somewhat more than color-pair themes in Limited. Staying flexible and always being ready to switch or jump colors will be a very important lesson for Duskmourn drafts.
