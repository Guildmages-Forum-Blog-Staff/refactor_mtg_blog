## Before We Start

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Terminal-Adamantoise/189b4a0b7906.webp)

In FFXV, the [Ancient Adamantoise](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/172/ancient-adamantoise) is the ultimate hunt target and the key to earning the Platinum Trophy. Its absurdly thick health bar is recreated on the Magic card as an eye-popping 8/20 body, and the generous rewards it drops on death become a pile of [Treasures](https://scryfall.com/card/tfin/36/treasure) on cardboard. Beyond protecting your own team and being very hard to kill, does this thing have a future in the current Standard environment? The new cards from *[Edge of Eternities](https://scryfall.com/sets/eoe?as=grid&order=set)* have the answer!

## Card Analysis

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Terminal-Adamantoise/f5d8eaa97a8b.webp)
>●**Ancient Adamantoise**
>**Creature - Turtle**
>**Vigilance, ward {3}**
>**Damage dealt to this creature isn't removed during cleanup steps.**
>**If a source would deal damage to you or another permanent you control, prevent that damage. This creature is dealt that much damage instead.**
>**When this creature dies, exile it and create ten tapped Treasure tokens.**

[Ancient Adamantoise](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/172/ancient-adamantoise) effectively gives you and your other creatures 20-plus extra life (excess damage your opponent pours into it gets absorbed too). As long as it doesn't eat removal, you almost never have to worry about being burst down, and when you're behind, holding on for two or three turns is usually no problem. When it dies it also produces ten [Treasures](https://scryfall.com/card/tfin/36/treasure) for you to play with, and that is where I focused my build. There are three problems, though:

1. How to cast [Ancient Adamantoise](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/172/ancient-adamantoise) quickly
2. How to reliably get it into the graveyard
3. How to make use of all those [Treasures](https://scryfall.com/card/tfin/36/treasure)

The decklist I came up with as the answer is below:
[Decklist](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7314212#paper)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Terminal-Adamantoise/a82c0085b804.webp)

The cards that pair with [Ancient Adamantoise](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/172/ancient-adamantoise) are [Biotech Specialist](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/214/biotech-specialist) and [Terminal Velocity](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/163/terminal-velocity). [Biotech Specialist](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/214/biotech-specialist) is an early-game accelerant: it creates a [Lander](https://scryfall.com/card/teoe/8/lander) token when it enters, and whenever you sacrifice an artifact it deals 2 damage to your opponent, so the ten [Treasures](https://scryfall.com/card/tfin/36/treasure) from [Ancient Adamantoise](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/172/ancient-adamantoise) add up to exactly 20. Then [Terminal Velocity](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/163/terminal-velocity) can drop a creature or artifact from your hand onto the battlefield, giving that permanent haste and an ability that deals damage to every creature, based on its mana value, when it leaves the battlefield. It is sacrificed automatically at the beginning of the end step. Paired with [Ancient Adamantoise](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/172/ancient-adamantoise), that is one punch for 8 damage plus an 8-damage sweep that wipes out your opponent's threats. Next turn, slam [Biotech Specialist](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/214/biotech-specialist) onto the battlefield, sacrifice your [Treasures](https://scryfall.com/card/tfin/36/treasure), and shoot your opponent until they burst.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Terminal-Adamantoise/eda8ab418456.webp)

To support this plan, the deck has to be a red-green ramp shell with big creatures as its finishers. To go with [Biotech Specialist](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/214/biotech-specialist), I used the new set's [Lander](https://scryfall.com/card/teoe/8/lander) ramp package: [Sami's Curiosity](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/203/samis-curiosity), [Larval Scoutlander](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/194/larval-scoutlander), and [Lithobraking](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/142/lithobraking), filling out the one-mana and three-mana [Lander](https://scryfall.com/card/teoe/8/lander) curve. [Sami's Curiosity](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/203/samis-curiosity) makes a [Lander](https://scryfall.com/card/teoe/8/lander) for one mana, and [Larval Scoutlander](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/194/larval-scoutlander) can sacrifice a [Lander](https://scryfall.com/card/teoe/8/lander) to fetch two basic lands, getting you to six mana on turn four at the fastest. [Lithobraking](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/142/lithobraking) helps you survive the early game as a small sweeper, and even against a non-creature deck it works as an instant-speed [Lander](https://scryfall.com/card/teoe/8/lander) maker.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Terminal-Adamantoise/b28436287ef0.webp)

Of course, having only four [Ancient Adamantoise](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/172/ancient-adamantoise) as big creatures is clearly unrealistic, so I added the usual red-green ramp cast: four each of [Overlord of the Hauntwoods](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/194/overlord-of-the-hauntwoods) and [Overlord of the Boilerbilges](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/146/overlord-of-the-boilerbilges) for ramp and damage support. Then four [Smuggler's Surprise](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/180/smugglers-surprise) refill your resources in the mid to late game or drop two creatures at instant speed to ambush your opponent, and with [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tfin/36/treasure) support you can pay for the full mode set with tons of mana (mill four and take up to two creature and/or land cards, put up to two creature cards from your hand onto the battlefield, and your creatures with power 4 or greater gain hexproof and indestructible until end of turn).

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Terminal-Adamantoise/d69494c45524.webp)

The last puzzle piece is a way to get the creature combo pieces into your hand: [Analyze the Pollen](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/150/analyze-the-pollen), which, after you collect evidence, can search your library for any creature or land card, and [Archdruid's Charm](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/151/archdruids-charm), a versatile card that lets you choose between searching for a creature at instant speed, searching for a land and putting it onto the battlefield tapped, exiling a target artifact or enchantment, or putting a +1/+1 counter on your creature and having it fight an opponent's creature. For lands I added a few [Fountainport](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/253/fountainport), using its ability to sacrifice tokens and draw cards to recoup the [Treasures](https://scryfall.com/card/tfin/36/treasure) from [Ancient Adamantoise](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/172/ancient-adamantoise) or [Landers](https://scryfall.com/card/teoe/8/lander) and boost the deck's staying power.

## Gameplay Demo

{%youtube IBUt43JUnGg %}

As the finisher of a big-creature ramp deck, [Ancient Adamantoise](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/172/ancient-adamantoise) really showed off its excellent ability to keep you alive. It not only protects your life total but also lets the [Overlords](https://scryfall.com/search?as=grid&order=name&q=overlord+commander%3ARG+%28game%3Apaper%29+legal%3Astandard+rarity%3Am) throw themselves into combat regardless of your opponent's creature lineup. It is a ruthless character that plays much stronger than it looks, truly worthy of being FFXV's most durable boss.
