## Grading Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick, gives you a nearly game-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors, gives you a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick, synergizes extremely well with a color pair's theme, and can be the deciding card for splashing/switching colors or settling into a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards, synergize well with a color pair's theme, and can be key cards for settling into a color pair
3.0: Important cards for a color pair's theme, plus other above-average cards that you want in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average, generally not played, can be used as filler if necessary but not too many
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable, basically never play it, doing so only helps you lose

Score: 3.0
Comment: This card actually hides a math problem: if your opponent's life total falls between 2^n and (2^(n+1))-1 (equal to either bound counts), then after at most n+1 ability triggers you can win the game.

> For example: your opponent has 20 life, which falls between 2^4 and (2^5)-1, and you attack with Alpharael. After at most 5 ability triggers, your opponent's life goes 20 → 10 → 5 → 2 → 1 → 0 and you win.

Okay, math done, now let us look at actual use. First you need Alpharael to survive a turn and attack, and then if you have enough to produce the corresponding number of ability triggers (including Alpharael itself), you win. Conversely, as long as you remove it before it attacks, you will not be affected by the ability (of course, having to discard a card at random because of ward is still quite annoying). This is a very interesting card, but its strength in Limited still needs to be verified.

## Conclusion

Many of the black cards support the Void theme, and relying on removal is still a very reliable game plan. There are also quite a few individual cards that can earn card advantage, as well as powerful bombs, and we believe that in Limited, black will be a very solid main or secondary color!
