## Before We Start

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Vehicle/16fddb353ab4.webp)

When reading [this set's Standard preliminary look](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/DFT-Standard-Preliminary/), did you feel something was missing? Now that we're back on Kaladesh/Avishkar, how could there not be a red-white-based Vehicle deck? In [Aetherdrift](https://scryfall.com/sets/dft?as=grid&order=set) we have neither [Smuggler's Copter](https://scryfall.com/card/kld/235/smugglers-copter) nor [Heart of Kiran](https://scryfall.com/card/aer/153/heart-of-kiran), and the Pilot structure is quite different too, so how should we build it?

## Card Analysis

First, let's look at the most representative legend, [Kolodin, Triumph Caster](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/210/kolodin-triumph-caster):

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Vehicle/5a914edb3b76.webp)
>Legendary Creature | Human Pilot
>Mounts and Vehicles you control have haste.
 >Whenever a Mount you control enters, it becomes saddled until end of turn.
 >Whenever a Vehicle you control enters, it becomes an artifact creature until end of turn.

[Kolodin](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/210/kolodin-triumph-caster) lets every Vehicle throw a punch the turn it enters without being crewed, and has excellent synergy with Vehicles with good bodies, but he has a serious flaw: only 2 power, and no Pilot ability to help crew the big ones. Mid- to large-sized Vehicles mostly need power 3 to crew, which gives [Kolodin](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/210/kolodin-triumph-caster) the risk of being unable to crew when cast behind a Vehicle.
So should we settle for mid- to small-sized Vehicles?
Like [Voyager Glidecar](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/36/voyager-glidecar), [Skyseer's Chariot](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/28/skyseers-chariot), or [Gastal Thrillroller](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/129/gastal-thrillroller)?

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Vehicle/e011b7f3b05b.webp)

That level of damage is no better than just attacking with the Pilot.
So I still built the deck around Vehicles with better bodies. The decklist is as follows:

[Decklist](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/6940642#paper)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Vehicle/b11afeef4dbb.webp)

The Vehicle lineup is [Lifecraft Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/234/lifecraft-engine), [Cultivator's Caravan](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/670/cultivators-caravan), and [Magmatic Galleon](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/157/magmatic-galleon). The first two are strong turn-3 damage sources when [Kolodin](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/210/kolodin-triumph-caster) is on the battlefield, but both need Crew 3, so what to do? The answer is to choose Pilot as [Lifecraft Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/234/lifecraft-engine)'s creature type, giving [Kolodin](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/210/kolodin-triumph-caster) +1/+1. In other words, **this deck is also a Pilot tribal deck**, and the only one that might fail to crew is [Cultivator's Caravan](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/670/cultivators-caravan). But [Cultivator's Caravan](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/670/cultivators-caravan) has another use: besides being a strong 5/5 beater, its mana-rock trait lets you reach five mana more smoothly to cast [Magmatic Galleon](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/157/magmatic-galleon), and on the turn you slam it down you can still cast a one-mana creature or hold up a one-mana removal spell for your opponent. [Magmatic Galleon](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/157/magmatic-galleon) is a strong beater that brings a removal spell on entering, and its passive ability gives you [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tlci/18/treasure) tokens when you deal excess damage, which can lubricate your tempo or become card-draw fuel.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Vehicle/6113531da96e.webp)

Since I've said above that this is a Pilot tribal deck, the creatures are naturally picked from Pilots. Besides [Kolodin](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/210/kolodin-triumph-caster), the other Pilots are [Dynamite Diver](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/123/dynamite-diver), [Canyon Vaulter](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/8/canyon-vaulter), and [Cloudspire Coordinator](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/196/cloudspire-coordinator). [Dynamite Diver](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/123/dynamite-diver) is a curve-filling choice; its dies-deal-1 effect is lackluster, but occasionally has a miraculous effect. [Canyon Vaulter](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/8/canyon-vaulter) can give a Vehicle trample, letting the Vehicle it crews fly over ground defenses and hit the opponent hard. The point of [Cloudspire Coordinator](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/196/cloudspire-coordinator) is scry 2 on entering, which is precious in a deck with weak card selection. In addition, its activated ability that creates [Pilot](https://scryfall.com/card/tdft/1/pilot) tokens based on the number of Vehicles that entered lets you go wide, and it interacts well with [Lifecraft Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/234/lifecraft-engine) and [Chandra, Spark Hunter](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/116/chandra-spark-hunter), making it the only two-drop Pilot played as a full playset.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Vehicle/9052758c72f3.webp)

Next is the section on our ace driver, [Chandra, Spark Hunter](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/116/chandra-spark-hunter).

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Vehicle/3626073c9b62.webp)
>Legendary Planeswalker | Chandra
>At the beginning of combat on your turn, choose up to one target Vehicle you control. Until end of turn, it becomes an artifact creature and gains haste.
>+2: You may sacrifice an artifact or discard a card. If you do, draw a card.
>0: Create a 3/2 colorless Vehicle artifact token with crew 1.
>-7: You get an emblem with "Whenever an artifact you control enters, this emblem deals 3 damage to any target."

Like [Kolodin](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/210/kolodin-triumph-caster), [Chandra, Spark Hunter](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/116/chandra-spark-hunter)'s static ability lets a Vehicle throw a punch right when it enters, and the better the Vehicle's body, the greater the benefit; pairing her with mid- to small-sized Vehicles would be a total waste. Her ability to create [Vehicle](https://scryfall.com/card/tdft/12/vehicle) tokens interacts with [Cloudspire Coordinator](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/196/cloudspire-coordinator), and sacrificing an artifact to draw pairs well with [Magmatic Galleon](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/157/magmatic-galleon)'s [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tlci/18/treasure)-making passive. Unfortunately, the legend rule makes it unsuitable to play a full set, so I added one [Alacrian Armory](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/2/alacrian-armory) as a supplement.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Vehicle/8c67c1e00484.webp)

For removal, besides the [Magmatic Galleon](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/157/magmatic-galleon) mentioned above, considering the synergy with its excess-damage passive and the deck's aggressive nature, I chose [Road Rage](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/145/road-rage), which scales damage with the number of Vehicles/Mounts, and [Lightning Helix](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/218/lightning-helix), which holds off aggro while finishing the opponent. The lands are the red-white fixing set [Inspiring Vantage](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/269/inspiring-vantage), [Battlefield Forge](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/271/battlefield-forge), [Sunbillow Verge](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/264/sunbillow-verge), and the creature land [Restless Bivouac](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/257/restless-bivouac), which doubles as a Pilot, plus one [Fountainport](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/253/fountainport) to provide a [Fish](https://scryfall.com/card/tblb/7/fish) to crew or to recur [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tblb/29/treasure) for card draw.

Game 1 shows the basic tempo: [Dynamite Diver](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/123/dynamite-diver) on turn 1, [Kolodin](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/210/kolodin-triumph-caster) on turn 2, [Lifecraft Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/234/lifecraft-engine) on turn 3, and [Lifecraft Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/234/lifecraft-engine) on turn 4, which rammed the opponent down to 1 in no time, and a [Lightning Helix](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/218/lightning-helix) finished it. Games 2 and 3 were against mono-black midrange and Esper planeswalkers; in these two games [Chandra](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/116/chandra-spark-hunter) basically drove unrivaled. It doesn't matter if the Pilots get removed: when [Chandra](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/116/chandra-spark-hunter) comes down with the [Armory](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/2/alacrian-armory), two or three 5/5 Vehicles rush out at once and ram the opponent until smoke comes out of their ears; [Liliana](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/97/liliana-of-the-veil) comes down and we ram however many she makes, driving the opponent so hard they can't take care of themselves, and we win.

## Conclusion

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Vehicle/db2fb86f8c9c.webp)
In the [Kaladesh](https://scryfall.com/sets/kld?as=grid&order=set) era, Vehicles were mostly just decorations until crewed. Although [Skysovereign, Consul Flagship](https://scryfall.com/card/kld/234/skysovereign-consul-flagship) had enters-and-attacks triggered abilities, its range was limited and it had the legend restriction, so decks were built around cheap-to-mid-cost beaters with trample. Now Vehicles have all sorts of additional abilities and have a certain degree of influence even when not crewed, and with the arrival of stronger auto-crewing cards, Vehicle strategies have changed substantially.
