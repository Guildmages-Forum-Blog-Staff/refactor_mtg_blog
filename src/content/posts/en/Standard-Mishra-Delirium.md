## Before We Start

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mishra-Delirium/4163a5cf92f1.webp)

The climax of [The Brothers' War](https://scryfall.com/sets/bro?as=grid&order=set) story is the three-way all-out war at [Argoth, Sanctum of Nature](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/256a/argoth-sanctum-of-nature). The leaders of the three factions, who are also the set's three melding giants, [Urza, Lord Protector](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/225/urza-lord-protector), [Mishra, Claimed by Gix](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/216/mishra-claimed-by-gix), and [Titania, Voice of Gaea](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/193/titania-voice-of-gaea), have already seen two of them find jobs in this site's Standard deck articles (see [Jeskai Meld](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/Standard-Jeskai-Mechtitan-Core/) and [Golgari Selfmill](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/Standard-Golgari-Selfmill/)). Where will the remaining [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/216/mishra-claimed-by-gix) end up?

## Card Analysis

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mishra-Delirium/0fe5b1e60e29.webp)
>Mishra, Claimed by Gix
>Whenever you attack, each opponent loses X life and you gain X life, where X is the number of attacking creatures. If Mishra, Claimed by Gix and a creature named Phyrexian Dragon Engine are attacking, and you both own and control them, exile them, then meld them into Mishra, Lost to Phyrexia. It enters the battlefield tapped and attacking.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mishra-Delirium/5eb2a69516b6.webp)

>‧Destroy target artifact or planeswalker.
>‧Creatures you control gain menace and trample until end of turn.
>‧Creatures you don't control get -1/-1 until end of turn.
>‧Create two tapped Powerstone tokens.

If the melded [Urza, Planeswalker](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/238b/urza-planeswalker) is overwhelming control over the battlefield, then [Mishra, Lost to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/163b/mishra-lost-to-phyrexia) is absolute destructive power. Unless the gap is far too large, a successful meld almost equals winning the game. Of course, we can't only look at the power after melding. The strength before melding is the point, since a weak front side directly hurts the deck's strength. [Mishra, Claimed by Gix](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/216/mishra-claimed-by-gix) has some power, but like the other two, it is rather underwhelming, so we should turn our attention to the other half, [Phyrexian Dragon Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/163a/phyrexian-dragon-engine). A three-mana 2/2 double striker looks pretty ordinary, doesn't it? Actually, the body and double strike aren't important. What matters are the artifact type and the unearth card-draw ability. Having read this far, readers should be able to guess how to use it. That's right, pair it with the delirium of [Duskmourn](https://scryfall.com/sets/dsk?as=grid&order=set).

Delirium is the returning mechanic of [Duskmourn](https://scryfall.com/sets/dsk?as=grid&order=set), with [Fear of Missing Out](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/136/fear-of-missing-out) and [Omnivorous Flytrap](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/192/omnivorous-flytrap) as the main attackers, supported by graveyard filling or looting so the graveyard can keep four or more card types to meet the delirium condition. Once [Fear of Missing Out](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/136/fear-of-missing-out) achieves delirium it grants an additional combat phase, letting a creature you control attack one more time. [Omnivorous Flytrap](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/192/omnivorous-flytrap) quickly pumps stats, and if you reach six types it doubles the counters.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mishra-Delirium/2f6653dc4c2b.webp)

Next, let's go through the card choices by type:

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mishra-Delirium/4023b462b8f2.webp)

There are seven artifacts. Besides the three thematic [Phyrexian Dragon Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/163a/phyrexian-dragon-engine), there is a one-mana self-mill engine, [Patchwork Beastie](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/195/patchwork-beastie), that fits the delirium theme, but given how rampant artifact and enchantment hate is right now, I went with [Haywire Mite](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/199/haywire-mite) instead. [Haywire Mite](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/199/haywire-mite) can disrupt the opponent while gaining life, and if there is a target, you can proactively sacrifice it to fill a delirium type.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mishra-Delirium/d7294dd44f22.webp)

There are also seven enchantments, of which the aforementioned [Fear of Missing Out](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/136/fear-of-missing-out) takes up four, plus two of the currently hot [Overlord of the Balemurk](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/113/overlord-of-the-balemurk), which self-mills to enable delirium while recurring creatures and planeswalkers. I also added one [Valgavoth's Lair](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/271/valgavoths-lair) for color fixing and filling a type.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mishra-Delirium/1b4447d698b3.webp)

For instants I went with removal, six in total: [Torch the Tower](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/153/torch-the-tower) to fight aggro and sacrifice outlets, alongside the common creature removal [Go for the Throat](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/102/go-for-the-throat).

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mishra-Delirium/05255897da43.webp)

For sorceries I put in a full set of [Say Its Name](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/197/say-its-name). Compared with casting [Overlord of the Balemurk](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/113/overlord-of-the-balemurk) with impending, [Say Its Name](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/197/say-its-name) can recur lands and is better in the early and midgame, and if you collect three copies you can also summon the 9/9 trampler [Altanak, the Thrice-Called](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/166/altanak-the-thrice-called), so it's a safe inclusion.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mishra-Delirium/c05104c2efbd.webp)

For the remaining six slots I put in two copies of the planeswalker [Liliana of the Veil](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/97/liliana-of-the-veil), along with four Battles, [Invasion of Mercadia](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/147/invasion-of-mercadia-kyren-flamewright) and [Invasion of Innistrad](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/115/invasion-of-innistrad-deluge-of-the-dead). The strength of [Liliana of the Veil](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/97/liliana-of-the-veil) is well known, so I won't elaborate. [Invasion of Mercadia](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/147/invasion-of-mercadia-kyren-flamewright) is early-game looting that helps fill types, while [Invasion of Innistrad](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/115/invasion-of-innistrad-deluge-of-the-dead) is powerful removal that cleanly kills indestructible creatures and creatures with death-triggered abilities that check their size. Both Battles have decisive combat power once flipped, and they are themselves the seventh type, so even if they get put into the graveyard they are very helpful.

## Gameplay Demonstration

{%youtube ugKLoNKA5_c %}

First up is the match against Dimir [Reenact the Crime](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/70/reenact-the-crime). This deck uses [Collector's Vault](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/244/collectors-vault) to accelerate and fill the graveyard, then uses graveyard-digging spells like [Reenact the Crime](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/70/reenact-the-crime) to slam its finisher onto the battlefield. In this game both sides were building up their own charge. Early on, my [Haywire Mite](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/199/haywire-mite) destroyed their [Collector's Vault](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/244/collectors-vault) to slow the opponent's looting (though they immediately played a second one). Then the opponent used a [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/twoe/14/treasure) to hard-cast [Atraxa, Grand Unifier](https://scryfall.com/card/one/196/atraxa-grand-unifier) and refill their hand, but I removed it with [Liliana of the Veil](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/97/liliana-of-the-veil). At that point my graveyard had six types, so the [Omnivorous Flytrap](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/192/omnivorous-flytrap) I played next doubled its counters, hitting the opponent hard and breaking through [Invasion of Mercadia](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/147/invasion-of-mercadia-kyren-flamewright) along the way, and on the next turn I unearthed [Phyrexian Dragon Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/163a/phyrexian-dragon-engine) to meld. Although the opponent staged a wild comeback with two [Reenact the Crime](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/70/reenact-the-crime), instantly building a defensive line of three flagship creatures, the whole team gained menace and trample from [Mishra, Lost to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/163b/mishra-lost-to-phyrexia) and was still knocked out in one swing.

{%youtube C_PQ1zzuSpk %}

Game two was against mono-black discard. Although the opponent used [Go for the Throat](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/102/go-for-the-throat) on turn four to stop me and avoid facing a [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/216/mishra-claimed-by-gix) meld on turn five, I had plenty of graveyard types, and just by having [Omnivorous Flytrap](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/192/omnivorous-flytrap) enter, I made a 6/6 double-striking [Dragon Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/163a/phyrexian-dragon-engine), forcing the opponent to wipe the board with [Rankle's Prank](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/102/rankles-prank). Then [Dragon Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/163a/phyrexian-dragon-engine) unearthed to quickly refill my discarded hand, and with the extra combat phases from [Fear of Missing Out](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/136/fear-of-missing-out), the [Bloodletter of Aclazotz](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/92/bloodletter-of-aclazotz) the opponent played couldn't stop the assault at all. After the second [Omnivorous Flytrap](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/192/omnivorous-flytrap) entered to make a 6/7 [Fear of Missing Out](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/136/fear-of-missing-out), the opponent's [Bloodletter](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/92/bloodletter-of-aclazotz) was forced to chump, and at 3 life they conceded.

{%youtube Srw-IOZl5Sk %}

Game three was against mono-white tokens. Under the interference of a [Haywire Mite](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/199/haywire-mite) that kept crawling back, the opponent was soon dismantled to the point of not being able to function. Although I made one misplay, forgetting to sacrifice the [Dragon Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/163a/phyrexian-dragon-engine) to bargain, so it was exiled for nothing, it didn't affect the outcome. In the end, the opponent threw in the towel on the very turn [Mishra, Claimed by Gix](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/216/mishra-claimed-by-gix) came down.


## Conclusion

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Mishra-Delirium/fb2f04a582b7.webp)

The unearth card draw of [Phyrexian Dragon Engine](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/163a/phyrexian-dragon-engine) interacts well with the delirium mechanic. It lies in the graveyard to fill types in normal times, unearths to draw three when your hand runs dry, and if [Mishra, Claimed by Gix](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/216/mishra-claimed-by-gix) is on the battlefield, there is a chance to meld for one earth-shattering punch. Beyond that, the delirium of [Duskmourn](https://scryfall.com/sets/dsk?as=grid&order=set) came out just right: strong enough, with core components that are cheap, built on low-rarity cards, and with plenty of room to adjust to your own preferences. It is a great fit for beginners who want to try a midrange deck.
