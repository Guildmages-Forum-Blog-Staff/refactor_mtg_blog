### Temur Rhinos

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Modern-Meta-Inspection-PostMKM/13646bafce87.webp)

As mentioned in the last meta inspection, Rhinos seems to be one of the decks that will add surveil lands to the build. After all, the Rhinos usually have little to do in the first two turns, so it is quite flexible to use a surveil land to filter the top of the library.

### Living End

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Modern-Meta-Inspection-PostMKM/445dc6659cff.webp)

Although it is also a cascade deck, Living End needs to cycle creatures early on, so it does not run surveil lands.
This list is a bit unusual: it runs four [Living End](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/121/living-end). Living End decks in the current format usually run only three to avoid drawing too many (after all, unlike [Crashing Footfalls](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/160/crashing-footfalls), Living End in hand does not have a very cheap suspend cost).

### 5C Creativity

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Modern-Meta-Inspection-PostMKM/1d7fa2508993.webp)

A Creativity deck that barely plays blue. It even swapped [Spell Pierce](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/63/spell-pierce) for [Mana Tithe](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/26/mana-tithe), probably so that when it fetches a [Sacred Foundry](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/285/sacred-foundry) on turn 1 for [Prismatic Ending](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/25/prismatic-ending), it is guaranteed to have a Mana Tithe to use (after all, the opening hand may not have multiple fetch lands).
The blue that remains is just the main deck's four [Teferi, Time Raveler](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/232/teferi-time-raveler), one [Prismari Command](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/279/prismari-command), and [Flusterstorm](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/55/flusterstorm) in the sideboard.
It includes one red-black surveil land. On turn 1 you will usually fetch a Naya tri-land or a Grixis tri-land, and black is not as important as white, so the Rakdos shock land slot was swapped for the surveil land.

### Domain Rhinos

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Modern-Meta-Inspection-PostMKM/af1a351e0cb9.webp)

You could call it the breakout star of this RC (?). If I remember correctly, Luis Scott-Vargas was also playing this deck.
The key card is the five-color Leyline [Leyline of the Guildpact](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/217/leyline-of-the-guildpact), and it is probably one of the few Leylines you actually want to cast from hand (provided, of course, you have [Scion of Draco](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/234/scion-of-draco) on the battlefield or in hand). Besides giving every land every basic land type and making all nonland permanents five-colored, it also lets [Scion of Draco](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/234/scion-of-draco) reliably come down on turn 2.
That alone might not sound like much, but don't forget that [Scion of Draco](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/234/scion-of-draco) gives creatures of the matching colors extra abilities, and being five-colored means you get all five abilities.
In other words, every creature has vigilance, hexproof, first strike, lifelink, and trample. Turn 2 [Scion of Draco](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/234/scion-of-draco), turn 3 cascade into Rhinos, and the board is a wall of first-strike lifelink creatures with 12 power. This game is so hard...
It also chooses to run [Drannith Magistrate](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/11/drannith-magistrate) to fight the cascade mirror. With a hexproof [Drannith Magistrate](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/11/drannith-magistrate) plus [Scion of Draco](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/234/scion-of-draco), you will basically win just by stonewalling the opponent.

### 4C Omnath

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Modern-Meta-Inspection-PostMKM/bea5d423d593.webp)

If I remember correctly, this 4C Omnath list went undefeated on day 1. It runs two main-deck [Chalice of the Void](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/222/chalice-of-the-void), setting it to 0 against cascade matchups, or to 1 against Murktide and Burn, which works quite well.
As a counter-measure, the deck runs no [Lightning Bolt](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/141/lightning-bolt) or [Delighted Halfling](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/158/delighted-halfling), and it can also add [Kaheera, the Orphanguard](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/224/kaheera-the-orphanguard) as a companion, as fodder for [Solitude](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/32/solitude) or to add to the board.

Let's look at the champion's deck first. It runs an extra [Incubation // Incongruity](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/197/incubation-incongruity) in the main deck, which is quite interesting: it smooths draws and can also serve as removal.
The sideboard does not carry many answers; instead it has plenty of haters (for example [Bonecrusher Giant](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/781/bonecrusher-giant-stomp), [Leyline of the Void](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/107/leyline-of-the-void), [Brotherhood's End](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/128/brotherhoods-end)).
The idea is probably to find the necessary answers with aggressive mulligans, especially since there are not that many graveyard haters around right now, and some matchups do not necessarily need those cards.

The other list is quite standard, with everything it should have.

The other two players also chose to run four [Living End](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/121/living-end), and neither prepared a black mana source, so they do not consider the situation where they need to hard-cast [Grief](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/87/grief) at all (when you can hard-cast [Grief](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/87/grief), you are probably about to lose anyway...).

### Goryo Reanimate

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Modern-Meta-Inspection-PostMKM/ef3b52294ec7.webp)

It has been a long time since I saw [Goryo's Vengeance](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/99/goryos-vengeance) show up in the meta.
This is basically a reanimator deck: after dumping powerful legendary creatures like [Atraxa, Grand Unifier](https://scryfall.com/card/one/196/atraxa-grand-unifier) and [Griselbrand](https://scryfall.com/card/mm3/72/griselbrand) into the graveyard, it uses [Goryo's Vengeance](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/99/goryos-vengeance) to bring them back onto the battlefield. They gain haste so you can get one attack in, but they are exiled at end of turn. Still, both creatures have powerful card draw, so if you find a blink card ([Ephemerate](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/7/ephemerate) or [Touch the Spirit Realm](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/40/touch-the-spirit-realm)), you can exile the creature and put it back onto the battlefield to dodge that drawback.
Also, since [Goryo's Vengeance](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/99/goryos-vengeance) is an instant and both blink spells can also be used at instant speed, you can wait until the opponent attacks or until the end of the opponent's turn to bring them back.

### Azorius Control (Kaheera)

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Modern-Meta-Inspection-PostMKM/301bb92b91a0.webp)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Modern-Meta-Inspection-PostMKM/bff86df28d04.webp)

The two players' builds differ slightly, but you can see roughly the same system: [Narset, Parter of Veils](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/853/narset-parter-of-veils) + [Day's Undoing](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/85/days-undoing) shuffles the opponent's graveyard and hand back into the library while you draw seven and the opponent can only draw one (any card after the first is stopped by [Narset, Parter of Veils](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/853/narset-parter-of-veils)).
If you have activated the [+1] ability of [Teferi, Time Raveler](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/232/teferi-time-raveler), you can even cast [Day's Undoing](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/85/days-undoing) during the opponent's draw step. At that point the opponent has already drawn a card for the turn (the draw step card), so they draw nothing, and since it is still the draw step, [Teferi, Time Raveler](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/232/teferi-time-raveler) stops them from responding with any spell, so by the main phase they have no cards left to use.
Alternatively, after emptying the opponent's hand, you can activate [Geier Reach Sanitarium](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/335/geier-reach-sanitarium) during the draw step, so again they cannot draw a second card and can only discard one.
Both players also chose not to run [Counterspell](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/81/counterspell) and instead run [Reprieve](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/26/reprieve), which can dodge some uncounterable spells and filter an extra card, at the cost of having to face that spell again next turn. But considering how many cascade decks are in the format, any card that must be suspended to be cast means you don't need to worry about facing it again. Also, compared with traditional control, this deck can pressure resources earlier: after stopping a spell with [Reprieve](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/26/reprieve), you may get to shuffle it away next turn with [Day's Undoing](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/85/days-undoing), so the impact is smaller.
To adapt to the format, they also moved some of the [Supreme Verdict](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/211/supreme-verdict), which usually sit in the main deck, to the sideboard, while putting four [Subtlety](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/67/subtlety) in the main deck.

The sideboards are similar too, both with [Veil of Summer](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/198/veil-of-summer) to dodge discard spells and the like, and with [Declaration of Naught](https://scryfall.com/card/mor/29/declaration-of-naught) and [Curse of Silence
](https://scryfall.com/card/mor/29/declaration-of-naught) respectively, aimed at decks that rely heavily on specific cards (such as [Crashing Footfalls](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/160/crashing-footfalls) or [Living End](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/121/living-end)). Very pointed.

## Other Decklist

Besides Domain Rhinos, here are a few more interesting or promising lists I happened to come across.

### Rakdos Scam w/ Money Toolbox

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Modern-Meta-Inspection-PostMKM/b5157965515e.webp)
[Decklist](https://melee.gg/Decklist/View/363249)

Let's start with another take where it's "I want it all."
There used to be a Rakdos Money Toolbox deck. Builds varied a lot, but they all had in common being expensive (?), packing high-value cards, and a [Beseech the Mirror](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/82/beseech-the-mirror) toolbox package, for example a Reanimator build mixing in [Archon of Cruelty](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/75/archon-of-cruelty) and [Persist](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/96/persist), plus some spells costing four or less as toolbox pieces.

But they also shared another trait: they were slow. For the first three or four turns there was basically no pressure, and they could only stall the board with some removal or discard.

This Scam list mixes in an [Urza's Saga](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/259/urzas-saga) toolbox and a bit of a [Beseech the Mirror](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/82/beseech-the-mirror) toolbox. Both are small systems that take up little space, shoring up Scam's tendency to run out of gas.
Personally, though, I don't really like the sideboard design. Maybe [Alpine Moon](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/128/alpine-moon) would be better than [Magus of the Moon](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/175/magus-of-the-moon), at least it wouldn't be awkward when you draw your own [Urza's Saga](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/259/urzas-saga), though the downside is that it is not as good against Tron.

### Amulet of Titan w/ Worldsoul's Rage

I forgot which RC I saw this deck at and could not find the original source.
Broadly, though, you can think of it as using the new MKM card [Worldsoul's Rage](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/244/worldsouls-rage) as an additional way to win.
Basically it combines [Mirrorpool](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/1010/mirrorpool), any bounce land, and **two** [Amulet of Vigor](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/121/amulet-of-vigor).

![](/images/Worldsouls_rage_combo.jpg)

First, use [Primeval Titan](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/183/primeval-titan) or any other land-fetching method to get [Mirrorpool](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/1010/mirrorpool) onto the battlefield with **two** [Amulet of Vigor](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/121/amulet-of-vigor) out. Then cast [Worldsoul's Rage](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/244/worldsouls-rage) with X = 2 or more, hold priority, and in response activate [Mirrorpool](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/1010/mirrorpool)'s ability, sacrificing it to copy [Worldsoul's Rage](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/244/worldsouls-rage) on the stack. When the copy resolves, put [Mirrorpool](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/1010/mirrorpool) from the graveyard and any bounce land from hand or graveyard onto the battlefield tapped together. The lands get untapped twice by [Amulet of Vigor](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/121/amulet-of-vigor): the first time, tap [Mirrorpool](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/1010/mirrorpool) for colorless mana and return the bounce land to hand, and again hold priority, sacrifice [Mirrorpool](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/1010/mirrorpool) to copy [Worldsoul's Rage](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/244/worldsouls-rage), and repeat the above. Infinite burn kills the opponent.

Some people are also already developing new [Scapeshift](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/201/scapeshift) or Titan Shift builds that use a similar win condition as an extra way to finish the game, which wins even if the opponent locks down [Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle](https://scryfall.com/card/zen/228/valakut-the-molten-pinnacle) with [Alpine Moon](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/128/alpine-moon).

## Conclusion

I have to say the format is really sick right now, with cascade everywhere.
Domain Rhinos especially: just don't be an idiot, open with [Leyline of the Guildpact](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/217/leyline-of-the-guildpact), turn 2 [Scion of Draco](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/234/scion-of-draco), turn 3 cascade into two Rhinos, and you push and win with zero effort.
When Scam was everywhere, I could at least write that turn-1 Scam isn't actually that easy and try to whitewash it a bit. Now Domain Rhinos is basically writing its rules text with its feet = =
Honestly, compared with today's Domain Rhinos, I'd rather play in the [Fury](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/126/fury) & [Up the Beanstalk](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/195/up-the-beanstalk) era; at least [Fury](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/126/fury) scam had a chance of being answered without a sweeper.
