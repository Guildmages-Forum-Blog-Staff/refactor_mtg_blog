## Before We Start

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Equipment/d3721c3ce2f4.webp
)

The much-anticipated crossover set *FINAL FANTASY* has finally launched! Led by FFVII protagonist [Cloud](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/10/cloud-midgar-mercenary), Equipment is a major theme running through the set. I published a [Standard Equipment deck](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/Standard-Boros-Equipment-Standard/) on this site back when it first launched, and all of that deck's core pieces are still in Standard, so this is a great time to retune it! What kind of evolution can this set bring to that deck?

## Card Analysis

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Equipment/c24a45d998ad.webp)
>Cloud, Midgar Mercenary
>Legendary Creature – Human Soldier Mercenary
>When Cloud enters, search your library for an Equipment card, reveal it, put it into your hand, then shuffle.
>As long as Cloud is equipped, if an ability of Cloud or an Equipment attached to it triggers, that ability triggers an additional time.

As the most famous protagonist in the entire *FINAL FANTASY* series, the booster-pack [Cloud](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/10/cloud-midgar-mercenary) is clearly designed as a tribute to [Stoneforge Mystic](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/31/stoneforge-mystic). It also finds an Equipment on entering, but it cannot drop the Equipment directly into play like the [Mystic](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/31/stoneforge-mystic). Instead, when it is equipped, both the Equipment's and its own triggered abilities trigger an additional time. That lets it get double the effect when equipped with Equipment that has triggered abilities (such as his signature weapon [Buster Sword](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/255/buster-sword) or the ward-granting [Lavaspur Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/243/lavaspur-boots)). Besides the booster version, the Standard card pool has another [Cloud](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/552/cloud-planets-champion):

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Equipment/e29f6d0c109d.webp)
>Cloud, Planet's Champion
>Legendary Creature – Human Soldier Mercenary
>During your turn, as long as Cloud is equipped, it has double strike and indestructible.
>Equip abilities you activate that target Cloud cost {2} less to activate.

The [Cloud](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/552/cloud-planets-champion) printed in the starter deck is a powerful finisher. His static ability not only lets every Equipment with an equip cost of {2} or less be equipped for free, but once equipped he also gains double strike and indestructible. As long as the Equipment gives haste and trample, a Limit Break that takes the opponent out is easy. The decklist is as follows:

After several rounds of testing, I ultimately kept to the deck's original core concept, building around [Bladehold War-Whip](https://scryfall.com/card/one/197/bladehold-war-whip) and [Kellan, the Fae-Blooded // Birthright Boon](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/230/kellan-the-fae-blooded-birthright-boon). [Bladehold War-Whip](https://scryfall.com/card/one/197/bladehold-war-whip)'s {1} equip cost reduction is the key of keys, and makes a night-and-day difference to tempo. [Kellan, the Fae-Blooded // Birthright Boon](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/230/kellan-the-fae-blooded-birthright-boon) is the important tutor for finding key cards like [Bladehold War-Whip](https://scryfall.com/card/one/197/bladehold-war-whip). With [Cloud, Midgar Mercenary](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/10/cloud-midgar-mercenary) added, the deck has as many as seven Equipment tutors, almost guaranteeing that [Bladehold War-Whip](https://scryfall.com/card/one/197/bladehold-war-whip) shows up.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Equipment/7010317cc478.webp)

Besides [Cloud](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/10/cloud-midgar-mercenary), *FINAL FANTASY* has many other legends that interact with Equipment. I chose [Zack Fair](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/45/zack-fair), the original owner of the [Buster Sword](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/255/buster-sword), the FFIX dragoon [Freya Crescent](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/138/freya-crescent), and the captain of the Knights of Pluto, [Adelbert Steiner](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/3/adelbert-steiner). [Zack Fair](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/45/zack-fair) is a one-mana creature that gives a teammate one-time protection. Because he hands over one Equipment attached to him along with it to the protected creature, you can equip him and use him as a threat. [Freya Crescent](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/138/freya-crescent) is a one-mana mana dork dedicated to Equipment, helping you land [Bladehold War-Whip](https://scryfall.com/card/one/197/bladehold-war-whip) quickly on turn two; on your own turn she also has the dragoon's signature flying passive, which you can use as a source of evasive damage. [Adelbert Steiner](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/3/adelbert-steiner) is a powerful life-gain engine whose size grows with the number of Equipment you control, the best partner when trading blows with aggro decks.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Equipment/90c76169dc71.webp)

For other Equipment, I chose [Cloud](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/10/cloud-midgar-mercenary)'s signature weapon [Buster Sword](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/255/buster-sword), the blue-black sword [Sword of Once and Future](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/265/sword-of-once-and-future) that can cast instants and sorceries (and Adventures) from the graveyard for free, the one-mana haste boots [Lavaspur Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/243/lavaspur-boots), the removal that can be tutored up, [Chainsaw](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/128/chainsaw), plus [The Irencrag](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/248/the-irencrag) with the Everflame sword stuck in it, and [Cragflame](https://scryfall.com/card/tblb/26/cragflame), the signature weapon of the Bloomburrow story protagonist [Mabel, Heir to Cragflame](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/224/mabel-heir-to-cragflame).

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Equipment/a03cdcb08c4b.webp)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Equipment/3fefa8358b0c.webp)

For removal, considering the deck's aggressive nature and the casting restriction on [Sword of Once and Future](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/265/sword-of-once-and-future), I dropped the Equipment-specific removal [Rebel Salvo](https://scryfall.com/card/one/144/rebel-salvo) and switched to the face-capable [Burst Lightning](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/192/burst-lightning) and [Lightning Helix](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/218/lightning-helix). Finally I added the all-purpose Aura [Sheltered by Ghosts](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/30/sheltered-by-ghosts), which can be tutored out by [Kellan](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/230/kellan-the-fae-blooded-birthright-boon).

## Gameplay Demo

The combination of [Cloud, Midgar Mercenary](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/552/cloud-planets-champion) and [Kellan, the Fae-Blooded // Birthright Boon](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/230/kellan-the-fae-blooded-birthright-boon) gives the deck excellent consistency. With nothing else to do, fetch [Bladehold War-Whip](https://scryfall.com/card/one/197/bladehold-war-whip) first; once you have the [War-Whip](https://scryfall.com/card/one/197/bladehold-war-whip), fetch [Lavaspur Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/243/lavaspur-boots); if you need removal, fetch [Chainsaw](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/128/chainsaw); once the board comes together, fetch a sword and put your opponent out of their misery. Compared with the early version, both the power and the satisfying feeling have gone up a level.


## Conclusion

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Boros-Equipment/5bc38ba5b308.webp)

The unfortunate part is that this deck has only about two more months of shelf life. After the September Standard rotation, [Bladehold War-Whip](https://scryfall.com/card/one/197/bladehold-war-whip) will be gone. As mentioned above, whether you have that one-mana equip reduction makes a world of difference, and it does not look like the next few sets will bring a replacement either. Players who want to try it had better act quickly!
