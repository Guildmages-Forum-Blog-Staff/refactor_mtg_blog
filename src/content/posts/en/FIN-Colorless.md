FINAL FANTASY (FIN) prerelease events kick off on June 6, 2025. Want to know more about how each card rates in Limited and what to pick? Then check out this series of FIN Limited guides!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [FINAL FANTASY Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/FIN-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick; gives you a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors; gives you a huge advantage
4.0: A first-pick-quality card that fits the archetype extremely well and can be the deciding card for splashing, switching colors, or moving into a color pair
3.5: Top uncommon/common of the set or another strong card that fits the archetype well and can be the key card for moving into a color pair
3.0: An important card for the archetype, or another above-average card that you want in your final deck
2.5: Average; usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average; you generally don't want to play it, but it can fill out the deck if needed, though not too many
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable; basically never play it, since all it does is help you lose

## Conclusion

This set emphasizes the Town theme, and the new Adventure lands are also a very interesting design. In addition, the large number of Equipment gives weaker-bodied creatures with good abilities more room to survive. I boldly predict that this set will be a very varied Limited environment, so players should pay close attention to the overall synergy of their decks, and making good use of legendary creatures and Saga creatures will be the key to winning.
