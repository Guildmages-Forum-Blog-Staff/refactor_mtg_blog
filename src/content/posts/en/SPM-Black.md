Marvel's Spider-Man (SPM) will hold its prerelease events on September 19, 2025, and be officially released a week later on September 26, 2025. Want to know more about how individual cards rate in Limited, with recommendations? Then check out this series of SPM Limited introductions!

In addition, because of licensing issues, this set's physical cards offline and digital cards online (MTGA, MO) will have different card names and different card art, but the same rules text (a small number of proper nouns on some cards still differ). Therefore, this Limited introduction shows both versions of the card art together so readers can compare.

- For a mechanics breakdown, see this article: [Marvel's Spider-Man Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/SPM-Mechanism/)
- The physical card set is named Marvel's Spider-Man ([SPM](https://scryfall.com/sets/spm))
- The online set is named Through the Omenpaths (tentative translation; the English set name is Through the Omenpaths, [OM1](https://scryfall.com/sets/om1))

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional must-pick, brings a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional must-pick if in the right colors, brings a huge advantage
4.0: First-pick value, extremely synergistic with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card for splashing/switching colors or entering a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other cards of sufficient power, synergize well with the color pair's theme, can be a key card for entering a color pair
3.0: Important cards for the color pair's theme and other above-average cards, you'd want them in your final deck
2.5: Average level, usually a decent curve filler
2.0~1.0: Below average, basically leaning toward not playing; can be used as filler if necessary, but not too many
0.5~0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play it, playing it will only help you lose
