## Grading Scale

5.0: Must-pick unconditionally, brings an advantage close to victory
4.5: Must-pick unconditionally in the right color pair, brings a large advantage
4.0: First-pick quality, extremely good synergy with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card for splashing/pivoting colors or entering a color pair
3.5: The set's top uncommons and commons and other cards of sufficient power, good synergy with the color pair's theme, can be the key card for entering a color pair
3.0: Important cards for a color pair's theme and other above-average cards; you'll want them in the final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0～1.0: Below average, generally lean toward not playing it; can be used as filler when necessary, but not too many
0.5～0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play it, it will only help you lose

## Conclusion
This set's green cards all do what they should for their cost, and have no overly complicated or hard-to-understand effects; in addition, this set has no common dual lands, so fixing options are quite limited. I recommend prioritizing green's excellent spells and pairing them with land-fetching spells to avoid losing games to color screw.
