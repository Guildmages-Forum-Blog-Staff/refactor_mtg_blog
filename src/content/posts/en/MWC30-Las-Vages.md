## Pre-Tournament Preparation

Compared with last time, when I played the Pro Tour the week after Outlaws of Thunder Junction released, this time there was much more prep time (release at the end of September, tournament at the end of October). The less prep time there is, the more it favors teams with a full roster, since they can test Limited and Constructed early with proxies. For Limited, my discussion partners this time were [蹦蹦 (陳柏宏)](https://www.facebook.com/UncleJund), 小石 (石惟中), [李俊廷](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100095059533609), [cephille](https://www.facebook.com/Pignosemtgworld), zhi-mao, 曾國輝 and 洪誠駿, while for Constructed they were 蹦蹦, 小石, cephille, 曾國輝, 洪誠駿, [葉秀軍](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100063848411689) and the members of 殺隊. Everyone plays some Limited when a new set comes out, so the discussion was lively, and we quickly found the stronger color combinations (Simic, Boros, Gruul) and the weaker ones (Selesnya, Orzhov). The weekend before Worlds was Taiwan's MIT#7 Pioneer event, and I'm very grateful to the other partners who took time to help research Standard, so that during our prep we didn't fall into an echo-chamber mindset and had more people helping to watch the meta direction and the strengths and weaknesses of decks.

### Constructed Deck Choice: Orzhov Demons

蹦蹦 started from Golgari for inspiration, while I began practicing with Dimir first, and 小石 seemed to start with American Control. After BLB, Standard's spectrum was stretched very wide: there was ultra-fast Boros Convoke and Gruul Prowess, but also very slow Mono White and Domain. After DSK arrived, [Leyline of Resonance](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/143/leyline-of-resonance) and [Turn Inside Out](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/160/turn-inside-out) let the red Mice aggro decks run even more rampant. DSK's [Overlord of the Hauntwoods](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/194/overlord-of-the-hauntwoods) was also a godsend for Domain, giving it both acceleration and a finisher, and it even triggers [Up the Beanstalk](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/195/up-the-beanstalk), which is absurd.

In the first week of October we decided to play a deck with [Unstoppable Slasher](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/119/unstoppable-slasher). For one, everyone fears its combination with [Bloodletter of Aclazotz](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/92/bloodletter-of-aclazotz); for another, it isn't very afraid of the common [Go for the Throat](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/250/go-for-the-throat) and [Cut Down](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/89/cut-down) ([Anoint with Affliction](https://scryfall.com/card/one/81/anoint-with-affliction) turned out to be what everyone later discovered kills [Unstoppable Slasher](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/119/unstoppable-slasher) and [Heartfire Hero](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/138/heartfire-hero) well), and of course it isn't afraid of [Liliana of the Veil](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/97/liliana-of-the-veil)'s edict either. By chance I noticed at a Japanese Pioneer tournament that Mono Black was using [Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/118/unholy-annex-ritual-chamber) together with Pioneer's [Mutavault](https://scryfall.com/card/m14/228/mutavault) and the like, which looked very powerful, so we started from Mono Black and built a lesser version of the Demon deck.

After only a few days we felt Mono Black's sideboard wasn't comprehensive enough. [Withering Torment](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/124/withering-torment) can only answer two types, and although Standard has many enchantments and few artifacts, [Urabrask's Forge](https://scryfall.com/card/one/153/urabrasks-forge) is easy to die to if left unchecked, so we figured we should go back to splashing green or white. The problem with green is that there are too many strong cards (huh?). Never mind the other green cards, playing Golgari means you will certainly include [Mosswood Dreadknight // Dread Whispers](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/231/mosswood-dreadknight-dread-whispers) and [Glissa Sunslayer](https://scryfall.com/card/one/202/glissa-sunslayer), and the whole deck structure would change.

In the end we chose to add white, with [Temporary Lockdown](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/36/temporary-lockdown), [Rest in Peace](https://scryfall.com/card/big/4/rest-in-peace), and [Beza, the Bounding Spring](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/2/beza-the-bounding-spring) in the sideboard to handle various unfavorable matchups. The new Dimir, although it looks cool with attacking and drawing at once, can't gain life and has too little room for error. In the first two turns, a single big hit from the Mice aggro decks leaves it helpless, and it can't answer artifacts and enchantments either, you can only rely on counterspells, discard and bounce. We have terribly short arms, unlike the foreign pros with their long reach, so we have to accept our fate.

#### Main Deck

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MWC30-Las-Vages/7ff5c3672265.webp)

Starting with the maindeck: [Archfiend of the Dross](https://scryfall.com/card/one/82/archfiend-of-the-dross) and [Unstoppable Slasher](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/119/unstoppable-slasher) are maxed out. I tested [Beza](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/2/beza-the-bounding-spring) in the main or a fourth [Bloodletter](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/92/bloodletter-of-aclazotz), but they're not as versatile as two copies of [Sheoldred, the Apocalypse](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/107/sheoldred-the-apocalypse). [Preacher of the Schism](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/113/preacher-of-the-schism), once very strong, now doesn't win as fast as Slasher. If I want card advantage, I'd use [Cruelclaw's Heist](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/88/cruelclaws-heist) rather than slowly drawing with the Preacher, let alone that the slow-draw engine has now become [Unholy Annex](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/118/unholy-annex-ritual-chamber).

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MWC30-Las-Vages/9493a54ef1b1.webp)

For the [comparison of Cut Down and Anoint with Affliction](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/Standard-Structure-Evolution/), the GF site already has an analysis, so I won't repeat it. The deck has many one-ofs. I mentioned this at the earlier Seattle PT, and recently [michaelchen also talked about it at MIT#7](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/14eeNaQDEX/). It has a lot to do with Open Decklists: for many cards with similar effects, I'd rather split them up and make the opponent guess, and some similar cards won't be all in or all out. For example, against Mono White I'd bring in [Go for the Throat](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/250/go-for-the-throat) but want to keep [Anoint with Affliction](https://scryfall.com/card/one/81/anoint-with-affliction), or against black Slasher decks I'd want [Ghost Vacuum](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/248/ghost-vacuum), and against Azorius Oculus I put in all four graveyard hate cards, because if I don't draw them I won't win.

While climbing the ladder we found the maindeck [Cruelclaw's Heist](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/88/cruelclaws-heist) very good. Against midrange/control we always choose Gift to gain the card-quantity advantage, and against aggro we don't Gift and just remove the biggest threat (per the World Championship deck/single-card statistics, there were five maindeck copies total: 3 mine, 2 蹦蹦's). The three-mana sorcery [Legions to Ashes](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/215/legions-to-ashes), although clunky, is also necessary, and it happens to kill a whole swarm of prolific Mice at once. The sideboard originally had a fourth copy, which I changed to [The End](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/87/the-end) as an instant-speed answer to anything. It's still seven pieces of one- or two-mana spot removal. I know some players still prefer three [Cut Down](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/89/cut-down) plus four Go for the Throat, and that's all open to discussion with no absolute right or wrong, but I'd still recommend having at least [Nowhere to Run](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/111/nowhere-to-run) and [Get Lost](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/14/get-lost) to be more comprehensive.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MWC30-Las-Vages/8cb6cf4e5112.webp)

For a big tournament, 26 lands: I'd rather draw two extra than get stuck for two turns. If I had to say where Orzhov really loses to Golgari and Dimir, it's that the manlands are too bad (at least give me a 4/4 lifelink), but the sideboard has many two-white cards, especially needing to cast [Temporary Lockdown](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/36/temporary-lockdown) on time on turn three, so more dual lands are still needed. At most three colorless utility lands. I tried [Escape Tunnel](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/261/escape-tunnel), which plays like [Fabled Passage](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/252/fabled-passage) ninety percent of the time but gives a chance for Slasher and Bloodletter to kill in one hit. Later I chose [Sandstorm Verge](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/263/sandstorm-verge), letting Archfiend and Bloodletter reliably finish off the opponent. I only activated it once during the tournament, but many opponents picked it up and thought about it for ages, and it felt like their attacks and defenses became a bit more conservative. Then there's [Cavern of Souls](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls). Since Standard has no strong [No More Lies](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/221/no-more-lies) / [Three Steps Ahead](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/75/three-steps-ahead) decks, at most there's [Negate](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/68/negate) and [Disdainful Stroke](https://scryfall.com/card/pcbb/2/disdainful-stroke), so in the end I chose not to play it.

Finally, there's [Blazemire Verge](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/256/blazemire-verge), which a great many people asked about. Standard doesn't have many fetch lands, and actually four or five basic lands are enough to find, so [Blazemire Verge](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/256/blazemire-verge) is, for the most part, just played as a Swamp. It has three uses, two of them related to Cruelclaw's Heist. First is Kicker: if you steal a Domain [Archangel of Wrath](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/3/archangel-of-wrath), you can kick it with black and red (I tested this on ARENA). Second is Adventure: if you steal [Imodane's Recruiter // Train Troops](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/229/imodanes-recruiter-train-troops) in game one, you can first (colorlessly) go on the adventure to make two tokens, then depending on the situation (using red) call the creature itself, so you can cast either side. The same goes for Gruul's [Picnic Ruiner // Stolen Goodies](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/232/picnic-ruiner-stolen-goodies): you can cast Stolen Goodies via the Adventure (colorlessly), or (using red) cast the 2/2 body to block, or just (colorlessly) cast it outright. The third use is confusing the opponent. In Open Decklist play, the time to look at a decklist is limited, so adding a variable for them to rack their brains over works: usually the opponent stares at it for ages and can't figure out the reason, and PROs always overthink, afraid of being tricked. In any case, once they start spiraling, they're already under my illusion (one Japanese player even said after the match that he thought I had [Torch the Tower](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/153/torch-the-tower). Buddy, it's an Open Decklist, please read the decklist properly). If I were playing Golgari, Orzhov or Dimir today, as long as the basic land count allowed it and I had Cruelclaw's Heist in the deck, I'd still put Blazemire Verge in place of some Swamps. Personally I think it would work even better outside of Open Decklist events. In the eight Standard rounds at MWC30, six opponents asked me after the match what Blazemire Verge was for, and I just said it's a Swamp~ wink~

#### Sideboard

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/MWC30-Las-Vages/c919d7384b8a.webp)

The reason to play Orzhov is the strong white sideboard cards. As I said earlier, cards with similar effects can be spread out, and you decide how many to bring in depending on the situation. For example, [Rest in Peace](https://scryfall.com/card/big/4/rest-in-peace) and [Ghost Vacuum](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/248/ghost-vacuum): against Azorius Oculus you need both, while against black Slasher decks you only need [Ghost Vacuum](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/248/ghost-vacuum). Some Orzhov sideboards on MTGGoldfish run 2 to 3 copies of [Kaya, Intangible Slayer](https://scryfall.com/card/one/205/kaya-intangible-slayer). I personally recommend having at least one [The Eternal Wanderer](https://scryfall.com/card/one/11/the-eternal-wanderer), who has built-in sweeping, single-target removal, and making soldiers for offense and defense, especially her first ability to flicker your own Archfiend of the Dross or the opponent's [Urabrask's Forge](https://scryfall.com/card/one/153/urabrasks-forge).

#### R7 [MASAHIDE MORIYAMA](https://melee.gg/Profile/Index/SakeIzumo) Gruul Prowess OO  

A big name from Hareruya, ninth place at last year's Worlds, very impressive~
In both games one and two I won by killing everything and then winning with Archfiend or Sheoldred XDD That's how I put it, but in the first two turns I used discard to reduce the damage taken, guessed at the opponent's possible plays to respond accordingly, or BLUFFed as if I had something.

In game two the opponent was a bit too hasty. He used [Rockface Village](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/259/rockface-village) to pump [Manifold Mouse](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/143/manifold-mouse), tapped it, and attacked. I asked whether I could block now, he said yes, so I blocked with Bloodletter, and then he said it was a 2/2 with double strike.

So I called a judge. I explained to the judge that the opponent had attacked without ever mentioning Manifold Mouse's ability. After questioning, we had also reached the declare blockers step. After asking both sides for their views, the judge ruled that Manifold Mouse has two choice points: first choosing which Mouse, second choosing double strike or trample. The opponent had made neither declaration legally, so it was just a 2/2. Bloodletter survived because of that, which made a huge difference, and with Unholy Annex I finished the game in two turns.

A small mistake like this is not allowed at the professional level~ Take it as a lesson~
