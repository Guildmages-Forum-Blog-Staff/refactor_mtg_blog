## Introduction

In 2024, a long-time friend from the local scene and I planned a trip to Nagoya together, booked cheap round-trip tickets to Nagoya on Peach Aviation, and set off in early September!


## Where Nagoya's Card Stores Are and What They Offer

The densest cluster of card stores is around the Osu shopping district. Between Kamimaezu and Sakae stations on the Nagoya subway there are about twenty card stores, and nearby attractions include Osu Kannon and the Chubu Electric Power tower, among others; the next densest area is around Nagoya Station.

The biggest share of what they carry is Pokémon and Yu-Gi-Oh!, followed by One Piece, Dragon Ball, Duel Masters and WS, while Magic is only a niche. In all of Nagoya city there are only about six card stores running Magic.

## The Standard Tournament at 晴屋大須店

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Nagoya-Card-Store/779850adb45d.webp)

Before entering you have to sign up for a 晴屋 membership, and you can also collect points when you buy things; you show your barcode when registering. The [event announcement](https://www.hareruyamtg.com/ja/events/366523/detail) is styled differently from Taiwan's, with extremely thorough details and caveats, which I suppose gives consumers more protection.

It was my first time playing in Japan, and I entered that day in an exchange spirit, hoping to improve my Japanese along the way, and of course getting some Japanese-art promo cards would be even better. The alternate arts of [Saheeli, the Sun's Brilliance](https://scryfall.com/card/pwcs/2024-05/ja/saheeli-the-suns-brilliance) and [Glimpse the Core](https://scryfall.com/card/pwcs/2024-03/ja/glimpse-the-core), wait for me!

And so, here is the match report.

### Quick Tournament Notes

There were seven players that day, and the share of aggro was very high; almost everyone was holding a different kind of aggro deck.

My own decklist is [here](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/6625362#paper).

#### Round 1: 0-2 vs. Rakdos Lizards

Game one, I was on the play; the opponent laid down too many Lizards late and I couldn't win, and I also made a rookie misplay... I had just drawn [Urabrask's Forge](https://scryfall.com/card/one/153/urabrasks-forge) and happily played it, then attacked with a 1/1 token, completely forgetting the opponent had a 2/2 [Deep-Cavern Bat](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/102/deep-cavern-bat) on the board.
Game two, I was on the play; the opponent mulliganed once, and although they had a sweeper in their opening hand, the tempo didn't go smoothly and they lost the game.

(When I first sat down I told the opponent: "Nice to meet you, I'm from Taiwan." We also chatted a little after the match; I said the Taiwan environment is more diverse, and they were curious whether I had traveled to Tokyo or Kyoto, and I answered that I had already been to both.)

#### Round 2: 0-2 vs. Red Aggro

Game one, I was on the draw; I flooded a bit and didn't draw burn, and was straight up finished on turn four.
Game two, I was on the play; I flooded, and in the midgame the opponent cast three [Manifold Mouse](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/143/manifold-mouse) in a row, picked [Heartfire Hero](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/138/heartfire-hero) as the combat-phase target and just pushed me dead.

(The opponent was also on red aggro, and he shared that [Manifold Mouse](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/143/manifold-mouse) combined with [Heartfire Hero](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/138/heartfire-hero) can trigger +1/+1 counters easily.)


#### Round 3: 2-1 vs. Gruul Valiant

Game one, I was on the draw, and it went back and forth. I forgot some details, but in the end I killed the opponent with a [Heartfire Hero](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/138/heartfire-hero) holding two +1/+1 counters.
Game two, my opening hand had plenty of burn, and the opponent also played quite a few one-mana tempo creatures this game; I killed the opponent's creatures early, but made a mistake on turn four and left the opponent two creatures, and got beaten down in one big wave.
Game three, the opponent got stuck on one land for a turn, and my opening hand had [Rockface Village](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/259/rockface-village) + [Voldaren Thrillseeker](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/171/voldaren-thrillseeker), so I took the opening. After that I solo-carried with Heartfire Hero, and when the game ended [Heartfire Hero](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/138/heartfire-hero) was a 5/5 and the opponent conceded.

## Conclusion

At first I was really nervous playing in Japan; even on the main day of the MIT I wasn't that nervous.
As game after game finished, I found it was actually about the same as in Taiwan, just with a slight language barrier, and I am glad I was willing to take this step, bravely sign up for the tournament, and finish my matches.
