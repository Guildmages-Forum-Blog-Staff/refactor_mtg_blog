Foundations (FDN) will hold its prerelease events on November 8, 2024, and officially launch one week later on November 15, 2024. Want to learn more about single-card ratings and recommendations in Limited? Then check out this FDN Limited primer series!

## Rating Scale

5.0: Must-take, unconditionally; gives you a near-winning advantage
4.5: Must-take if you're in the right colors; gives you a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick, with excellent synergy with the color pair's theme; can be the deciding card for splashing, switching colors, or moving into a color pair
3.5: The best uncommons and commons in the set and other cards of sufficient strength, with good synergy with the color pair's theme; can be the key card for moving into a color pair
3.0: Important cards for a color pair's theme and other above-average cards; you'll want them in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0-1.0: Below average; you generally prefer not to play them, but they can be filler when necessary (not too many)
0.5-0.0: Unplayable; basically never play them, as they will only help you lose

## Conclusion

Looking at blue overall, it revolves around drawing your second card each turn, flying creatures, and a little bit of flash tempo. However, blue lacks big bombs that settle the game on entering, and its relatively strong cards mostly need some resources and board built up early to reach their full potential. In addition, blue creatures' bodies still lag far behind red and green, and its removal is generally inefficient, so it's reasonable to guess that blue cards in FDN Limited will still take the secondary-color route, though some interesting ideas may emerge, so let's wait and see!
