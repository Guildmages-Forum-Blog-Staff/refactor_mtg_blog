## Masterpiece Series

### _Enchanting Tales_

> Note: Masterpiece Series cards opened in draft boosters can be used in Limited.

![Blind Obedience](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/1/c/1cb0ea8e-0707-46a8-9dc6-77c17244fd7b.jpg?1692932024)

**Limited Rating: 4.0**

[Blind Obedience](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/a/8/a8254f50-b941-433d-9867-a8e5cda79b20.jpg?1561840875) was a powerful standalone card in GTC Limited and even Constructed back in the day, and it is just as strong in WOE Limited. It not only slows down or even wrecks an opponent's plan of slapping down creatures to defend, it also forces the opponent's Foods and Treasures to wait an extra turn before they can be activated. The Extort ability also lets you widen the life gap in the mid to late game when you have plenty of mana. Incidentally, life gained from Extort also interacts with the activated ability of [Will, Scion of Peace](https://magicwizards.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com/images/cards/0218_MTGWOE_Main.png), which reduces the cost of white/blue spells you cast this turn when you gain life.

![Dawn of Hope](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/d/e/de36ecaa-62e0-4f1c-b07f-837a786dc897.jpg?1692932039)

**Limited Rating: 4.0**

A permanent that makes soldiers in Limited is just great. The creatures it makes have lifelink, and whenever you gain life you can pay two mana to draw a card, which is a real bargain! That said, in my own play I still treat it as a mid to late game mana sink, and mainly rely on other sources of lifelink or life gain to draw cards and generate value.

![Grasp of Fate](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/d/9/d983b297-e341-40fa-ba9b-b9f87eb25057.jpg?1692932056)

**Limited Rating: 3.5**

In Limited it is simply a [Banishing Light](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/f/1fbc3657-cbff-4af7-872b-7e165a042096.jpg?1646194838), and a nonland catch-all answer like that is always welcome.

![Greater Auramancy](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/7/0/7088f502-662e-4083-b9b2-fdc0574592cd.jpg?1692932072)

**Limited Rating: 2.0**
**Limited Rating: 4.0 (if you have many sources of Role tokens)**

The more Role tokens or enchantments you have, the stronger it gets. Note, though, that it grants Shroud, not Hexproof, so occasionally you will be unable to target your own enchanted creature when you need to use a trick on it. The reason I gave this card a high score is that making enchanted creatures should not be hard in WOE Limited, and Shroud restricts the opponent to trading with creature combat, which is crucial in a Limited format dominated by creature battles.

![Griffin Aerie](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/b/0/b0c1cd62-aa6e-4120-ba70-1f12c8efe389.jpg?1692973532)

**Limited Rating: 2.0**
**Limited Rating: 4.5 (if you have many Foods)**

Eating a Food for 3 life before your end step each turn to make a 2/2 flier is just too strong! But if you can't gain life, don't play it.

![Intangible Virtue](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/b/b/bb1bc2f9-d02a-4515-aa07-fcf98a3d194d.jpg?1692932101)

**Limited Rating: 2.0**
**Limited Rating: 4.0 (if you have many sources of creature tokens)**

The [Glorious Anthem](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/9/7/976124db-12e8-4ad2-80f5-634fdfe2c967.jpg?1645825039) for tokens. It is strong when you can make plenty of creature tokens, but don't play it if you can't make many.

![Karmic Justice](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/4/5/45078d81-91ea-402c-ab3c-8464fc2ebb42.jpg?1692932116)

**Limited Rating: 2.5**

Creatures are usually what matters most in Limited, and if the opponent just removes your creatures, they won't face any retribution. But when you have many powerful enchantments that trouble the opponent, you can also add this card to make them even more miserable. Oh, and it can also blow up the opponent's lands!

![Knightly Valor](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/8/4/844b8b36-6c45-445c-92a3-f1058669d66a.jpg?1692932132)

**Limited Rating: 3.5**

A powerful Aura, but note that the 2/2 vigilance Knight it creates on entering the battlefield cannot be the target it enchants.

![Land Tax](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/c/b/cb8796fd-ebf4-4fe3-b785-f48c65ff8257.jpg?1692948867)

**Limited Rating: 4.0**

Landing it on turn one on the draw is outrageous... but it is less useful when topdecked late.

![Leyline of Sanctity](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/d/f/dfaae165-add6-491f-9521-60e30accc528.jpg?1692932160)

**Limited Rating: 0.5**

Leyline of Sanctity is a defensive standalone card. Bring it in from the sideboard if the opponent has many annoying spells that target you. The main deck should be left to other standalone cards that directly help you win.

> In Limited with this set, you should now and then see the amusing situation of a Leyline already on the battlefield at the start of the game.

![Phyrexian Unlife](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/f/d/fdbbcb80-bd9f-4b45-af55-c1264e73d602.jpg?1692932176)

**Limited Rating: 5.0**

When you would lose because your life total hit zero, you have to be dealt another 10 poison counters before you die. What sort of sorcery is this?

> The Phyrexians have already been defeated, and in the WOE environment there are no other sources of Toxic, Infect, or even Proliferate.

![Rest in Peace](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/4/3/43eb0e85-1c26-4d2c-bdac-6e94717f0762.jpg?1692932191)

**Limited Rating: 0.5**

Limited shouldn't really need graveyard hate, but if you want to shut off the opponent's dies triggers or enchantment-to-graveyard triggers, you could bring it in from the sideboard to mess with them.

![Smothering Tithe](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/f/6/f6c69c2f-729b-49ed-909d-57190a728e11.jpg?1692932207)

**Limited Rating: 4.0**

With this in play your Treasures will overflow...

![As Foretold](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/3/0/306937c6-0eb3-43d3-bd71-40ef7cefa9e6.jpg?1692932220)

**Limited Rating: 0.0**

I can't think of a moment when it is good in Limited, but I could be wrong, so you are welcome to slap me in the face.

![Compulsion](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/c/3/c39d78e6-fa15-43a2-8c8e-5436e82926b7.jpg?1692932235)

**Limited Rating: 3.0**

Any permanent in Limited that can reliably filter your cards deserves a 3.0 to start, but if it were just four mana to draw a card, it would be terrible.

> This card is an early reprint from the [Torment (TOR)](https://scryfall.com/sets/tor) set. Perhaps at the time there wasn't yet a strict requirement that blue mostly loots draw first then discard, and so it ended up as one of the few blue cards that discards first and then draws (nowadays most of those are on red cards).

![Copy Enchantment](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/3/6/36924d70-5ede-4eed-a7db-7bc74d3eede9.jpg?1692932249)

**Limited Rating: 3.5**

Since WOE is an enchantment-themed environment, this card is a case of "the stronger the enemy, the stronger you are." Bring it in if the opponent has an enchantment bomb, and putting it in the main deck is fine too. Still, pay attention to the timing of use; of course, if there is no target, paying three mana to make an enchantment for its enters trigger is not out of the question either.

![Curiosity](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/9/6/96056dc1-b989-4c87-8ad9-7a9dd99dd7fc.jpg?1692932264)

**Limited Rating: 3.5**

Attach it to a creature that can get through, so that you draw cards steadily by attacking. Draw a few extra cards and you pull ahead on resources.

![Forced Fruition](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/b/8/b8281681-a243-4995-8657-c3730aa67c57.jpg?1692934382)

**Limited Rating: 2.0**

Spending six mana to do nothing, and then the opponent casts a spell and draws seven cards. That trade doesn't seem very good... But if the opponent casts two more spells, they'll probably deck themselves. The timing is delicate, so it is a card that is very strong when it is strong; cast at the wrong time, it may be you who suffers.

![Fraying Sanity](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/3/0/303ecebb-cadc-465a-8ef8-7b314f971f6c.jpg?1692932293)

**Limited Rating: 5.0**

After the opponent is enchanted with this Curse, whenever they cast an instant or sorcery, have a permanent go to the graveyard, or get milled, they also have to mill an equal number of cards at the end step. Basically this card is a super disruptive piece of the Limited experience. After all, it's a completely different class of card, so if you run into it you lose eight or nine times out of ten, and if you have it, it's simple, brutal, and wins.

> Incidentally, the Curse enchantment subtype also suits Eldraine's flavor very well. I had originally thought "Wicked Slumber", the focus of this story, would be printed as a Curse card (but it wasn't, QQ, maybe because a card with the same name [already came out in MOM](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/4/140cd8bd-b74d-4cb1-b78f-8de3cbd878ff.jpg?1682203484)). My idea was this:
>
> Wicked Slumber Spell 3BB
>
> Enchantment ~ Aura/Curse
>
> Enchant player
> When Wicked Slumber Spell enters the battlefield, or at the beginning of each end step, for each player, you choose a creature, artifact, or land they control that has no awake counter on it, and put three stun counters on each of them. When the last stun counter is removed from such a permanent, you choose one:
>
> 1. Sacrifice it,
> 2. Or exile it and return it to the battlefield with an awake counter on it, or something like that.

![Hatching Plans](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/e/8/e8ebcbfb-1522-4ff5-b23a-f3ea9e08ad9b.jpg?1692932307)

**Limited Rating: 3.0**

Perfect for adding extra value!

![Intruder Alarm](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/0/c/0c7eafcf-49aa-4134-b2d8-d9804160bcca.jpg?1692948844)

**Limited Rating: 3.5**

At first glance, aggressive decks should love this card. As long as you can keep deploying creatures and the opponent can't keep up, one day you will break through their defensive line.

![Kindred Discovery](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/0/d/0db7f9e6-4f27-4743-a456-1386f24dbd5f.jpg?1692973549)

**Limited Rating: 4.0**

If you pick the right creature type, you draw a card whenever a creature of that type enters the battlefield. It is perfect for a featured tribe such as Rats or Faeries, and you can rake in a fortune.

> Note: Food is an artifact subtype, not a creature type, so it can't be chosen.

![Leyline of Anticipation](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/d/7/d789703e-81ae-48a7-8831-201ccdec5eba.jpg?1692932352)

**Limited Rating: 3.0**

Spending four mana to give all your spells flash... I'm not sure it is a good investment; but I do know that if the blue Leyline appears directly on the battlefield in your opening hand, the tempo of your whole game will be explosively good.

![Omniscience](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/a/3/a3c11195-144e-44c4-aaa9-c534037748ea.jpg?1692932367)

**Limited Rating: 0.5**

When you have ten mana to cast it, you aren't short on mana anymore, and most of the time it will undoubtedly get stuck in your hand. I'm giving it 0.5 so that everyone can try it out and tell me how it goes; Limited always has infinite possibilities.

![Rhystic Study](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/0/4/043b2d30-a40f-4d47-933b-80544512f9c2.jpg?1692932382)

**Limited Rating: 4.0**

Like the earlier tax-heavy card, you will soon have more cards in hand than your opponent. However, because it only charges a one-mana tax, most opponents will still choose not to let you draw, but once you choke the opponent's tempo you will eventually get to draw.

![Spreading Seas](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/9/a/9ad6dfe9-90b6-4582-af77-1355c950a50d.jpg?1692948954)

**Limited Rating: 0.5**

Use it if you need to deal with the opponent's two-color transforming land, or simply want to trigger an enchantment's enters effect without losing card advantage.

![Bitterblossom](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/b/d/bdf9661d-b581-4496-9004-f292d9ac03ef.jpg?1692932415)

**Limited Rating: 5.0**

Land it on turn two and you've basically won. However, when the opponent has a flier or a reach blocker and you can't remove it, the side effect of losing one life each turn will still make things a bit uncomfortable.

![Dark Tutelage](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/a/2/a2b11a54-b38c-4e22-8b6d-dfc6c037db0e.jpg?1692973512)

**Limited Rating: 3.0**

A three-mana enchantment version of [Dark Confidant](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/0/b0d88239-43dc-46b8-8d46-626e2f8f1070.jpg?1598304507). It's a great deal, but be careful not to be hurt to death; if your deck has quite a few cards costing more than five, don't play it.

![Grave Pact](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/b/7/b728cdab-e4fe-478b-b7b2-5912bd6a80a3.jpg?1692932447)

**Limited Rating: 4.0**

As long as you have slightly more creatures than the opponent and they are weak, the opponent easily ends up with no creatures; it's even better paired with this card if you have sacrifice outlets.

![Leyline of the Void](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/0/3/034bfd23-8f10-4d98-9500-47d392531c69.jpg?1692932463)

**Limited Rating: 0.5**

I give it the same evaluation and score as [Rest in Peace](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/4/3/43eb0e85-1c26-4d2c-bdac-6e94717f0762.jpg?1692932191).

![Necropotence](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/f/f/ff727bfb-8cd8-43dd-afdf-eb74a4631eac.jpg?1692948899)

**Limited Rating: 5.0**

Once this resolves you will never lose the resource war, and paying life to draw is something you decide whether to do yourself, so the side effect is controllable and relatively safe. Note that the card it exiles from the top of your library is put into your hand at the beginning of your next end step, so when you have no answer in hand but want to draw an instant that kills the opponent's creature, you need to activate it before the end of your own turn; and if the card you want to draw is a sorcery, it is the other way around: do it before the opponent's end step.

![Oppression](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/2/e/2e8ffc89-6436-4e4d-aa43-8f704b658b96.jpg?1692932499)

**Limited Rating: 4.0**

In Limited, creatures are resources, and one extra card that lets you recur from the graveyard in the mid to late game is no different from drawing an extra card every turn.

![Polluted Bonds](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/e/1/e15e0521-1abf-4426-9f31-4081f068b184.jpg?1692932533)

> Edit Sep 7 2023
> Thanks to a reader for the correction: this enchantment makes each creature deal damage equal to the damage dealt to it to its controller, so what I originally wrote about tanking the reflected damage with a bunch of high-toughness creatures was wrong; that only makes you die faster. That wraps up this Limited analysis of WOT Enchanting Tales. I hope everyone finds their own fun in the upcoming sealed and draft events!

> Looking it over, I feel most of the reprinted enchantments in Enchanting Tales (WOT) might be better suited to being added to some XX Masters set. Putting them into WOE Limited raises the power level by too much all at once. Limited is supposed to build advantage through small combos and careful combat, but in WOE Limited you might get flipped over by the opponent topdecking a single well-timed WOT card. It feels like enchantment removal has to go in the main deck, otherwise getting blown out by one of these game-swinging enchantments would be incredibly frustrating.
