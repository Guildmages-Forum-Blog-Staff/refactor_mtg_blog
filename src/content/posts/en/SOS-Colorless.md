Secrets of Strixhaven (SOS) holds its Prerelease events on April 17, 2026, and releases officially a week later on April 24, 2026. Want to know more about how each card rates and plays in Limited? Then check out this series of SOS Limited reviews!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Secrets of Strixhaven Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/SOS-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick, gives a near-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you're in the right colors, gives a huge advantage
4.0: First-pick value, plays extremely well with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card to splash/switch colors or move into a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards that fit the color pair's theme well, can be the key card for moving into a color pair
3.0: Important cards for the color pair's theme, plus other above-average cards, which you'll want in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average, generally leave it out, can be used as filler if necessary but not in large numbers
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play it, it will only help you lose

## Conclusion

Back in Strixhaven to experience an environment full of magic flying everywhere. Besides dual land support and artifact help, the Special Guests also include quite a few famous, versatile wizards. I look forward to everyone experiencing in Limited a special environment with even more instants and sorceries than ever before.
