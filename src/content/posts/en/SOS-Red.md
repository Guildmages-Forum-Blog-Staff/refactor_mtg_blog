Secrets of Strixhaven (SOS) prerelease events take place on April 17, 2026, with the official release a week later on April 24, 2026. Want to know more about individual card ratings and advice for Limited? Then check out this series of SOS Limited overviews!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Secrets of Strixhaven Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/SOS-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick, brings an almost game-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right colors, brings a huge advantage
4.0: Worth a first pick, great synergy with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card to splash/switch colors or to move into the colors
3.5: Top uncommon/common of the set and other cards of sufficient power, good synergy with the color pair's theme, a key card for moving into the colors
3.0: An important card for the color pair's theme, and other above-average cards; you will want it in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average, generally not played, can be used as filler when necessary but not in excess
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play it, it will only help you lose

## Conclusion

Like black, here is a B-tier rating table for your reference.
![Red B-tier ratings](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/SOS-Red/a42fe021e850.webp)


In this set, red looks like it works well in red-white or red-blue, but I basically think red should have a hard time running Converge.
Cards that serve multiple purposes are still very powerful, but on average the mono-red cards in this set don't seem as strong as the other colors.
