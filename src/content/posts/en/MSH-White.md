## Introduction

Long time no see! I wonder if GF's readers still recognize me xDD. I just checked, and the last time I reviewed a limited set was [FDN](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/FDN-Black/), a year and a half ago. As a longtime Marvel fan, with two IPs I love very much, MTG and Marvel, teaming up, there's no way I could miss this one, hehe.

In this set, white's character selection and positioning is basically the side of justice: the familiar S.H.I.E.L.D., members of the Defenders, and all sorts of "Captains" have a place in white.

As one of the writers on the team with more knowledge of Marvel, rather than only evaluating the strength of individual cards, this time I'll also give simple character background introductions for some key characters. I hope everyone enjoys them.

## Conclusion

 Writing this far, I feel a bit crazy; I should have a shot at a limited-review word-count award. Overall, white's creatures this time are all pretty good, and it also has three good common-uncommon removal spells, making it a color well worth investing in for limited. For readers who don't yet know Marvel well, white is the color of justice, and starting from the side of justice is definitely a safe bet. I also hope my notes on each card sparked everyone's interest! May you all enjoy fighting side by side with the heroes in the world of MTG!
