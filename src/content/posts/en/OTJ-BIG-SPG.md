Outlaws of Thunder Junction (OTJ) will hold its prerelease events on April 12, 2024, and be officially released a week later on April 19, 2024. Want to learn more about the Limited ratings of the individual cards and some potentially fun synergies? Then check out this series of OTJ Limited introductions!

For the mechanics, see this article: [OTJ Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/OTJ-mechanism/)

## Grading Scale

5.0: An unconditional must-pick
4.0: Worth a first pick
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you will really want it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick in a draft
2.0~1.0: Below average (you would rather not play it than play it)
0.5~0.0: Unplayable (basically don't play it; it will only help you lose)

## The Big Score (BIG)

14 of the 30 cards in BIG are artifacts, which fits the spirit of [Big Score](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/102/big-score) followed by coming home with [The Big Score](https://scryfall.com/sets/big) perfectly! But how powerful are all these artifact cards? Let's take a look!

![001](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/a/3/a33703bb-51c0-4d57-9d06-1148507ddc4f.jpg?1712091372)

**cephille:** 3.0

To unlock the card exiled with hideaway 5, the difficulty is probably keeping three creatures alive. As long as you have three or more creatures with at most two sharing the same power, you can activate its ability to reach the goal! Remember that if you want to interfere with the opponent's hideaway card, you have to respond to the trigger that puts the counters on it; once they're placed, it's too late.

![002](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/e/ee793ed2-7d59-4640-8868-ad486600df2c.jpg?1712089432)

**cephille:** 3.0

A reprint of [Grand Abolisher](https://scryfall.com/card/m12/19/grand-abolisher). Although the two white mana in its cost makes it harder to land on time, as long as it enters successfully it guarantees that the rest of your turn goes undisturbed. Note that its effect only affects activated abilities of artifacts, creatures, and enchantments on the battlefield; activated abilities of creature cards in other zones can't be locked. The cards in this Limited format that interact with it are [Harvester of Misery](https://scryfall.com/card/big/9/harvester-of-misery), [Colossal Rattlewurm](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/159/colossal-rattlewurm), and [Spinewoods Armadillo](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/182/spinewoods-armadillo).

![003](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/f/4/f4f2a818-9fd1-41db-967e-7d2c9b4e4c2f.jpg?1711501246)

**cephille:** 4.0

A three-mana 2/4 body is not easy to remove, and it fits very well into black-white/red-white token-making plans; if you have powerful artifacts, the ceiling of its second ability gets even higher.

![004](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/1/d108c2b1-236e-4b8d-8445-d9749ccc4fea.jpg?1712089558)

**cephille:** 0.5

A reprint of [Rest in Peace](https://scryfall.com/card/rtr/18/rest-in-peace). In Limited you don't need graveyard hate in the main deck; just swap it in from the sideboard if needed. Note that when it enters it **exiles all graveyards**, including your own.

![005](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/d/3dbb2755-97d9-492e-8697-5548160678c8.jpg?1712090122)

**cephille:** 1.5

The effect triggers far too slowly, and it only becomes a little more useful alongside Treasures, Clues, or other artifacts with sacrifice effects. Of course, the worst-case combo line is that once it lands on turn three, you can pay four mana each turn to draw a card (2+2, sacrificing it and then copying it) and repeatedly trigger the enter/leave effects of artifacts. I'll leave that to interested friends to develop.

![006](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/a/a/aaa05ad1-5cda-4edd-b6bf-562ae3e5011a.jpg?1712089292)

**cephille:** 2.0

Speak of the combo and it appears! Paired with an [Esoteric Duplicator](https://scryfall.com/card/big/5/esoteric-duplicator), you can repeatedly sacrifice itself to put out a pile of enormous Constructs. It feels like BIG was designed with a white-blue artifact axis in mind, and it can also pair with the earlier [Oltec Matterweaver](https://scryfall.com/card/big/3/oltec-matterweaver), but considering how rarely BIG gets opened, it's fine to just know about it.

![007](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/7/b74ad496-05bc-4c5a-9027-b14df9c387ab.jpg?1712090576)

**cephille:** 2.0

If you're good at making Treasures it will perform well, but it still can't escape the fate of single cards that need support to become strong. And the odds of opening BIG in Limited are still a real weakness.

![008](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/5/b/5b60a1a6-b2ca-4dc2-a6d9-eff97092079a.jpg?1712352755)

**cephille:** 2.0

Want to enjoy a burst first and then destroy it yourself, bounce it, or give it to the opponent with [Shifting Grift](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/66/shifting-grift)

![009](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/a/3/a3012af9-621d-4fae-b00d-079a89ae35fe.jpg?1712156539)

**cephille:** 4.0

A five-mana 5/4 with menace that enters and casts an [Infest](https://scryfall.com/card/ala/80/infest) that doesn't include itself; when you're stuck with it in hand you can also discard it for two mana for a single-target -2/-2, which makes it quite flexible. It isn't well suited to being played by yourself in a token-flood deck, though.

![010](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/5/158c000e-7960-4518-b034-a529622b7bf1.jpg?1712145611)

**cephille:** 4.0

A four-mana 4/3 [Ravenous Rats](https://scryfall.com/card/m13/106/ravenous-rats); afterwards, while it's on the battlefield, the first time each turn a discard happens, you investigate, including your own looting actions! You can imagine casting a [Disinformation Campaign](https://scryfall.com/card/grn/167/disinformation-campaign) for six mana that also gives you a 4/3, with no need to bounce anything and a chance to keep earning Clues. So strong!

**cephille:** 3.0

During upkeep you can choose whether to give both sides Treasures; you can use yours first, and attacking out on your turn deals extra damage. If you don't want the opponent to profit from Treasures, just don't make any yourself. But people are always greedy, and sometimes you may lose the big picture over a small gain.

**cephille:** 3.5

**cephille:** 3.0

Besides being a decent three-mana mana-fixing artifact, each turn you cast a colored spell you gain life equal to the number of colors. Considering how many multicolor cards this set has, it should help you ramp while pulling your life total back. If you open it in a more midrange deck, use it!

A shrunken, weakened version of [Umezawa's Jitte](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/163/umezawas-jitte). Of course, this Jitte with a bunch of small flyers should let you grow while attacking, every now and then make the whole family unable to block and keep attacking, and occasionally untap a land for acceleration, so happy! Facing an opponent who plays a creature on turn one and equips the Jitte on turn two and starts attacking, the game begins to tilt their way, so it's a card with a very high threat level when it comes out early! However, in builds with insufficiently sharpened decks or creatures with weak evasion, the Jitte becomes much weaker, and don't forget a Jitte with no charge counters is just a waste of equip mana!

As for whether that is actually powerful, don't ask me.

## Conclusion

BIG and SPG, which replace The List, have a third of their cards reinforcing the artifact theme, and in SPG there aren't many overly broken cards aside from [Morbid Opportunist](https://scryfall.com/card/spg/32/morbid-opportunist), or [Notion Thief](https://scryfall.com/card/spg/36/notion-thief) in extreme situations, so the cards worth watching are still the strong ones in BIG, especially the impact of the three super-strong pieces of equipment on Limited.
