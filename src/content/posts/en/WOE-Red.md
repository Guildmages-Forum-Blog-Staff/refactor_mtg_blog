## Red

![Belligerent of the Ball](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/a13fc47d8682.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.5**

A three-mana 3/3 is a reasonable body, and if you can trigger celebration, it gives a target creature you control +1/+0 and menace, which is very powerful on offense. It performs better in decks with lower curves; red-white and red-black should both be fine, while red-green may not be as good since the slightly higher costs make celebration less likely to trigger.

![Bellowing Bruiser](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/0e9c7a32a4d7.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.5**

A five-mana 4/4 with haste is a very good attacking body, and the adventure side lets you spend three mana to remove the two most annoying blockers on the turn you attack.

![Bespoke Battlegarb](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/419238a1b586.webp)

**Limited rating: 2.0**

Apart from [The Irencrag](https://magicwizards.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com/images/cards/0248_MTGWOE_Main.png) it transforms into, this is the only Equipment in the set ([Kellan](https://www.mtgct.com/card/7450), you were born at the wrong time...), and this Equipment is very ordinary, with celebration merely making equipping a bit smoother. But if your deck is mostly creatures with 3 power or less, you can still play it to add punch; if your creatures have good bodies, skip it.

![Boundary Lands Ranger](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/3804682ba59f.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.0**

A two-mana Bear fits red-green's mandatory 4-power plan. It discards first and then draws, but it's red after all, so nothing to be done; if you can trigger it reliably, you can swap your hand for something better, but if you can't, you might not need to use it.

![Charming Scoundrel](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/f1ed58c348c9.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.0**

A two-mana 1/1 body is simply too bad. Of the three-way enter ability, only putting a Wicked Role on her to make her a 2/2 with haste does much, and making the opponent lose 1 more life when it leaves might be slightly stronger; the other two options have no immediate effect on the board. Although it's a rare, the base body is below standard, and the flexibility of the abilities adds a little to the rating. Worth noting is that the second and third enter abilities can both trigger celebration.

![Cut In](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/9f8b3bf9b348.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.0**

A four-mana sorcery that deals 4 damage to a creature and makes a Young Hero Role, a practical Limited card. Remove an annoying blocker before combat, then have a small creature enchanted with the Young Hero swing in and grow; when the board is even or slightly behind, you should be able to turn the situation around.

![Edgewall Pack](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/12185c644f1a.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.0**

A four-mana 3/3 with menace that enters with a 1/1 Rat token. The Rat can't block, so the whole card leans toward offense, but it's still a decent four-mana filler.

> By the way, I find this art of a Rat riding a hound really funny, and the art style of this card is very un-Eldraine, more like the dogs happily running around [Gavony Township](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/9/a/9aa18ecb-c2f6-4dba-ae15-f4e22ab9e434.jpg?1562834429) in the Innistrad plane.

![Embereth Veteran](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/30a418cfc820.webp)

**Limited rating: 2.0 (sealed)**
**Limited rating: 3.0 (draft)**

The problems with a one-mana 2/1 are all about the same: in sealed the pace is slow, and drawing it late is a very poor topdeck, while in draft you can build toward an extreme aggro deck, so it needs different scores for different formats.

![Flick a Coin](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/137a745c1bff.webp)

**Limited rating: 2.0**

A three-mana instant that does three things; are you trying to make me call it [Coin Confluence](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/7/b/7b61c9bc-16e8-417f-99e7-8bd83d4666c5.jpg?1562706203)? But honestly, the three effects together don't affect the board much. Maybe try it in a blue-red spells deck?

![Food Fight](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/3ef8336ee1dc.webp)

**Limited rating: 4.0 (when your deck has lots of Food)**
**Limited rating: 0.0 (when your deck has little Food)**

Don't play with your food! A two-mana Enchantment that gives your Foods an extra ability to sacrifice for at least 2 damage to any target; basically the more Food, the stronger it is. However, note that mono-red has basically no sources of Food, so you'd probably play it in red-green, red-black, or black-green splashing red. Of course, since this card has no floor, if you have too few Food sources, don't play it.

![Frantic Firebolt](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/caf7fdfd8c1d.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.5**

The floor is a three-mana instant dealing 2 damage to a creature, which is usable, but as the game goes long and your graveyard accumulates more instants, sorceries, and adventure cards, it gets better and better. It's a quality removal spell worth taking many copies of, or holding back a little to answer the opponent's bigger creatures!

![Gnawing Crescendo](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/77de8965f25b.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.0**

A [Burn Bright](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/4/84ffc17a-067c-40c1-99e3-858c836eb632.jpg?1645826208) with an extra ability is still worth playing in aggressive red Limited decks; and when your nontoken creatures die, you can make an equal number of 1/1 Rats, which let you launch the next wave of attacks or serve as sacrifice fodder for other cards, which is quite nice. Basically red-black Rats needs a card like this, otherwise 1/1 Rats that can't block are really too bad.

![Goddric, Cloaked Reveler](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/6e70761871e1.webp)

**Limited rating: 4.5**

A three-mana 3/3 with haste, and celebration can turn it into a 4/4 Dragon with a built-in [firebreathing](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/0/107e54f5-26f0-4f62-b2a3-96584262b269.jpg?1562632877) ability, which is really strong. Note that its celebration ability says **this turn**, so when the opponent tries to kill it on their turn with a 3-damage spell, you can trigger celebration to make it a 4/4 and survive.

> By the way, its mannerisms remind me of VOW's [Alluring Suitor](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/0/8075878b-b673-4d0c-b335-8a37295cd8d8.jpg?1645055005); both feel a little naughty.

![Grabby Giant](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/986fe03dccfa.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.5**

First use the two-mana instant adventure to make a Treasure for a little ramp, then slam a 4/3 reach creature for four mana, which is very comfortable. On the creature side, in the mid to late game you can occasionally use its ability to sacrifice an artifact/land for three mana and draw a card, a nice resource trade when both sides have creatures lined up.

![Grand Ball Guest](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/f9a3808ad8b8.webp)

**Limited rating: 2.0 (hard to trigger celebration)**
**Limited rating: 3.0 (easy to trigger celebration)**

A two-mana 2/2 buddy; triggering celebration gives +1/+1 and trample. If you can trigger it reliably it deserves a higher score, and if not it's just ordinary filler.

> By the way, I think the creature types should add Dwarf (Drumstick Bro's inner voice: I only look short because I'm sitting down, who told you I'm a Dwarf! Your whole family are Dwarves (angry)).

![Harried Spearguard](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/9b715f6dd04f.webp)

**Limited rating: 1.0**

A one-mana 1/1 is too weak no matter what ability you give it (OK, indestructible might be an exception, but it's still weak), and a 1/1 Rat that can't block when it dies doesn't make me happy either. I won't play it.

> And the people on this card really look unhappy (hey).

![Hearth Elemental](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/e7640f8b3c37.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.5**

Unless you have lots of instants/sorceries/adventures in hand on turn two and use its two-mana adventure to discard them all and draw two, you're unlikely to land a 4/5 on turn three; but that is such a loss, please don't do that. Normally you hold it in hand until your hand runs out, then use its adventure to draw two cards and follow up with a discounted 4/5, and that is the correct way to use it.

![Imodane, the Pyrohammer](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/81e442555835.webp)

**Limited rating: 4.0**

A four-mana 4/4 is a very solid body, and while she's around, your single-target burn spells also deal equal damage to the opponent; the more burn, the stronger she is.

![Kindled Heroism](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/86e583d5790b.webp)

**Limited rating: 1.5**

Although cards with this same effect have many different names: [Coming In Hot](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/4/f/4f6eb966-67af-4b18-8899-4a99a5179aa3.jpg?1682204111), [Daring Escape](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/104/daring-escape), or [Storm Strike](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/8/d/8de24cba-545a-438b-9516-1c19a50ca78c.jpg?1584831070), they're all the same ordinary small trick. I'd only consider it in a deck with many prowess creatures.

![Korvold and the Noble Thief](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/94bef5afad54.webp)

**Limited rating: 2.0**

Chapters I and II each give a Treasure; Chapter III exiles the top three cards of your library, which you can play this turn. The resource gain is a little too slow, and Treasure has no supporting mechanic in this set, so I probably wouldn't want to play this Saga.

> So in the ELD Brawl precon, [Korvold, Fae-Cursed King](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/9/2/92ea1575-eb64-43b5-b604-c6e23054f228.jpg?1571197150) was a human cursed by the Faeries into becoming a Dragon; no wonder his legs are so long...

![Merry Bards](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/fedf0a65ca73.webp)

**Limited rating: 2.5**

> Singing and singing, hey! Our young hero makes his entrance!

A three-mana 3/2, or four mana to enter and put a Young Hero Role on a target creature you control; I think both are just fine, but if you put it on itself, it attacks next turn as a 4/3, and if it survives, hits again as a 5/4, which seems OK?

![Minecart Daredevil](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/aefc177e43ba.webp)

**Limited rating: 2.5**

A three-mana 4/2 that encourages you to attack, and the adventure side is a two-mana instant +2/+1 small trick; both sides are mediocre, and if you're behind on board this creature may not help much.

![Monstrous Rage](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/bc021c35ba0e.webp)

**Limited rating: 2.5**

A one-mana instant giving +3/+1 and trample, and leaving a Monster Role behind if the creature survives the turn is actually pretty good. Trample is strong!

![Raging Battle Mouse](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/d021099695c0.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.0**

> I believe I can fly... but actually it can't fly, it just jumps high (maybe the photographer lay on the ground and shot upward to frame it?).

A two-mana 2/1 that makes the second spell you cast each turn cost one less, and with celebration it can also give a target creature +1/+1 until end of turn. Although its standalone combat power isn't strong, the cost-reduction static ability is quite attractive. Play it on turn two and on turn three you can cast spells in three cost combinations: (0+4), (1+3), (2+2). Setting aside that (0+4) can't happen in WOE Limited, the other two combinations are both very good, and it's worth noting that thanks to adventure cards, the chance of those two combinations triggering the discount should be considerable.

![Ratcatcher Trainee](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/5a837778a793.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.0**

Five mana paid in installments to make two 1/1s and one 2/1 is flexible. The 2/1 has first strike on your own turn, which suits aggressive decks, and the adventure side can be cast at instant speed to trigger celebration, so watch for the best timing. Of course, this card is very bad when you need to defend or are behind, so on the draw or against a deck that's faster than you, you can swap it out for big-butt defensive creatures.

![Realm-Scorcher Hellkite](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/2e7525832b9a.webp)

**Limited rating: 4.5**

Simply a six-mana 4/6 flying Dragon with a pinging ability is already very strong, no kicker needed; if you do kick it, the Dragon deals 2 damage on entering, which is worth considering at the right moment, but don't force it.

![Redcap Gutter-Dweller](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/c0c52cba4aea.webp)

**Limited rating: 4.0**

A four-mana 3/3 with menace that enters with two 1/1 Rats, and during your upkeep you can eat a creature to grow and exile the top card of your library, which you can play that turn. In a red-black Rats deck it should be a great backbone creature, combining Rat-making, solo fighting, and card-advantage ability in one.

![Redcap Thief](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/c6f0d7edb1f5.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.0**

A three-mana 2/3 that makes a Treasure on entering. It fits red-green plans, jumping up to big creatures, and getting two bodies on entering can trigger celebration, so red-white is worth considering too.

![Rotisserie Elemental](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/4547c4d60bea.webp)

**Limited rating: 1.0**

A one-mana creature loaded with abilities; without pump it probably can't get through, and it's a poor late-game topdeck. And if we look closely at its abilities, it needs to hit a player to put a skewer counter, and then you still have to sacrifice it to draw cards... I think there's no need to play it. Of course, if your deck is full of prowess creatures and lots of pump spells it should work a bit better, but I think that's quite hard.

![Skewer Slinger](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/1b999a9e4158.webp)

**Limited rating: 2.0**

A two-mana 1/3 creature with reach; if you don't pump it, the opponent will probably just let it hit them. Basically I wouldn't main deck it, and I'd just swap it in from the sideboard when I'm short on creatures to block flyers.

![Song of Totentanz](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/f44c4b0755f2.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.0**

Makes a ton of hasty Rats, which is pretty strong on an empty board, but when you're behind or both sides have creatures lined up, this card can't help you much.

![Stonesplitter Bolt](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/3897963e66e8.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.5**

Flexible removal just like black's [Faerie Fencing](https://magicwizards.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com/images/cards/0090_MTGWOE_Main.png); it's worth playing without kicker, but more efficient with it.

![Tattered Ratter](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/92a58f6e5c67.webp)

**Limited rating: 2.0**
**Limited rating: 4.0 (red-black Rats only)**

It's basically a lord that gives your Rats a conditional +2/+0; if you're a red-black Rats deck you'll be happy to see it.

![Torch the Tower](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/2ddd5e4ab70c.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.0**

A one-mana instant dealing 2 is usually a good card early, and when 2 damage isn't enough late, you can kick it to deal 3. Sometimes against black decks that recur or dig up graveyard cards, you'll be very grateful for its exile ability.

![Twisted Fealty](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/aece23f8b002.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.0**

A three-mana [Act of Treason](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/8/b/8bd01aa2-dcb5-4bb5-8f61-84c0f6ffd20a.jpg?1646058587) that also gives you a Wicked Role, very good for red-black and red-white decks that need to break through the opponent.

![Two-Headed Hunter](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/b5e5afe9bad6.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.5**

A two-mana instant granting double strike is very efficient on offense, and a five-mana 5/4 with menace is also a very good body.

![Unruly Catapult](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/12547d236bc4.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.5**

A three-mana 0/4 defender is nothing special, but what if it's a pinger? It fits red-blue spells very well, blocking early creatures while continually stealing damage. It feels like in draft there's a chance to build a control-leaning deck around it and instants/sorceries, and adventure cards can also fill in the creature shortage of spells decks.

![Virtue of Courage](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/eb9899ae11dd.webp)

**Limited rating: 3.5**

On the Aura side, for five mana you need to deal noncombat damage to the opponent to start drawing cards, but it's quite flexible that you don't have to. The two-mana instant adventure dealing two is still decent, but if your deck doesn't have many other cards that can damage opponents, it's just a two-mana deal-two. By the way, the [Unruly Catapult](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/12547d236bc4.webp) I just covered together with the Enchantment side can get a 5.0.

![Witch's Mark](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/33217a2c32ca.webp)

**Limited rating: 2.5**

[Tormenting Voice](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/d/4/d4cae0e4-ed3f-4745-b11b-6419403421a2.jpg?1562794070) that also gives you a Wicked Role, pretty good.

![Witchstalker Frenzy](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/WOE-Red/93d46bfb16d5.webp)

**Limited rating: 4.0**

A four-mana instant dealing 5 can kill over 90% of creatures, and with three or more creatures attacking it can be cast for as little as one mana, which doesn't seem hard to achieve in a red-based deck. But the main point is that dealing 5 is just too good!
