Bloomburrow (BLB) holds its prerelease events on July 26, 2024, and is officially released a week later on August 2, 2024. Want to learn more about individual card ratings and recommendations for Limited? Then check out this series of BLB Limited reviews!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Bloomburrow Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/BLB-mechanism/)
- For a first look at the Limited themes, see this article: [A First Look at Bloomburrow Limited](https://guildmagesforum.tw/BLB-Limited-Preliminary/)

> The Limited review articles in this series focus on ratings. Some cards that are average or have relatively simple abilities will not get a specific review.

## Rating Scale

5.0: Unconditional first pick
4.5: Unconditional first pick if you are in the right color pair
4.0: Worth taking first
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you would really like it to make your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around a 6th or 7th pick in a draft
2.0 to 1.0: Below average (you would rather not play it than play it)
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable (basically never play it, it will only help you lose)

- [Dour Port-Mage](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/47) has few high-value enter-the-battlefield abilities to match in this set, and as a two-drop there are plenty of better options.
- [Eddymurk Crab](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/48) has flash, which gives it excellent tempo. It can enter at the end of the opponent's turn and tap two creatures, then attack on your own turn to gain an advantage. However, in Limited, with few sorceries and instants, you usually need at least 5-6 mana to cast it.
- [Eluge, the Shoreless Sea](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/49) has a heavy triple-colored requirement, and its body depends on the number of Islands, so it can only shine in mono-blue decks.
