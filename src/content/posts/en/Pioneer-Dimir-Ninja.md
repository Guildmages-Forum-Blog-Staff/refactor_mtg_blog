## Before We Start

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Dimir-Ninja/0dfb1ae736c3.webp)

I never expected that the Ninja theme that returned to Standard in Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty would keep receiving support, especially this time with Duskmourn: House of Horror, which brings us a brand-new Ninja lord, [Kaito, Bane of Nightmares](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/220/kaito-bane-of-nightmares). How will it perform in a Pioneer Ninja deck?

## Card Analysis

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Dimir-Ninja/f6769a9b75d4.webp)
>Kaito, Bane of Nightmares
>Legendary Planeswalker — Kaito
>Ninjutsu {1}{U}{B} ({1}{U}{B}, Return an unblocked attacker you control to hand: Put this card onto the battlefield from your hand tapped and attacking.)
>During your turn, as long as Kaito has one or more loyalty counters on him, he's a 3/4 Ninja creature and has hexproof.
> [+1]: You get an emblem with "Ninjas you control get +1/+1."
> [0]: Surveil 2. Then draw a card for each opponent who lost life this turn.
> [−2]: Tap target creature. Put two stun counters on it.

[Kaito, Bane of Nightmares](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/220/kaito-bane-of-nightmares) is a four-mana planeswalker that can enter via a three-mana ninjutsu. He is a powerful damage source that is hard to remove, and he also grants all Ninjas a permanent [Glorious Anthem](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/21/glorious-anthem) [emblem](https://scryfall.com/card/tdsk/17/kaito-bane-of-nightmares-emblem). For a Ninja deck that emphasizes offense, you can imagine its great destructive power. However, being legendary, he can't be copied by [Thousand-Faced Shadow](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/86/thousand-faced-shadow), which is a bit of a shame.

Anyway, here is the list for readers' reference first:

This deck is adjusted from the version I took to the Top 4 of a 士林貓 MITQ two years ago. First, it runs a full set of the trio [Thousand-Faced Shadow](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/86/thousand-faced-shadow), [Silver-Fur Master](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/236/silver-fur-master), and [Prosperous Thief](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/73/prosperous-thief), the base components for ninjutsu to achieve a turn-four kill. For details, see the [previous article](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/Standard-Dimir-Ninja/).

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Dimir-Ninja/c5fe8dec3326.webp)

The Pioneer card pool gives us a quality one-mana Rogue, [Faerie Miscreant](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/58/faerie-miscreant). The second and later copies draw a card when they enter, which fits perfectly with Ninja decks that keep hopping creatures between hand and battlefield. There is also the four-mana [Whirler Rogue](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/122/whirler-rogue), which, besides entering with two [Thopters](https://scryfall.com/card/totc/27/thopter) to expand the board and provide ninjutsu carriers, grants a target creature the ability to be unblockable, which is crucial for Ninja decks, and it works together with [Prosperous Thief](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/73/prosperous-thief). [Brazen Borrower // Petty Theft](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/39/brazen-borrower-petty-theft) is used as a versatile soft answer, and a three-mana 3/1 flash flyer is also a serviceable evasive damage source.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Dimir-Ninja/debf818012fd.webp)

As for the other Ninjas, besides [Kaito, Bane of Nightmares](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/220/kaito-bane-of-nightmares), newly added this time to take the place of [Biting-Palm Ninja](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/88/biting-palm-ninja), I also added [Moon-Circuit Hacker](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/67/moon-circuit-hacker) and [Satoru, the Infiltrator](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/230/satoru-the-infiltrator). Although [Moon-Circuit Hacker](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/67/moon-circuit-hacker) was given a poor review in the [previous article](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/Standard-Dimir-Ninja/), in the Pioneer card pool it can pair with [Faerie Miscreant](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/58/faerie-miscreant) and [Satoru, the Infiltrator](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/230/satoru-the-infiltrator) to form a highly efficient card draw loop, with far better returns than in Standard, so I tried it. Next is [Satoru, the Infiltrator](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/230/satoru-the-infiltrator) from Outlaws of Thunder Junction, a two-mana trampling Ninja with a good body, whose ability lets every creature you bring in with ninjutsu trigger a draw. Although he has the same legendary problem when paired with [Thousand-Faced Shadow](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/86/thousand-faced-shadow), his copy still triggers the enter-the-battlefield draw, so the compatibility isn't actually that bad.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Dimir-Ninja/999dd395f65d.webp)

For removal I chose the common [Thoughtseize](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/109/thoughtseize), [Fatal Push](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/93/fatal-push), and the new card from the previous set, [Feed the Cycle](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/94/feed-the-cycle). [Fatal Push](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/93/fatal-push) triggers revolt very easily in a ninjutsu deck: you can meet the revolt condition with ninjutsu, or with a [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/tneo/17/treasure) created by [Prosperous Thief](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/73/prosperous-thief). [Feed the Cycle](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/94/feed-the-cycle) is a flexible two-mana instant-speed removal spell. This deck has no graveyard recursion anyway, so exiling three cards from the graveyard to use it like [Murderous Cut](https://scryfall.com/card/ktk/81/murderous-cut) is perfectly fine, and at worst it is still a [Hero's Downfall](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/112/heros-downfall).

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Dimir-Ninja/7a2a37b5c0a4.webp)

For lands, besides running lots of dual lands that can be played early to fit the tempo, considering the late-game power an aggressive deck needs, I added four manlands. Since Ninjas depend heavily on colored mana, I didn't use the [Mutavault](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/903/mutavault) common in tribal decks, and instead used a mix of [Hall of Storm Giants](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/257/hall-of-storm-giants), [Hive of the Eye Tyrant](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/258/hive-of-the-eye-tyrant), and [Restless Reef](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/282/restless-reef).

## Gameplay Demonstration

{%youtube 6w_9VrtaSRA %}

Although my opening hand had a good aggressive combination, I was on the draw and the opponent played black, so if I got hit by a string of removal, my offensive tempo would collapse. So on my first action I used [Thoughtseize](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/109/thoughtseize) first, confirmed the opponent was Golgari Sacrifice with no removal, and decided to discard [Insidious Roots](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/208/insidious-roots) to avoid the hard-to-handle wide board.
Then both sides developed. The opponent laid out [Forsaken Miner](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/88/forsaken-miner), [Stitcher's Supplier](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/121/stitchers-supplier), [Scavenger's Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/111/scavengers-talent), and the [Cauldron Familiar](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/216/cauldron-familiar) and [Witch's Oven](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/237/witchs-oven) combo, while I had [Faerie Miscreant](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/58/faerie-miscreant), [Satoru, the Infiltrator](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/230/satoru-the-infiltrator), and [Silver-Fur Master](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/236/silver-fur-master). Undisturbed, one attack on turn four had [Satoru](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/230/satoru-the-infiltrator) draw three cards in a row, drawing [Kaito](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/220/kaito-bane-of-nightmares) straight into my hand to ninjutsu in. With a board advantage and a full hand, [Kaito](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/220/kaito-bane-of-nightmares) naturally chose to use +1 to pump the whole team, greatly compressing the time the opponent could buy with the [Cauldron Familiar](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/216/cauldron-familiar) chump wall.
The opponent of course also moved toward their goal as fast as possible, self-milling nonstop until a big creature let [Scavenger's Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/111/scavengers-talent) level up to level three to dig it back and turn the game around... if I hadn't had [Borrower](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/39/brazen-borrower-petty-theft) in hand. In response to the [Scavenger's Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/111/scavengers-talent) level up, I bounced it back to hand, and the opponent promptly conceded.


{%youtube eTOlTKCG6dc %}

Selesnya Angels is a bad matchup for Ninjas. The Angel deck's many solid flyers and powerful lifegain nearly pin down a Ninja deck that relies on small flyers to activate ninjutsu, but with [Kaito](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/220/kaito-bane-of-nightmares)'s support it's different. [Kaito](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/220/kaito-bane-of-nightmares), arriving on time on turn three, started with a [+1] to bolster stats, and together with the [Satoru, the Infiltrator](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/230/satoru-the-infiltrator) next to him, pressured the opponent. The opponent's turn-three [Righteous Valkyrie](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/24/righteous-valkyrie) and turn-four big lifegain from [Collected Company](https://scryfall.com/card/dtk/177/collected-company) were successfully disrupted by [Borrower](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/39/brazen-borrower-petty-theft), gaining only 8 life. The next turn I went further with a [-2], tapping down [Giada, Font of Hope](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/14/giada-font-of-hope) so [Faerie Miscreant](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/58/faerie-miscreant) could slip through the defenses and ninjutsu out [Silver-Fur Master](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/236/silver-fur-master), then, while at it, used the drawn [Fatal Push](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/93/fatal-push) to kill [Bishop of Wings](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/8/bishop-of-wings) and cut off their lifegain for the next turn. The opponent, at 5 life, faced the sturdy Ninja army and conceded.


{%youtube Z_QPbrdhSCA %}

Control is a somewhat favorable matchup for Ninjas. In the past, [Thoughtseize](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/109/thoughtseize) and the discard combination of [Biting-Palm Ninja](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/88/biting-palm-ninja) stripped the opponent's hand to apply pressure, but without [Thoughtseize](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/109/thoughtseize) as cover, [Biting-Palm Ninja](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/88/biting-palm-ninja) risks eating removal. In this game against Jeskai Control, the opponent holding [The Wandering Emperor](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/42/the-wandering-emperor) would hamstring Ninjas, but with [Kaito](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/220/kaito-bane-of-nightmares) you can freeload thanks to hexproof on your turn and his planeswalker status. The creature removal in hand that was otherwise useless could be used for defense and to clear the way, so I easily broke through [The Wandering Emperor](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/42/the-wandering-emperor)'s defense and started using [0] to gain resources. Before the opponent drew creature removal, their life total had already been beaten down to the lethal line, so they had to concede.


## Conclusion

As a creature-style planeswalker that can enter via ninjutsu, [Kaito, Bane of Nightmares](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/220/kaito-bane-of-nightmares) improves Ninja decks' resistance to creature removal, combining damage, path-clearing, and card draw in one. The [emblem](https://scryfall.com/card/tdsk/17/kaito-bane-of-nightmares-emblem) permanently buffing Ninjas can serve as a second way to win, and his arrival has indeed improved the matchups that were originally bad, so it may well take Dimir Ninja to new heights in the Pioneer arena.
