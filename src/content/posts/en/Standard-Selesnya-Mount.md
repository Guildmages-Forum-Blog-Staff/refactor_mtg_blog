## Before We Start

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Mount/eaa5e81adb4e.webp)

Mounts are one of the signature abilities of Outlaws of Thunder Junction. In Limited they're a real force with the boost from [Miriam, Herd Whisperer](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/221/miriam-herd-whisperer), but looking at the Mounts alone their bodies aren't especially outstanding and they need to attack to trigger their abilities. Can a Standard tribal deck really work?

## Deck Introduction

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Mount/c32b76c2b001.webp)

Take the White-Green rare Mount [Seraphic Steed](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/232/seraphic-steed) as an example. A two-mana 2/2 with first strike and lifelink that makes a 3/3 [Angel](https://scryfall.com/card/totj/2/angel) when it attacks saddled looks pretty good, but its body is really not outstanding, it doesn't gain stats when saddled, and unless you find a way to fatten it up or give it trample, it can easily go out and never come back. Although the Mount system has ways to put +1/+1 counters like [Miriam, Herd Whisperer](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/221/miriam-herd-whisperer) and [Ornery Tumblewagg](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/171/ornery-tumblewagg), the growth is rather slow, and against medium and large creatures you can only stare helplessly. In addition, Saddle 4 is no small restriction, and saddling with [Wylie Duke, Atiin Hero](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/239/wylie-duke-atiin-hero) alone is really not enough. So how do we meet that requirement? See the following example:

[Decklist](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/6406797#paper)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Mount/76adacde656b.webp)

The answer that patches [Seraphic Steed](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/232/seraphic-steed)'s flaws is the Backup ability from March of the Machine. [Doomskar Warrior](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/185/doomskar-warrior) and [Boon-Bringer Valkyrie](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/9/boon-bringer-valkyrie) simultaneously satisfy power 4, add counters, and grant trample, and come with effects that gain resources and lots of life, making them a wonderful pairing. I also picked [Archangel Elspeth](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/6/archangel-elspeth) for the planeswalker slot. Her [-2], putting counters and making Angels, lets [Seraphic Steed](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/232/seraphic-steed) truly grow wings and fly over the opponent's ground defenses, her [+1] making lifelink [Soldiers](https://scryfall.com/card/tmom/2/soldier) can stall the opponent's offense, and if she survives to her [-6] ultimate she can rebuild the board immediately.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Mount/dfae9371214a.webp)

Besides [Seraphic Steed](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/232/seraphic-steed), this deck's Mounts additionally include [Ornery Tumblewagg](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/171/ornery-tumblewagg) and [Stubborn Burrowfiend](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/184/stubborn-burrowfiend), two Mounts that can grow themselves, plus the Mount-specific Vehicle [Mobile Homestead](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/245/mobile-homestead). [Ornery Tumblewagg](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/171/ornery-tumblewagg)'s counter doubling and counter pumping interact well with the Backup creatures above and [Archangel Elspeth](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/6/archangel-elspeth), producing quite brutal output, and it's not unheard of to finish the opponent in two swings. [Mobile Homestead](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/245/mobile-homestead) has the advantage of being a two-mana Vehicle that rarely gets removed, and when attacking it can put a land from the top of your library onto the battlefield, accelerating you all the way up if things go smoothly. [Stubborn Burrowfiend](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/184/stubborn-burrowfiend) is a curve-filling choice, but since it grows based on the number of creatures in your graveyard, it can still deliver amazing hitting power in the mid to late game.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Mount/d860720f7a9d.webp)

For removal I chose [March of Otherworldly Light](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/28/march-of-otherworldly-light) and [Get Lost](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/14/get-lost). [Get Lost](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/14/get-lost) removes creatures, enchantments or planeswalkers at instant speed, and its high versatility is probably well known, so I won't go into it here. [March of Otherworldly Light](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/28/march-of-otherworldly-light) is also a highly versatile card. Although the X cost restriction makes it very hard to use against expensive cards like [Leyline Binding](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/24/leyline-binding), it's quite handy against prowess-style small aggro decks or convoke, especially when you spend one mana at instant speed to pop the opponent's [Clue](https://scryfall.com/card/tcmm/40/clue) or [Blood](https://scryfall.com/card/tvow/17/blood) and make [Gleeful Demolition](https://scryfall.com/card/one/134/gleeful-demolition) fizzle, which will certainly leave the opponent stunned. I also included two [Skrelv, Defector Mite](https://scryfall.com/card/one/33/skrelv-defector-mite) to provide protection and trample.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Mount/3acaa1391370.webp)

## Gameplay Demo

{%youtube g24GNG8YEVY %}

The first opponent was Jeskai Artifacts, built around the Gundam factory [Simulacrum Synthesizer](https://scryfall.com/card/big/6/simulacrum-synthesizer). The opponent smoothly went [Simulacrum Synthesizer](https://scryfall.com/card/big/6/simulacrum-synthesizer) on turn three and [Skitterbeam Battalion](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/165/skitterbeam-battalion) on turn four, and the board was instantly filled with three prototype [Skitterbeam Battalions](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/165/skitterbeam-battalion) and three 8/8 [Constructs](https://scryfall.com/card/tbig/3/construct). Although I had [Skrelv, Defector Mite](https://scryfall.com/card/one/33/skrelv-defector-mite), [Seraphic Steed](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/232/seraphic-steed), [Mobile Homestead](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/245/mobile-homestead) and [Wylie Duke, Atiin Hero](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/239/wylie-duke-atiin-hero), I could only stare helplessly at this board... so [Archangel Elspeth](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/6/archangel-elspeth) gracefully descended. [Archangel Elspeth](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/6/archangel-elspeth) gave [Seraphic Steed](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/232/seraphic-steed) a pair of wings, flying over the opponent's defenses to gain life and make [Angel](https://scryfall.com/card/totj/2/angel) tokens to stall the offense. Although the opponent later slammed the reach creature [Twinshot Sniper](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/168/twinshot-sniper) to try to stop [Seraphic Steed](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/232/seraphic-steed)'s attack, my [Doomskar Warrior](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/185/doomskar-warrior), a clean one-for-one, gave [Seraphic Steed](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/232/seraphic-steed) trample, and in the end it trampled over [Twinshot Sniper](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/168/twinshot-sniper) for a successful comeback.

{%youtube XgO50AlfYJw %}

The second game was against everyone's old friend, Selesnya Toxic. For the first three turns both sides built their boards. The opponent went [Jawbone Duelist](https://scryfall.com/card/one/18/jawbone-duelist) on turn two, then [Slaughter Singer](https://scryfall.com/card/one/216/slaughter-singer) and [Venerated Rotpriest](https://scryfall.com/card/one/192/venerated-rotpriest) on turn three, while I went [Skrelv, Defector Mite](https://scryfall.com/card/one/33/skrelv-defector-mite) on turn two and [Ornery Tumblewagg](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/171/ornery-tumblewagg) on turn three. What looked like an even board tilted sharply my way with the arrival of [Doomskar Warrior](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/185/doomskar-warrior) on turn four. After [Doomskar Warrior](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/185/doomskar-warrior) saddled [Ornery Tumblewagg](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/171/ornery-tumblewagg), the first swing grew it to an 8/8 trampler, and the opponent gave up blocking and took 8, which let me pull another [Doomskar Warrior](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/185/doomskar-warrior) into hand. Realizing the situation was against him, the opponent went all in on the counterattack, but it was no use. The second [Doomskar Warrior](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/185/doomskar-warrior) entered and rode [Ornery Tumblewagg](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/171/ornery-tumblewagg) again, letting this swing grow it to an 18/18 trampler, and before its doubling ability resolved, the opponent decisively chose to concede.

## Conclusion

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Selesnya-Mount/fab98838d6a0.webp)

Besides Selesnya, the Mount tribal theme can also be tried in Abzan colors. In this site's recent article [Where Did 莎莉雅 Go? Standard Abzan Mount Build](https://guildmagesforum.tw/Standard-Abzan-Mount/), you can see that this color combination has quality Mounts such as [Caustic Bronco](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/82/caustic-bronco) and [The Gitrog, Ravenous Ride](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/206/the-gitrog-ravenous-ride) to use, and bringing black removal and hand disruption can also raise overall stability. However, the previous article's version didn't address the Mounts' flaws, and easily turned into a plan that relies on [The Gitrog, Ravenous Ride](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/206/the-gitrog-ravenous-ride) to force through, which is awkward if the opponent's removal catches it or if it comes down too late to break through the opponent's defenses. I believe this article has provided an apt answer for improvements, and interested readers might try blending the two versions to build something.
