## Introduction

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Fire-Lord-Zuko/00d602fac5f3.webp)

As the firebending representative of the heroes, Zuko's rares are not very popular in *[Avatar: The Last Airbender](https://scryfall.com/sets/tla?as=grid&order=set)* Limited. Setting aside [Zuko, Conflicted](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/253/zuko-conflicted), which is obviously hard to use, [Fire Lord Zuko](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/221/fire-lord-zuko) is also a card that often gets passed around the table, for no reason other than that it takes many colors and its effect depends entirely on your teammates. So how much can it shine in constructed, where you can deliberately assemble those teammates?


## Card Analysis

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Fire-Lord-Zuko/439835b565b7.webp)
>**Fire Lord Zuko**
>**Creature - Human Noble Ally**
>**Firebending X, where X is Fire Lord Zuko's power.**
>**Whenever you cast a spell from exile or a permanent you control enters from exile, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control.**

In the *[Avatar: The Last Airbender](https://scryfall.com/sets/tla?as=grid&order=set)* card pool, excluding high-rarity cards, the main cards that pair with [Fire Lord Zuko](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/221/fire-lord-zuko) are the [Airbending](https://scryfall.com/search?as=grid&order=name&q=oracle%3Aairbend+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atla+prefer%3Abest) cards, and his own uncommon version, [Zuko, Exiled Prince](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/163/zuko-exiled-prince). [Fire Lord Zuko](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/221/fire-lord-zuko) himself has unremarkable combat power, his effect depends on support, and having to be three colors without green makes him genuinely unattractive in Limited. But in the Standard card pool, impulse draw, exiling the top of your library and playing it, has always been red's specialty, and there are plenty of cards to use. The decklist is as follows:

[Decklist](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7525079#paper)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Fire-Lord-Zuko/41eb0590c30e.webp)

Led by the best impulse draw of the day, the firebending king's biography [The Legend of Roku](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/145/the-legend-of-roku-avatar-roku), we summon from the Spider-Man universe the Ghost-Spider [Gwen Stacy](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/78/gwen-stacy-ghost-spider), [Araña, Heart of the Spider](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/123/ara%C3%B1a-heart-of-the-spider), and [Spinneret and Spiderling](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/94/spinneret-and-spiderling), plus [Kellan, Planar Trailblazer](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/91/kellan-planar-trailblazer), who has traveled here.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Fire-Lord-Zuko/4162e186ddce.webp)
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Fire-Lord-Zuko/97ccde42315f.webp)

[The Legend of Roku](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/145/the-legend-of-roku-avatar-roku) impulse draws three on entering, and after transforming into [Avatar Roku](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/145/the-legend-of-roku-avatar-roku?back) it is a powerful finisher that keeps creating [Dragon](https://scryfall.com/card/ttla/9/dragon) tokens. [Gwen Stacy](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/78/gwen-stacy-ghost-spider) impulse draws one on entering, and after transforming into [Ghost-Spider](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/78/gwen-stacy-ghost-spider?back) she is both a strong beater and can impulse draw on her own.
![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Fire-Lord-Zuko/2c75a6f1b082.webp)

[Araña](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/123/ara%C3%B1a-heart-of-the-spider) makes each [[飾裝]] creature impulse draw when it hits your opponent, and when you attack she puts a +1/+1 counter on a target attacking creature, boosting your hitting power while fulfilling the impulse draw trigger condition; [Spinneret and Spiderling](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/94/spinneret-and-spiderling) needs a bit of time to grow, and once fattened up, as long as it deals enough damage (it doesn't need to hit your opponent) it impulse draws; as for [Kellan](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/91/kellan-planar-trailblazer), once he has leveled up once, hitting your opponent triggers an impulse draw, and after leveling up twice he becomes a 3/2 double striker, and the cost to level up can be paid with the mana produced by firebending. Together these form a solid, powerful line of impulse-draw creatures.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Fire-Lord-Zuko/34efd4f6f268.webp)

For spells, considering the red mana added by firebending, I used the kickable mono-red one-mana burn spell [Burst Lightning](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/192/burst-lightning) and the old friend two-mana [Lightning Strike](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/146/lightning-strike) as the main removal.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Fire-Lord-Zuko/4eb190b79776.webp)

Since mono-red can't handle many threats, I mixed in white removal [Get Lost](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/14/get-lost) and the uncounterable three-color removal [Inevitable Defeat](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/194/inevitable-defeat), plus [Spectacular Spider-Man](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/14/spectacular-spider-man), which comes with flash to protect your whole team, helping you survive all kinds of spot-removal or sweeper threats.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Fire-Lord-Zuko/0aa92107a73d.webp)

I configured the lands rather casually, only handling color-fixing needs with [Starting Town](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/289/starting-town), [Nomad Outpost](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/263/nomad-outpost), [Sacred Foundry](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/256/sacred-foundry), and [Sunbillow Verge](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/264/sunbillow-verge). When building, readers can further add the impulse-draw land [Cori Mountain Monastery](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/252/cori-mountain-monastery) or other utility lands for reinforcement.

## Gameplay Demo

With the firebending king [Roku](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/145/the-legend-of-roku-avatar-roku), the planeswalker-traveler [Kellan](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/91/kellan-planar-trailblazer), and all the good friends from the Spider-Man universe helping out, [Fire Lord Zuko](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/221/fire-lord-zuko)'s army-wide pumping ability is truly formidable. The large amount of resources and direct-damage ability let this deck hold its own against mid-to-slow resource decks. While recording matches this time, I also ran into a four-color [Fire Lord Azula](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/220/fire-lord-azula) deck, and the two sides had a recreation of the original [Destined Confrontation](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/15/destined-confrontation). ## Conclusion

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Standard-Fire-Lord-Zuko/657c1a69c9e0.webp)

The bigger problem with this deck is that its creatures are all rare or higher, so recreating it on MTGA takes a lot of wildcards; in paper it may be relatively easier to put together. But as a mid-speed creature deck, it has a very high standard in hitting power, removal range, and resource volume, and is worth optimizing further.
