## Legacy European Championship: Lille

Let's look at the European event first ([Melee link](https://melee.gg/Tournament/View/16639)).
It was held in Lille, a city in northern France (although it's called a city, its permanent population is only around 230,000).

690 players entered, with Pioneer as the format.
After 15 Swiss rounds, the top eight play single elimination, and the event is split over two days:
Day 1: nine rounds, and only players at 6-3 or better advance to Day 2.
Day 2: six more rounds, then a top-eight single-elimination bracket.

### Rakdos Sacrifice

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Meta-Inspection-20231007/14f3ac5a7511.webp)

The Sacrifice build is quite standard, at least the main deck is.

### Fire-Color Combo

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Meta-Inspection-20231007/16a87305483a.webp)

![](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/f/4/f487b582-e73f-4325-939f-95fc5a9aba49.jpg?1682203390)

As I understand it, this is a variant of Rona Combo. It keeps looting, then produces infinite mana with the following line:
Cast [Retraction Helix](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/7/a/7aa3fdd0-34d8-47b3-9753-d2929838732e.jpg?1562437109) targeting [Rona, Herald of Invasion](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/f/4/f487b582-e73f-4325-939f-95fc5a9aba49.jpg?1682203390). Together with [Mox Amber](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/6/6/66024e69-ad60-4c9a-a0ca-da138d33ad80.jpg?1685554120), this produces infinite blue (tap Rona to return Mox Amber to hand, cast Amber to make mana, then do it again).

![](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/3/e3399260-a81a-475c-9b87-1efb1a13f8d6.jpg?1673148415)

It also has [Bring to Light](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/3/e3399260-a81a-475c-9b87-1efb1a13f8d6.jpg?1673148415) to find whichever piece is missing (Rona or Retraction Helix), or to find [Valki, God of Lies](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/e/a/ea7e4c65-b4c4-4795-9475-3cba71c50ea5.jpg?1636100811) and cast the back side, [Tibalt, Cosmic Impostor](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/back/e/a/ea7e4c65-b4c4-4795-9475-3cba71c50ea5.jpg?1636100811), for a huge amount of resources, or to find a sweeper or another toolbox card.

![](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/e/3ec0c0fb-1a4f-45f4-85b7-346a6d3ce2c5.jpg?1566819742)

With infinite mana, you can switch to bouncing [Karn, the Great Creator](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/e/3ec0c0fb-1a4f-45f4-85b7-346a6d3ce2c5.jpg?1566819742) back to hand. Each time Karn lands, fetch [The Stone Brain](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/5/3570ebf2-a94c-4621-8808-b06e6e830c06.jpg?1674422057) from the sideboard or exile zone and exile the opponent's whole hand/library.
Another approach is to find a way to land Tibalt after getting infinite mana and repeat the earlier infinite-mana loop. By now you also have red and black, so you can cast Tibalt infinitely, using the [+2] each time it enters to mill the opponent's library dry.
The worst-case option is probably to land [Atraxa, Grand Unifier](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/a/4a1f905f-1d55-4d02-9d24-e58070793d3f.jpg?1676519555) to dig for resources and see whether you can combo next turn, or simply beat the opponent to death with Atraxa, which is not a bad option either.

![](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/6/6/66605fe1-9a20-4c95-b53e-1249cedb978b.jpg?1675957216)

[Tyvar, Jubilant Brawler](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/6/6/66605fe1-9a20-4c95-b53e-1249cedb978b.jpg?1675957216) is used after infinite mana to loot infinitely, or to find resources, and also lets creatures' activated abilities ignore summoning sickness.

> Here's a quick note on how to loot infinitely with Tyvar.
>
> 1. With Tyvar on the battlefield, first use the earlier loop to produce infinite black-green
> 2. Tap Rona to bounce Tyvar to hand
> 3. Cast Tyvar to untap Rona
> 4. Activate Tyvar's [+1] ability to untap Rona, and in response to that ability, use Rona's loot once
> 5. Repeat steps 2 - 4, looting infinitely until you find Karn or Valki

Last of all are [Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/6/3/63cda4a0-0dff-4edb-ae67-a2b7e2971350.jpg?1591228085) and [Lukka, Coppercoat Outcast](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/5/7/5798fdf0-d178-43d9-b821-8f3f654654b4.jpg?1591227292). I think Kinnan and Lukka are also there for acceleration and finding resources, and in the mid-to-late game they can find Atraxa as a final backup plan.

### Rakdos Midrange

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Meta-Inspection-20231007/46a5890604b5.webp)

### Izzet Phoenix

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Meta-Inspection-20231007/91dd6f4d4166.webp)

As mentioned before, Phoenix is back in the meta now that it has Sleight of Hand. With more ways to find cards, it's easier to dig up the right resources, and since Sleight of Hand isn't drawing, it slightly lowers Sheoldred's threat.
The build takes the older route: copying for two extra turns, keeping resources for a mid-to-late-game burst.
The sideboard has flexibility against control, plus the Saheeli combo.

### Rakdos Sacrifice

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Meta-Inspection-20231007/f50b4278d447.webp)

The Sacrifice build is similar too, but the sideboard only adds one more Duress, then runs a third Ob Nixilis, and also four Leyline of the Guildpact for the mirror and for Greasefang.
However, four Leylines in the sideboard take up a lot of room, so you may need to think about how to handle sideboarding.

### Gruul Vehicles

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Meta-Inspection-20231007/36c406881e69.webp)

Gruul Vehicles did quite well this weekend, with two lists in the RC top eight.
The builds are much the same, again adding the WOE Saga for resources and damage.

### Rakdos Midrange

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Meta-Inspection-20231007/892d07fd3e64.webp)

This Rakdos Midrange list moved the two [Reckoner Bankbuster](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/2/7/279acd17-6c17-427b-a69d-fc02442ff4a3.jpg?1685368706) that would usually be in the sideboard into the main deck, and moved [Misery's Shadow](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/5/1/514c5e77-9ecc-41c9-b104-76343601fafb.jpg?1674420938), which usually goes in the main deck, to the sideboard. Presumably it expected more midrange mirrors, but the price is that game 1 is harder to win against Green Devotion and Sacrifice.

### Boros Heroic

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Meta-Inspection-20231007/277839f5199d.webp)

Heroic uses a newer build. The main deck has a one-mana red pump spell that gives +2/+0 and trample (plus a Monster Role), and the sideboard has three [Showdown of the Skalds](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/3/d/3d9d840e-1f13-44e3-a4de-903cfa58a346.jpg?1631051876) to add resources for matchups that go to the mid-to-long game.

### UW Control

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/Pioneer-Meta-Inspection-20231007/ce69ee4e32a8.webp)

This control deck cut down on sweepers and added more cheap removal and cheap counterspells (even [Absorb](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/1/e/1e8a43c1-42d1-45ef-8a63-4b87775a6e88.jpg?1584831352) is only at three copies). It feels like without [Teferi, Hero of Dominaria](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/5/d/5d10b752-d9cb-419d-a5c4-d4ee1acb655e.jpg?1562736365) it's easy to run out of resources.
I'm not sure what the builder was thinking, but judging by the results, it's a good build.

### Enigmatic Fires

## Conclusion

Finally, a very irresponsible metagame guess. ~~Can't help it, the meta still needs nearly a month to settle~~
I think there will again be lots of Rakdos Midrange, Green Devotion, and Rakdos Sacrifice, which should make up over 40%.
Phoenix, Spirits, Mono-White Humans, Gruul, Greasefang, and Lotus Field will also see play, but not that many copies (probably none above five), which I estimate at 25 - 30%.
The rest will be a mix of scattered red-white aggro (Heroic, Convoke), Fires, Indomitable Creativity, Neoform, and similar decks.

Maybe someone will play Golgari Midrange, but I don't expect many, and it still needs testing in the environment. Looking at the card list, it feels like you'd end up better off just playing Rakdos Midrange: every extra draw card costs an additional 1 life, which is 3 against Sheoldred, and even when you have Sheoldred yourself you're constantly losing life. Unless the metagame is absolutely packed with enchantments, I'm personally barely considering it.
And even if you do splash green, it should still be built around Green Devotion; Kiki and Bloodtithe are too hard to give up.

Finally, combining how things felt in this week's MA Explorer Event, I'll probably choose between Rakdos Sacrifice and Rakdos Midrange, still undecided.

Finally, I hope everyone finds the deck they love, see you all on the tournament floor in November!
