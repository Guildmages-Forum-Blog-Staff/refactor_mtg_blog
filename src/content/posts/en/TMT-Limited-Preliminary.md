Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles ([TMT](https://scryfall.com/sets/tmt)) holds its prerelease events on February 27, 2026, and launches officially a week later on March 6, 2026. Want to know more about TMT's color-pair themes and deckbuilding strategies? Then check out this series of first looks at TMT Limited!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [TMT Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/TMT-mechanism/)
- The official [Magic: The Gathering® | Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Prerelease Guide](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-prerelease-guide), published on February 20, 2026, mentions "**key removal spell**". Click the mana symbol of each color to look up the relevant info. By the way, the colorless {% mtglink "Henchbots" %} and {% mtglink "Weather Maker" %} (if you put more than 3 charge counters on it) can just about count as removal spells too, so keep them in mind. 

## Color-Pair Themes

![TMT draft five-color-pair card image](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/59d219f41c69.webp)

### <span style="color:#C3B270">White</span><span style="color:#808080">Black</span> Sneaky Ninjas — (Sneak)

[There are 27 cards with the Sneak mechanic](https://scryfall.com/search?as=grid&order=name&q=oracle%3ASneak+(game%3Apaper)+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest), of which 15 are Ninjas and 11 are instants/sorceries (2 in each of the five colors, plus 1 White-Black sorcery, {% mtglink "Karai's Technique" %}).

> There is also one black Class, {% mtglink "Ninja Teen" %}, whose Level 3 gives the creatures in your graveyard Sneak {three}{<span style="color:#808080">B</span>}, though that is a bit indirect.

As for [Ninja creature cards, there are 42 in TMT](https://scryfall.com/search?as=grid&order=name&q=(type%3Acreature+type%3Aninja)+(game%3Apaper)+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest). Subtract the 15 with a Sneak cost mentioned above and there are still 27. The Ninjas available to White-Black (including hybrid mana cards) number 26, so the options are plentiful.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/1204510c5368.webp)

Let's start with the three two-color uncommon signposts on the far right of the image above. Both White-Black cards have a Sneak cost. If the Ninja creature {% mtglink "Karai, Future of the Foot" %} is cast for its Sneak cost, the effect that triggers when she later deals combat damage gets stronger. The other one, {% mtglink "Koya, Death from Above" %}, exiles a creature as soon as it enters, and at end of turn lets you choose whether to pay {three}{<span style="color:#808080">B</span>} to exile that creature permanently. You can treat it as a plain {% mtglink "Turn to Mist" %}, or pay 7 mana in total to eliminate the threat for good!

> Like {% mtglink "Koya, Death from Above" %}, each color in TMT has one mono-colored creature whose rules text uses an activated or triggered ability of another color. They also serve Limited as signpost uncommons.

As for the two cards on the far left, {% mtglink "Leonardo, Cutting Edge" %}, as a rare version of {% mtglink "Ajani's Pridemate" "war" %}, naturally has to be a bit stronger. Besides coming with lifelink, if you Sneak him in and grab the signature gear {% mtglink "Quintessential Katana" %} that is lying in wait on the battlefield, after combat damage resolves you get a 4/4 lifelink Turtle that can block!

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/a224758afcda.webp)

However, to play the Ninja theme well, you first need enough one- and two-drops, ideally with some evasion. Among the five White-Black one-drops above, apart from {% mtglink "Leonardo, Leader in Blue" %}, which may be better in the late game (though it also has an activated ability that gives first strike for {one}{<span style="color:#C3B270">W</span>}), the other four are all great starting points for your Ninjas.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/96fd15366ea7.webp)

Once you attack on turn two and go unblocked, you have a chance to cast one of these five cards whose Sneak cost is only two mana or less. Among them, {% mtglink "Dark Leo & Shredder" %} and {% mtglink "Splinter, Hamato Yoshi" %} are the most destructive. The former can spread out an army of Ninjas, while the latter is a Ninja lord whose Sneak cost is only one {<span style="color:#808080">B</span>}, which means after combat you may get to slam the one-drop you returned to hand back onto the battlefield. In addition, {% mtglink "The Last Ronin's Technique" %} on the far right makes for an explosive development if it lands on turn two, and it also fits well in the Red-White team-up theme, killing your opponent before they know what hit them.

### <span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span><span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span> Machines — (Machines)

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/96a2e37be072.webp)

Both Blue-Red cards relate to artifacts, so we know that TMT's Blue-Red pair focuses on an artifact theme. {% mtglink "Baxter Stockman" %} can turn one of your artifact creatures into an unblockable-ish killing machine, while {% mtglink "Brilliance Unleashed" %}, besides being a clunky creature removal spell, depends on whether the second mode works out. It is well worth using if there is an artifact in your graveyard, and if what comes back is a noncreature artifact, turning it into a 3/3 flier may be even better! Another one, {% mtglink "Metalhead" %}, returns an artifact or creature to hand as it enters, and if you have a spare artifact to sacrifice, you can pay one {<span style="color:#AF2321">R</span>} to activate it and make it a haste creature that is at least 5/5 the moment it enters!

For the two blue cards on the far left, if {% mtglink "Donatello, Gadget Master" %} deals combat damage to the opponent, whether it copies a {% mtglink "Bespoke Bō" %} or a {% mtglink "Metalhead" %}, you instantly pull ahead on tempo!

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/05d31f826522.webp)

The common {% mtglink "Donatello, Turtle Techie" %} can immediately make up a card deficit as long as you have an artifact on the battlefield when it enters, and the mythic rare {% mtglink "Donatello, Mutant Mechanic" %} also needs an artifact on the battlefield for support, after which it simply and brutally makes an army of artifacts and keeps your size advantage! However, neither of these cards works on its own. They need support from other artifacts to deliver their full value.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/1013b2c7cb23.webp)

The uncommon {% mtglink "Donatello, Way with Machines" %} is not strong by itself, but pair it with cards that give two for one when an artifact enters, like {% mtglink "Mouser Foundry" %} or {% mtglink "Mechanized Ninja Cavalry" %}, and Donatello instantly becomes a 3/4 or bigger flier! In addition, a card like {% mtglink "The Ooze" %} keeps exiling cards from graveyards and making Mutagen tokens while it is on the battlefield, so it is also a good one to interact with.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/913b7a653d5a.webp)

It finally came! That dreaded mechanic, Affinity for Artifacts! As long as you keep deploying artifacts, {% mtglink "Krang, Master Mind" %} can be cast for as little as two blue mana. Besides coming with an effect like {% mtglink "Cranial Plating" "tsr" %}, it also refills your hand up to four cards as soon as it enters. {% mtglink "Improvised Arsenal" %}, the red version of Cranial Plating, can even put out a copy of itself in the mid-to-late game if you have enough mana. Another one, {% mtglink "Ravenous Robots" %}, is even more fearsome: every time you cast an artifact, you get an extra 1/1 Robot token. {% mtglink "Purple Dragon Punks" %} on the far right plays more of a supporting role. Don't forget this set has this artifact mana dork, whose mana can pay for activated abilities such as Equip. As for {% mtglink "Don & Raph, Hard Science" %}, as soon as it attacks, your next noncreature spell gains Affinity for Artifacts, which really helps with casting expensive spells like {% mtglink "Turtles in Time" %}. But I guess most of the time it will just be used to get a free [pizza](https://scryfall.com/search?as=grid&order=name&q=Pizza+color%3C%3DC+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest). What do you all think?

However, don't forget there is one card that can blow the Blue-Red artifact theme right out of the water. If you know your opponent has it, remember to bring a {% mtglink "Negate" "tmt" %} to stay safe.

### <span style="color:#808080">Black</span><span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span> Disappear — (Disappear)

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/e931f5aef950.webp)

[There are 9 cards with the Disappear mechanic](https://scryfall.com/search?q=oracle%3ADisappear+(game%3Apaper)+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name), mostly in Black-Green, apart from one Blue-Black hybrid creature, {% mtglink "Krang & Shredder" %}. Mechanic-payoff cards like {% mtglink "Pizza Face, Gastromancer" %} and {% mtglink "Lord Dregg, Insect Invader" %} encourage you to head in this direction. The black Equipment {% mtglink "Shredder's Armor" %} is itself an engine that reliably triggers Disappear, and the mythic rare {% mtglink "Super Shredder" %} keeps growing stronger with each Disappear trigger. Don't forget that the Sneak mechanic mentioned above in White-Black, as well as {% mtglink "Escape Tunnel" %}, which can be sacrificed to fetch a land onto the battlefield, can also trigger Disappear!

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/02b2310a6a4f.webp)

> A little combo: if you control both {% mtglink "Super Shredder" %} and {% mtglink "Michelangelo, Weirdness to 11" %}, each time you sacrifice a Mutagen token, you can put up to 4 +1/+1 counters on Super Shredder! Hmm... so do those two get along well?
> 1. The +1/+1 counters put by sacrificing the Mutagen itself are doubled (and can be put on other creatures)
> 2. Super Shredder triggers because a permanent left the battlefield, and the +1/+1 counters it puts are doubled

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/8adfd2c9a786.webp)

In addition, Black-Green also has a Food subtheme, the pizza delivery series centered on {% mtglink "Courier of Comestibles" %}. What flavor would you like tonight? Is it the {% mtglink "Spicy Oatmeal Pizza" %} that deals a thousand to the enemy and seven hundred and fifty to yourself, or the deadly-yet-delicious {% mtglink "Anchovy & Banana Pizza" %}? The sweet and smooth {% mtglink "Guac & Marshmallow Pizza" %} looks good, but the classic, never-fail {% mtglink "Omni-Cheese Pizza" %} is still hard to give up... Why not just order the {% mtglink "Everything Pizza" %} that has it all! As long as the Courier of Comestibles delivers on time, a piping hot pizza is always mouthwatering! ~~Writing this made me hungry XD~~

>  The card design of {% mtglink "Courier of Comestibles" %} is also wonderful! If you can find a Food card you need in your library, congratulations, the delivery arrives on time and you get a piping hot pizza! Even if you can't find one, you still get a cold pizza (a "plain" Food token, still pretty tasty), but the {one}{<span style="color:#4A8A3D">G</span>} you paid to request the delivery is non-refundable. Just read the flavor text and you can feel the unique charm of telling a little story with a single card!

### <span style="color:#AF2321">Red</span><span style="color:#C3B270">White</span> Alliance — (Alliance)

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/6126fa40d15f.webp)

[There are 10 cards with the Alliance mechanic](https://scryfall.com/search?q=oracle%3AAlliance+(game%3Apaper)+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name), all in Red-White. {% mtglink "The Neutrinos" %} has the basic bonus of Alliance adding to its power, and when it attacks it can make another creature you control re-enter the battlefield tapped and attacking, earning an extra Alliance trigger without missing that turn's attack; {% mtglink "Go Ninja Go" %}, besides triggering Alliance once, can also serve as removal. As for {% mtglink "Old Hob, Alleycat Blues" %}, just by standing there it can put out a creature each combat to trigger Alliance, and its ability can also give a target attacking creature indestructible for {one}{<span style="color:#C3B270">W</span>}. {% mtglink "Raphael, Most Attitude" %}'s own Alliance effect can exile the top card of your library, and when it attacks you can play one of those cards, and if you equip it with {% mtglink "Hard-Won Jitte" %}, the attack becomes even more aggressive.

> The reason I did not pick the rare {% mtglink "Raphael, the Nightwatcher" %} is that even with two double strike abilities, it won't let you hit a third time. 

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/fcd2727fb135.webp)

 {% mtglink "Sally Pride, Lioness Leader" %} is the big bomb of Red-White. It can make plenty of 2/2 Mutant tokens as it enters to trigger Alliance, and when attacking it pumps the whole team with counters! Others like {% mtglink "Mechanized Ninja Cavalry" %} and {% mtglink "Mighty Mutanimals" %} are good two-for-ones that trigger the Alliance ability twice as they enter. Also, {% mtglink "Wingnut, Bat on the Belfry" %} gains multiple keyword abilities whenever Alliance has triggered, and its team-wide attack boost the moment it attacks is quite powerful! As for {% mtglink "Raphael, Tough Turtle" %}, it is the creature version of {% mtglink "Impact Tremors" "fdn" %}, and paired with Alliance it can deal extra damage.

### <span style="color:#006DB3">Blue</span><span style="color:#4A8A3D">Green</span> Mutagen — (Mutagen)

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/028503d1e0ab.webp)

[There are 12 cards with the Mutagen mechanic](https://scryfall.com/search?q=oracle%3AMutagen+(game%3Apaper)+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name), mostly in Blue-Green, 11 in total, plus 1 legendary artifact, {% mtglink "The Ooze" %}. Although many cards in Blue-Green already let you make extra Mutagen tokens, as long as {% mtglink "Genghis Frog" %} is on the battlefield, every other Mutant and Frog creature that enters afterward keeps making Mutagen tokens. {% mtglink "Ray Fillet, Man Ray" %} lets you remove +1/+1 counters to draw cards, and {% mtglink "Venus, Torn Between Worlds" %} puts an equal number of +1/+1 counters on herself whenever she deals damage; and for each creature with a counter on it that deals combat damage to a player, you can pay {<span style="color:#006DB3">U</span>} to draw a card! In Limited she should be about as strong as {% mtglink "Enduring Curiosity" %}. 
  
Thanks to green's size advantage, a creature equipped with {% mtglink "Novel Nunchaku" %} can easily fight and kill the opponent's smaller creatures, while {% mtglink "Michelangelo, Mutant BFF" %} makes your creatures with counters hard to block, and keeps making Mutagen on every attack! The whole point of Blue-Green is to keep converting these counters into size, cards in hand, and your victory!

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/1b332c14d64f.webp)

Also, don't forget to pick up some noncreature spells that make Mutagen tokens! They will be your reliable helpers when you need them.

### Let's Take Stock of TMT's Main Creature Types!

[There are 117 creatures in total](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%28type%3Acreature%29+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name), about 61.6% of the 190 cards in the set.

- [Ninja: 42 cards](https://scryfall.com/search?as=grid&order=name&q=(type%3Acreature+type%3Aninja)+(game%3Apaper)+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest), in all five colors.
- [Turtle: 29 cards](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%28type%3Acreature+type%3Aturtle%29+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name), in all five colors. Incidentally, only 3 Turtles are not Ninjas: {% mtglink "Metalhead" %}, {% mtglink "Slash, Reptile Rampager" %}, and {% mtglink "Tokka & Rahzar, Terrible Twos" %}.
- [Mutant: 61 cards](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%28type%3Acreature+type%3Amutant%29+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name), in all five colors. And if you count only [Mutant creatures that are neither Ninjas nor Turtles, there are 28](https://scryfall.com/search?as=grid&order=name&q=%28-type%3Aninja+-type%3Aturtle%29+%28type%3Acreature+type%3Amutant%29+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest).
- [Other non-Mutant creatures: 56 cards](https://scryfall.com/search?q=-%28type%3Amutant%29%28type%3Acreature%29+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name), in all five colors. But "[ordinary people](https://scryfall.com/search?q=-type%3Aninja+-type%3Amutant+%28type%3Acreature+type%3Ahuman%29+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name)" really are a minority in the Ninja Turtles universe, with only 12 cards, a population share a bit over 10%.

![](https://images.guildmagesforum.tw/posts/TMT-Limited-Preliminary/920d77757b32.webp)

So why divide them up like this? The image above reveals a small extra theme: in TMT, four cards can interact with the Ninja, Turtle, and/or Mutant creature types. Although the land {% mtglink "Turtle Lair" %} excludes interaction with the Mutant type for flavor reasons, overall, in Limited (especially in the green-adjacent color pairs), these cards are worth a little attention. As for the rest, let's explore to our heart's content after release!

## Conclusion: What Do the Ninja Turtles Look Like in Your Memory?

Maybe it's not bad to imagine this UB set as the Ninja Turtles traveling through time and space to Kamigawa. Or rather, it would be hard to claim that some of the earlier Kamigawa designs borrowed nothing from, or paid no homage to, the Ninja Turtles. The obvious ones are {% mtglink "Silver-Fur Master" %} and {% mtglink "Splinter, Hamato Yoshi" %}, and {% mtglink "Kappa Tech-Wrecker" %} and the Turtles; more subtly, the [Wind Avatars](https://scryfall.com/search?as=grid&order=name&q=wind+avatar+(game%3Apaper)+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest) in the Ninja Turtles set also mirror Kamigawa's [Tree Kodama](https://scryfall.com/search?q=Tree+Kodama&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name).

Let me share my childhood memories of the Ninja Turtles. In the days when Final Fantasy was still translated as "Space Warrior" and Doraemon was still called "Robot Cat Jingle Bell," the four Ninja Turtles also had very quirky Taiwanese names: Red Bean Ice ([Raphael](https://scryfall.com/search?as=grid&order=name&q=Raphael%2C+type%3Acreature+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest)), Blue Angel ([Leonardo](https://scryfall.com/search?q=Leonardo%2C+type%3Acreature+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name)), Purple Grape ([Donatello](https://scryfall.com/search?q=Donatello%2C+type%3Acreature+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name)), and Orange Blossom ([Michelangelo](https://scryfall.com/search?q=Michelangelo%2C+type%3Acreature+%28game%3Apaper%29+set%3Atmt+prefer%3Abest&unique=cards&as=grid&order=name)). Only when I grew up did I learn that their names come from Renaissance masters, and even the fact that Raphael's weapon is a "jitte" and not a trident only updated my understanding after TMT was announced. My impression of the Ninja Turtles also swung back and forth from "so cool!" as a kid, through "not that cool..." and "kinda cool again?" Although I am not a longtime devotee, I know there must be a group of people who still think the Ninja Turtles are super cool and deeply love this IP.

As players, all we need is to keep supporting the values we believe in and do our best to satisfy our own experience. Well then, see you at TMT Limited!
