Murders at Karlov Manor (MKM) prerelease events run on February 2, 2024, and the set is officially released one week later on February 9, 2024. Want to know more about how each card rates in Limited, plus some potentially fun interactions? Then check out this series of MKM Limited primers!

For the mechanics, see this article: [Murders at Karlov Manor Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/MKM-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: An unconditional first pick
4.0: Worth a first pick
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you will really want it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick in a draft
2.0–1.0: Below average (you would rather not play it than play it)
0.5–0.0: Unplayable (basically do not play it; playing it will only help you lose)

## Red

![111](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/9/d/9dc52b53-3e4f-4d7d-851f-86c6e0ac67b2.jpg?1706119306)

**cephille:** 1.0

Because MKM is not an artifact environment, this is basically used to destroy your opponent's Clues, and occasionally you can hit a few decent artifact creatures or Vehicles. After destroying some of your opponent's artifacts you also have to exile X cards from the top of your library. Although there is a chance to gain a creature's ETB and an attack, this unstable card advantage can also make your library suddenly three to five cards smaller, so it may not be a good choice. If I want to destroy artifacts, I would rather play a single-target removal.

I do not think this is a card that belongs in the main deck in this set's Limited.

![112](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/8/b87683f7-8a61-4e4a-8b8b-3bf812454096.jpg?1705953436)

**cephille:** 2.0

A six-mana 3/2 with double strike and trample is probably still not good without an Aura or Equipment, but you can first cast it face down for three mana and turn it face up for five, which is much more flexible. However, it fails the [Shock](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/2/9/298747bb-eb40-4b58-bb22-4ac2bc1d795c.jpg?1701811240) test, so I will not give it too high a score.

![113](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/2/9/29ee07df-215f-45a6-9a5a-708143d73e45.jpg?1705953537)

**cephille:** 3.5

A three-mana creature that deals 3 is a solid, steady play, and the condition to solve the Case should not be hard in red: after it enters and deals 3, you just attack with two creatures. After solving it, you can sacrifice it at any time to impulse-draw by looking at three and choosing one you can play this turn, which is very flexible and should deserve this score.

![114](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/b/b/bb18b1de-bc08-4522-b891-6117a8271534.jpg?1705953551)

**cephille:** 4.0

Entering for three mana, it first does a [Tormenting Voice](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/6/d/6da46103-a14c-4aa2-92fc-fd758335caf4.jpg?1604198268), which is ordinary, but once you empty your hand and solve this Case, you get two extra cards at the beginning of each of your upkeeps. Although you cannot get past your next upkeep, as long as the deck is built well, the board advantage you gain will quickly and powerfully drown your opponent!

![115](https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/9/5/95bc5f89-2f01-40c4-9883-4c90ab89fcbb.jpg?1705957169)

**cephille:** 4.0

Being able to arrest at instant speed has endless uses, and after taking the creature you can also suspect it. Not only do you get an extra point of trample damage that turn, but when it goes back next turn, it cannot block either!

> Let's briefly go over what happens when you take it at instant speed:
>
> - If your opponent attacks on their turn and you take it, it is removed from combat (your opponent no longer controls it), but unfortunately this card also suspects the creature, so you cannot use it to block and make a 2-for-1 trade.
> - If you take it after the beginning of your opponent's end step, you untap and then attack, and saving mana can even catch your opponent off guard.

An Equipment that costs two to cast and two to attach, giving +3/+0, is actually quite strong. With your creatures that have evasion (menace, flying, and so on) it can attack very quickly. Cast it face down early on, and in the mid game when it is about to die, turn it face up and attach it to another creature to hit even harder and faster!

## Afterword

The red cards of MKM look like a rather fast color. If you want to play a fast draft deck and you see a red signal, commit decisively! I hope everyone has fun!
