Lorwyn Eclipsed (ECL) holds its prerelease events on January 16, 2026, and is officially released one week later on January 23, 2026. Want to know more about individual card ratings and advice for Limited? Then check out this series of ECL Limited introductions!

- For the mechanics, see this article: [Lorwyn Eclipsed Mechanics Explained](https://guildmagesforum.tw/ECL-Mechanism/)

In addition, ECL includes as many as 20 [Special Guest (SPG)](#special-guest-spg) cards, with one non-foil appearing in every 55 regular Play Boosters. They can be used in ECL Limited, as well as in the formats where each card was originally legal.

## Rating Scale

> **Note**: This ECL Limited format places a high demand on tribal synergy within each color pair. If a creature card has the tribe of that color pair's theme (e.g., Merfolk, Goblin, Kithkin, Elemental, Elf, etc.), the rating already reflects its strength in that color pair.

5.0: Unconditional first pick, brings an almost game-winning advantage
4.5: Unconditional first pick in the right colors, brings a huge advantage
4.0: First-pick value, extremely synergistic with the color pair's theme, can be the deciding card for splashing, switching colors, or entering a color pair
3.5: Top uncommons and commons of the set and other sufficiently strong cards, synergistic with the color pair's theme, can be a key card for entering a color pair
3.0: Important cards for a color pair's theme, plus other above-average cards you want in your final deck
2.5: Average, usually a decent curve filler
2.0 to 1.0: Below average, generally leave out, can be used as filler when necessary but not too many
0.5 to 0.0: Unplayable, basically don't play it, playing it only helps you lose

## Conclusion
Thanks to the return of tribal themes in this set, many Special Guests are heavy hitters from various eras and formats, and pairing them with this set's Changelings makes the play experience even richer. In addition, the set's multicolor theme is supported by shocklands and many color-fixing artifacts, which not only help produce mana but also help produce the colors of your permanents on the battlefield. Looking at all the cards mentioned in this article, I think this set will be an extremely exciting play experience, and I recommend readers give it a try whenever they have the chance!
