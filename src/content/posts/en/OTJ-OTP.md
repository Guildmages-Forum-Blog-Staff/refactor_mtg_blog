Outlaws of Thunder Junction (OTJ) holds its prerelease events on April 12, 2024, and launches officially a week later on April 19, 2024. Want to know more about how each card rates in Limited, plus some interesting possible combinations? Then check out this series of OTJ Limited introductions!

For the mechanics, see this article: [Outlaws of Thunder Junction Mechanics Breakdown](https://guildmagesforum.tw/en/OTJ-mechanism/)

## Rating Scale

5.0: Always take it
4.0: Worth a first pick
3.5: Strong enough, but not necessarily a first pick
3.0: Above average (you would really like it in your final deck)
2.5: Average, maybe around the 6th or 7th pick of a draft
2.0–1.0: Below average (you would rather not play it than play it)
0.5–0.0: Unplayable (basically don't play it; it will only help you lose)

## Breaking News (OTP)

Every card in OTP is a reprint, positioned similarly to Multiverse Legends (MUL) in MOM or Enchanting Tales (WOT) in Wilds of Eldraine. Besides being usable in Limited, these cards are legal only in the formats where they were already legal, and generally are not added to Standard, except for [Leyline Binding](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/24/leyline-binding), which was already Standard-legal.
